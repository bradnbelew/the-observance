# The Observance — OFFICIAL RECORDS (the clinical / bureaucratic layer)

> The administrative corpus of the Hold: ledgers, posted notices and ordinances, depth-survey
> records, and "observed" reports authored by ROLES, not names — the Lamp-Registrar, the Counter
> of the Kept, WARDEN-3, the Survey of the Deep Line, the Quartermaster of Cistern 7. Flat clinical
> register. Third person. Role, date by watch and winter, "it is observed," redactions `[████]`,
> torn lines, missing pages. The flatness is the point; one human word that slips the form lands hard.
>
> Grounded in `arc/WORLD-BIBLE.md` and `arc/lore/canon-spine.md`. Cross-references the journals/letters
> in `arc/lore/documents/`. Never names the Dark (the Unspoken). Never states the induction twist
> (FACT 15) in words. People are counted as instances/heads/lamps/hands — this is a primary engine for
> the "were they human?" question: the record does not distinguish a person from a lamp it must account for.
>
> Eras, not calendar years. Depth in marks (carved down the Stair); time in watches (four to a day) and
> winters kept. The forms degrade across the breaking; the last sheets are barely forms at all.

---

## INTERLINK MAP (how these corroborate the journals)

- **R01 (the founding charter / first ordinance) UNDERWRITES D03** (`learn-them-as-we-learned-them`):
  the charter posts the seven ways as standing law, "received, not enacted" — the bureaucracy itself
  records that the ways were found, not made (FACT 4).
- **R03 (the Offering ledger, Cistern-7 quarter) CONTRADICTS nothing and CONFIRMS D02** (`counted-them-in-the-dark`):
  the deep-cairn returns ledger shows the second column blank against the name struck `vaun` for seven
  winters — the official tally agrees with Vaun's own (FACT 5).
- **R05 (the lamp-count rolls, the Lamp-Registrar) RUNS PARALLEL TO D05/Mara** and decays toward her line
  "i can't keep them all lit": the rolls go from full muster to "lit, of the count: [████]."
- **R06 (the depth survey of the Deep Line) is the official face of D11/D09** — it records the Line's mark,
  then a later survey finds digging *below* the mark "by a hand not entered on the work-roster" (Iss),
  unnamed, redacted.
- **R08 (WARDEN-3's observed report on the threshold) is the official twin of D04/D07** — same event as
  Orin's, in the watchman's form: observed, warned, left; "the rest accounted kept."
- **R10 (the muster that will not come even) ECHOES D01's** seventh-mark: every count of heads against
  lamps against names is off by one the record cannot reconcile, "and the surplus is not an error."
- **R12–R16 (the failing forms) DEGRADE alongside the keepers' late journals** (D08 Brann, the abandonment),
  the watch-log losing its hours, the ration sheet its hand, the last notice its sentence.

## THE RECORDS

### R01 — FOUNDING ORDINANCE OF THE HOLD (the first ordinance · era: the finding → the taking-hold)

> *Cut into the lintel of the Threshold, the topmost gallery, in the proto-script the later stones
> are written in. Human-scaled lettering; the only record up here a person can read standing straight.
> Underwrites D03.*

```
            BY THE STANDING ORDER OF THE FIRST KEEPERS
                    AT THE MOUTH OF THE HOLD

  IT IS SET DOWN, that we came down out of the Long Cold, the sun having
  failed and the sky having failed, and that the deep was warm, and that we
  were RECEIVED here as tenants and are not the owners of this place.

  IT IS SET DOWN, that the seven ways were NOT ENACTED BY THIS ORDER but
  received with the place, and are entered here only that they be kept, viz:

      i.   KEEP THE LAMP.            keep your light. the unlit are not kept.
      ii.  THE DEEP LINE.           pass not the marked depth.
      iii. THE DARK HOURS.          lie not down on the black moon.
      iv.  THE OFFERING.            return the first of the deep to the deep.
      v.   THE BOW.                 bow at the markers. honor the kept.
      vi.  THE UNSPOKEN.            speak not the name. [the line is left blank]
      vii. THE DEEP-BIRD.           keep the bird; it goes dark before we do.

  IT IS SET DOWN, that the order is SEVEN, and that we are tenants of seven,
  and that the rent of this place is the keeping of them.

  This order does not say to WHOM the rent is paid. The first keepers were
  asked, and did not answer, and the blank against the sixth way is theirs.
```

> *Margin, a much later official hand, the form of the breaking era:*
> *the rent was always being paid. we only stopped being able to read the receipt.*

### R02 — STANDING NOTICE: THE KEEPING OF LIGHT (posted notice · the taking-hold · Lamp-works quarter)

> *A posted ordinance, the cleanest form in the corpus, repeated at every lamp-stand down to the Cisterns.
> The plainest law, plainly posted. Confirms the Lamp-Registrar role and the muster R05 will keep.*

```
                          POSTED AT THE LAMP-WORKS
                         BY ORDER OF THE LAMP-REGISTRAR

  EVERY HEARTH, EVERY STAND, EVERY HAND-LAMP IS ENTERED ON THE ROLL.

  1.  Each light is numbered. Each number is mustered at the turning of each
      watch, four to the day.
  2.  A light found UNLIT is entered UNLIT against the hand it was issued to.
      The hand, not the lamp, is answerable.
  3.  Oil is drawn at the Lamp-works only, by the jar, against the roll. A jar
      unaccounted is a light unaccounted.
  4.  No light is to be carried below the Deep Line. Lights returned from below
      the mark are NOT re-issued. They are entered and set apart.
  5.  The deep-bird shares the roll with the lamp. When the bird is down, the
      stand is mustered though it still burns.

  A LIT HOLD IS A KEPT HOLD. KEEP YOUR LAMP.
```

> *Below, scratched not printed, in a hand that did not have the authority to amend a notice:*
> *we kept them all lit for so long. tell whoever reads this we kept them so long.*

### R06 — SURVEY OF THE DEEP LINE (depth-survey record · the breaking of faith · the Survey of the Deep Line)

> *The official record of the marked depth and of its breach. The bureaucracy's account of Iss's sin
> (FACT 8 / the Break) — without his name, which is redacted by ordinance. The OFFICIAL twin of D06's

  SURVEY OF THE DEEP LINE · mark 33 below the Threshold
  the Line was carved in the taking-hold and has been re-marked each winter.
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   1.  The Line at mark 33 is whole along its length, save at the third bay.
   2.  At the third bay the rock is opened DOWNWARD, below the mark, by tool.
       The work is recent. The work is the work of one hand, practised, alone.
   3.  The hand is NOT ENTERED ON THE WORK-ROSTER. No order was given to dig
       below the Line. No order could be given. The way forbids it.

       it, "the Line being the Line for a reason older than the order that marks it."

   THE HAND IS NAMED IN THE WORK-BOOK AS:  [████████]

### R07 — ORDINANCE ON THE SPEAKING (posted notice · the breaking · WARDEN-3)

> *A containment-style notice about the Unspoken, posted after the Break, when the ways had begun to be

```
                      NOTICE · BY THE WATCH · WARDEN-3

  IT HAS BEEN OBSERVED that since the opening at the third bay (R06) certain
  hands have begun to SPEAK OF the matter below the Line. Some name it. Some
  argue it is nothing. Some argue the ways are only fear.

  THE WATCH ENTERS, FOR THE RECORD:

  - The sixth way stands. The name is not to be spoken. This is not amended.
  - Speaking of it is observed and entered. Speaking it BY NAME is observed,

    being understood to draw the attention it names."
  - The watch makes NO CLAIM as to what attends a speaking. The watch only
    enters that, of the hands entered for speaking it by name this winter,

```

### R13 — POSTED NOTICE OF THE GOING-OUT (posted notice · the going-out · "by the standing watch")

```

                  POSTED AT THE THRESHOLD AND THE WARRENS

  IT IS OBSERVED that the rolls are coming off faster than the heads.
  IT IS OBSERVED that the lamps are not staying lit.
  IT IS OBSERVED that the markers are full and the table is not.

### R16 — THE SHEET AFTER (the abandonment → now · no office, no era)

> *Not a form. A sheet that was kept after there was anyone to keep forms. It is in the official hand —
> flat, clinical, third person — but it is not a record of the Hold. It is a record of the reader. It is
> placed last, and it is the only official document addressed, however flatly, to "the present hands."
> It does not state the twist. It files the reader into the same book as everyone above.*

```
  THE RECORD OPENS.
  ──────────────────
  it is observed that new hands are at the Mouth.
  it is observed that they have come down past the Threshold, past the founding
  lintel, and that they are reading.
  it is observed that they carry lamps, and that the lamps are lit, and that
  this is well, and that it has begun the same way every time, with lit lamps
  and a good first line written above them.

  the count is begun. it is not yet unkind.

  the rolls of those before are closed and were not reconciled (R10). the books
  do not come out even and the surplus has never been struck. there is room in
  the markers' column. there has always been room. the column is open, and an
  open column is a thing that fills.

  the present hands will say no one told us. the record does not answer that.
  the record only knows what is done, and writes it true, and keeps what it
  writes, and does not let the writing go when the keeping is done.

  ──────────────────
  [in the steadier hand, beneath, the way every page in this book now ends:]

                              keep your lamp.
```

# The Observance — FOUND-DOCUMENT SET (design)

> Internal design doc. The 12 artifacts players discover and piece together across
> the five Movements. Obeys `canon-spine.md` (registers, keeper voices, foreshadow-
> never-blurt, eras-not-years, found-not-narrated). Each entry below is a brief —
> the authored fragment is written later from these constraints.
>
> Coverage proven: all six keepers voiced (D02 Vaun, D05 Mara, D06 Sella, D07 Orin,
> D08 Brann, D09 Iss); plus the Archivist/record (D01, D04, D10), the founders
> (D03), and the Keeper NPC (D12). Spread M1–M5. Four marked clue_bearing
> (D03, D05, D06, D09). Liar thread: D09 (seed) ↔ D10 (catch). Seventh Stone
> thread: D06 (margin pointer) → D11 (cast-out payoff).

## INTERLINK MAP (the web the documents make)

- **D02 (Vaun's ledger) CONTRADICTS D01 (the opening record).** The record says the
  Offering was kept faithfully in the first winters; Vaun's own tally proves he gave
  nothing back to the deep. A ledger that contradicts a record. (FACT 5 door.)
- **D09 (Iss's letter) is ANSWERED by D10 (the Stone-after inscription).** Iss writes
  the comforting lie ("a wall against the watching"); the later carving, in the
  record's flat hand, contradicts him word for word. The letter seeds; the
  inscription catches. (FACT 7 → 8.)
- **D06 (Sella's reflection-journal) MARGINS POINT TO D11 (the far-water map-note),
  which POINTS TO the Seventh's shrine.** A journal whose margins point to a map-note.
  (Seventh Stone thread.)
- **D07 (Orin's crouched inscription) is ANSWERED by D04 (the escalation record).**
  Orin's three withheld words ("i thought it small") are completed by the record's
  observation→warning→threshold ladder written *about him*. (FACT 6.)
- **D03 (founders' margin-note) OPENS D02, D05, D06, D09** — it is the Rosetta seed:
  "the ways were here before us; learn them as we learned them." Every keeper hand
  after it is written in the script it teaches.
- **D05 (Mara's lectern letter) and D12 (the Keeper's summons) BRACKET the rite** —
  Mara always had the map and never the tool (M2); the Keeper asks for the tool
  itself, the personal token (M5). The map finds its shovel only at the end.

## THE TWELVE

### D01 — "the record opens" (Archivist record · M1 · clue-bearing: no)
The base lectern's first readable report. Third-person, ledgerlike. Names a living
player for a habit *before* the group knew it was a custom (FACT 2, grounded — only
something the tracker measured). States, flatly, that a list of the living is being
kept by name (FACT 1). Warmth under dread: "the count is begun. it is not yet
unkind." Reveals 1, 2; foreshadows 14. Links → D02 (the Offering claim it makes will
be contradicted), D04. Placement: spawn-area lectern, session one.

### D02 — "i counted them in the dark" (Vaun's hand · ledger · M2 · clue-bearing: no)
Vaun's tally-ledger, inventories only, never feelings — three of each, counted twice,
nothing given back. Read against D01 it proves the founding line did **not** keep the
Offering: the ledger contradicts the record. His fate (starved in the hoarded light)
is implied by the last column going to zero with nothing crossed out for the deep.
Reveals 5; foreshadows 4 (his line "learned to count from the land" leans on it).
Links → D01 (contradicts), D03 (founder origin). Placement: Vaun's keeper-stone vision
/ a buried strongbox of tally-sticks.

### D03 — "learn them as we learned them" (founders' hand · margin-note · M1→M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
The oldest, most water-damaged fragment — `[...]`-heavy. A first-keeper margin note in
the proto-cipher script: the ways were **not invented but found**, learned from the
land, something here before them (FACT 4 seed; FACT 3). **Clue payload:** the rune
ring it is written around matches the server-icon rune ring — the master Rosetta. CTA:
*assemble the ring; the script you decode here reads every later stone.* The Discord
answer-oracle confirms the ring solution. Reveals 3; foreshadows 4, 15. Links → D02,
D05, D06, D09 (teaches the script they're all written in). Placement: founding-era
journal page pinned beside the first glyph-stone; server icon as the off-world twin.

### D04 — "observed, warned, left at the threshold" (Archivist record · M2→M4 · clue-bearing: no)
A late, cooled report: the escalation ladder, per-conduct, named on a keeper of the
breaking era (Orin's mirror). Observation → warning → consequence; "the one called
___ was not kept; the rest were." Proves judgment is per-conduct and that kept/left is
a real binary (FACT 6). Completes Orin's withheld sentence (D07). Reveals 6 partially;
foreshadows 10, 12. Links → D07 (answers it), D01 (same hand, colder). Placement:
deep-lectern report, gated behind literacy from D03.

### D05 — "page, line, word" (Mara's hand · letter · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
Mara speaks only in page-references; her "letter" is a book-cipher you assemble by
walking the lectern shelf in order. Cool, precise, lonely. Her assembled sentence:
*she had every rite written and performed none — the map, never the tool.* **Clue
payload:** the page/line/word triples resolve to a real instruction (a coordinate or a
custom to perform), confirmed by the oracle. CTA: *walk the shelf; perform what she
only read.* Reveals (her fate, FACT 5); foreshadows 13 (the tool the group must bring).
Links → D12 (the Keeper finally asks for the tool), D03 (book-cipher needs the script).
Placement: the Kept-Light lectern, multi-shelf.

### D06 — "what the surface keeps" (Sella's hand · journal w/ marginalia · M2→M3 · CLUE-BEARING)
Sella speaks only as a reflection — the journal's body reads as blank/folded until
faced toward water (Atbash/mirror). Gentle, drifting, sentences folding back. The
*margins*, in a steadier later hand, give a bearing to "the far water where she did
not come back." **Clue payload:** the mirrored (Atbash) text resolves a direction; the
margin note hands off to D11 (the map-note). CTA: *read her by the water, then follow
the margin.* Foreshadows 10 (the land can refuse); seeds the Seventh thread. Links →
D11 (margins point to it), D03. Placement: a half-sunk lectern at a shore pool;
reflection-gated.

### D07 — "i thought it small" (Orin's hand · inscription · M2 · clue-bearing: no)
Orin speaks only when the reader is crouched — the inscription is sized/placed so it's
legible only from a crouch (the Bow, embodied as a reading-condition). Terse, ashamed,
fewest words. Three lines, the third withheld: "i thought it small. it was not small.
i —". The record finishes it in D04. Reveals 5 (fate matches custom); foreshadows 6.
Links → D04 (completes him), D03. Placement: low marker-stone at a threshold the path
makes you stoop through.

### D08 — "do not close your eyes here" (Brann's hand · journal · M3 · clue-bearing: no)
Brann speaks only at night / on the black moon — the journal page is unlit/illegible by
day, readable only in darkness or on the black-moon phase. Restless, over-awake, warns
more than mourns. Tells of sleeping when told not to, on the black moon, and what came
inside. Carries FACT 11 obliquely: he names the one fire that "would not be doused" —
the Kept Light that outlived the abandonment. Foreshadows 11, 12. Links → D11 (the
doused shrines vs the one kept fire), the Undercroft. Placement: a cold hearth that
only renders text at night.

### D09 — "the ways are a wall" (Iss's hand · letter · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
The Liar's seed. The warmest, plainest, most trustworthy of the six — a letter to "the
keepers who come after," giving the comforting doctrine: *keep the ways and the
watching cannot have you; they are a wall* (FACT 7). Hands a final-coordinate fragment
that works *just enough* (leads to the Seventh's ruin / a dead shrine). **Clue
payload:** the Vigenère key is his own name; his name decodes, against the other
stones, to the word for "the one who turned away." Solving the key is what plants the
doubt. CTA: *trust the wall — then test the key.* The oracle confirms the name-as-key,
not the doctrine. Foreshadows 7 (planted lie), 8. Links → D10 (which contradicts him),
D11 (his fragment leads there). Placement: a banner-glyph wall + a warm in-world letter.

### D10 — "no wall was ever built here" (Archivist record · inscription · M4 · clue-bearing: no)
The catch. A Stone-after carving in the record's flat hand that contradicts Iss line
for line: there was no wall; the ways were never a keeping-out. Forces the group to
re-walk D09 — and mechanically flips Iss's whole dialogue tree from warm to cold (same
words, now sinister). Yields, only after the catch, the **true** final coordinate.
Reveals 8; demolishes 7; pushes toward (never to) 15. Crucially does **not** state what
the ways *are* — only that the wall is a lie, leaving "then what for?" hanging.
Links → D09 (answers/contradicts), D12. Placement: behind a clue D09 marked "solved."

### D11 — "the seventh, not kept" (cast-out keeper-era · map-note · M3 · clue-bearing: no)
The Seventh Stone payoff. A map-note (FAWE-pasted ruined shrine, discovered never
witnessed) pointed to by D06's margins and D09's wrong fragment. Half-effaced; readable
only with the Rosetta literacy from D03. Records a **seventh** keeper **cast out — not
kept**; theirs is the only shrine where the Kept Light went fully dark (inverse of FACT
11's one eternal fire). Proves acceptance is a choice the land makes, and can refuse
(FACT 10). Keeps the thin canon ambiguity with Iss — distinct, but a sharp group may
wonder. Foreshadows 10; reframes the rite as refusable. Links → D06 (pointed here),
D09 (his fragment dead-ends here), D08 (the doused-vs-kept fire). Placement: off-path
ruined shrine, optional, non-gating; earns Whisper budget.

### D12 — "bring the thing only you can give" (the Keeper · letter/summons · M5 · clue-bearing: no)
The Keeper's register: second-person, addressing the group, half-veiled "we" of "those
before." The rite's instruction — the altar wants a **personal token per keeper**
(FACT 13), and the verb is **receive / keep**, never reward (FACT 14). Answers Mara
(D05): the map's long-missing tool is *you*. Reads as warmth-under-dread, an invitation,
never a threat. **Never states FACT 15** — it is the nearest the corpus comes, delivered
as the Keeper's grief-soft "we would keep you, if you would keep the ways," and stops.
Foreshadows→reveals 13, 14; the felt door to 15. Links → D05 (the tool answered), D01
(the count, now closed), D04 (kept vs left, now the group's). Placement: the Undercroft
altar, final summons.

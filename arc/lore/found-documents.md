> Internal design doc. The artifacts players discover and piece together across
> the five Movements. Obeys `canon-spine.md` (registers, keeper voices, foreshadow-
> never-blurt, eras-not-years, found-not-narrated). Each entry below is a brief —
> the authored fragment is written later from these constraints.
>

> D08 Brann, D09 Iss); plus the Archivist/record (D01, D04, D10), the founders
> (D03), and the Keeper NPC (D12). Spread M1–M5. Four marked clue_bearing
> (D03, D05, D06, D09). Liar thread: D09 (seed) ↔ D10 (catch). Seventh Stone
> thread: D06 (margin pointer) → D11 (cast-out payoff).
>

> web (`design/WEB-MASTER.md`, `INTEGRATION-V2.md`, `BUILD-MANIFEST.md`) added on
> top of the original twelve. They obey the **frozen namespace** (FACTs 16/10b/2b/

## INTERLINK MAP (the web the documents make)

- **D02 (Vaun's ledger) CONTRADICTS D01 (the opening record).** The record says the
  Offering was kept faithfully in the first winters; Vaun's own tally proves he gave
  nothing back to the deep. A ledger that contradicts a record. (FACT 5 door.)
- **D09 (Iss's letter) is ANSWERED by D10 (the Stone-after inscription).** Iss writes
  the comforting lie ("a wall against the watching"); the later carving, in the
  record's flat hand, contradicts him word for word. The letter seeds; the
  inscription catches. (FACT 7 → 8.)
- **D06 (Sella's reflection-journal) MARGINS POINT TO D11 (the far-water map-note),
  which POINTS TO the Seventh's shrine.** A journal whose margins point to a map-note.
  (Seventh Stone thread.)
- **D07 (Orin's crouched inscription) is ANSWERED by D04 (the escalation record).**
  Orin's three withheld words ("i thought it small") are completed by the record's
  observation→warning→threshold ladder written *about him*. (FACT 6.)
- **D03 (founders' margin-note) OPENS D02, D05, D06, D09** — it is the Rosetta seed:
  "the ways were here before us; learn them as we learned them." Every keeper hand
  after it is written in the script it teaches.
- **D05 (Mara's lectern letter) and D12 (the Keeper's summons) BRACKET the rite** —
  Mara always had the map and never the tool (M2); the Keeper asks for the tool
  itself, the personal token (M5). The map finds its shovel only at the end.

## THE TWELVE

### D01 — "the record opens" (Archivist record · M1 · clue-bearing: no)
The base lectern's first readable report. Third-person, ledgerlike. Names a living
player for a habit *before* the group knew it was a custom (FACT 2, grounded — only
something the tracker measured). States, flatly, that a list of the living is being
kept by name (FACT 1). Warmth under dread: "the count is begun. it is not yet
unkind." Reveals 1, 2; foreshadows 14. Links → D02 (the Offering claim it makes will
be contradicted), D04. Placement: spawn-area lectern, session one.

### D02 — "i counted them in the dark" (Vaun's hand · ledger · M2 · clue-bearing: no)
Vaun's tally-ledger, inventories only, never feelings — three of each, counted twice,
nothing given back. Read against D01 it proves the founding line did **not** keep the
Offering: the ledger contradicts the record. His fate (starved in the hoarded light)
is implied by the last column going to zero with nothing crossed out for the deep.
Reveals 5; foreshadows 4 (his line "learned to count from the land" leans on it).
Links → D01 (contradicts), D03 (founder origin). Placement: Vaun's keeper-stone vision
/ a buried strongbox of tally-sticks.

### D03 — "learn them as we learned them" (founders' hand · margin-note · M1→M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
The oldest, most water-damaged fragment — `[...]`-heavy. A first-keeper margin note in
the proto-cipher script: the ways were **not invented but found**, learned from the
land, something here before them (FACT 4 seed; FACT 3). **Clue payload:** the rune
ring it is written around matches the server-icon rune ring — the master Rosetta. CTA:
*assemble the ring; the script you decode here reads every later stone.* The Discord
answer-oracle confirms the ring solution. Reveals 3; foreshadows 4, 15. Links → D02,
D05, D06, D09 (teaches the script they're all written in). Placement: founding-era
journal page pinned beside the first glyph-stone; server icon as the off-world twin.

### D04 — "observed, warned, left at the threshold" (Archivist record · M2→M4 · clue-bearing: no)
A late, cooled report: the escalation ladder, per-conduct, named on a keeper of the
breaking era (Orin's mirror). Observation → warning → consequence; "the one called
___ was not kept; the rest were." Proves judgment is per-conduct and that kept/left is
a real binary (FACT 6). Completes Orin's withheld sentence (D07). Reveals 6 partially;
foreshadows 10, 12. Links → D07 (answers it), D01 (same hand, colder). Placement:
deep-lectern report, gated behind literacy from D03.

### D05 — "page, line, word" (Mara's hand · letter · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
Mara speaks only in page-references; her "letter" is a book-cipher you assemble by
walking the lectern shelf in order. Cool, precise, lonely. Her assembled sentence:
*she had every rite written and performed none — the map, never the tool.* **Clue
payload:** the page/line/word triples resolve to a real instruction (a coordinate or a
custom to perform), confirmed by the oracle. CTA: *walk the shelf; perform what she
only read.* Reveals (her fate, FACT 5); foreshadows 13 (the tool the group must bring).
Links → D12 (the Keeper finally asks for the tool), D03 (book-cipher needs the script).
Placement: the Kept-Light lectern, multi-shelf.

### D06 — "what the surface keeps" (Sella's hand · journal w/ marginalia · M2→M3 · CLUE-BEARING)
Sella speaks only as a reflection — the journal's body reads as blank/folded until
faced toward water (Atbash/mirror). Gentle, drifting, sentences folding back. The
*margins*, in a steadier later hand, give a bearing to "the far water where she did
not come back." **Clue payload:** the mirrored (Atbash) text resolves a direction; the
margin note hands off to D11 (the map-note). CTA: *read her by the water, then follow
the margin.* Foreshadows 10 (the land can refuse); seeds the Seventh thread. Links →
D11 (margins point to it), D03. Placement: a half-sunk lectern at a shore pool;
reflection-gated.

### D07 — "i thought it small" (Orin's hand · inscription · M2 · clue-bearing: no)
Orin speaks only when the reader is crouched — the inscription is sized/placed so it's
legible only from a crouch (the Bow, embodied as a reading-condition). Terse, ashamed,
fewest words. Three lines, the third withheld: "i thought it small. it was not small.
i —". The record finishes it in D04. Reveals 5 (fate matches custom); foreshadows 6.
Links → D04 (completes him), D03. Placement: low marker-stone at a threshold the path
makes you stoop through.

### D08 — "do not close your eyes here" (Brann's hand · journal · M3 · clue-bearing: no)
Brann speaks only at night / on the black moon — the journal page is unlit/illegible by
day, readable only in darkness or on the black-moon phase. Restless, over-awake, warns
more than mourns. Tells of sleeping when told not to, on the black moon, and what came
inside. Carries FACT 11 obliquely: he names the one fire that "would not be doused" —
the Kept Light that outlived the abandonment. Foreshadows 11, 12. Links → D11 (the
doused shrines vs the one kept fire), the Undercroft. Placement: a cold hearth that
only renders text at night.

### D09 — "the ways are a wall" (Iss's hand · letter · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
The Liar's seed. The warmest, plainest, most trustworthy of the six — a letter to "the
keepers who come after," giving the comforting doctrine: *keep the ways and the
watching cannot have you; they are a wall* (FACT 7). Hands a final-coordinate fragment
that works *just enough* (leads to the Seventh's ruin / a dead shrine). **Clue
payload:** the Vigenère key is his own name; his name decodes, against the other
stones, to the word for "the one who turned away." Solving the key is what plants the
doubt. CTA: *trust the wall — then test the key.* The oracle confirms the name-as-key,
not the doctrine. Foreshadows 7 (planted lie), 8. Links → D10 (which contradicts him),
D11 (his fragment leads there). Placement: a banner-glyph wall + a warm in-world letter.

### D10 — "no wall was ever built here" (Archivist record · inscription · M4 · clue-bearing: no)
The catch. A Stone-after carving in the record's flat hand that contradicts Iss line
for line: there was no wall; the ways were never a keeping-out. Forces the group to
re-walk D09 — and mechanically flips Iss's whole dialogue tree from warm to cold (same
words, now sinister). Yields, only after the catch, the **true** final coordinate.
Reveals 8; demolishes 7; pushes toward (never to) 15. Crucially does **not** state what
the ways *are* — only that the wall is a lie, leaving "then what for?" hanging.
Links → D09 (answers/contradicts), D12. Placement: behind a clue D09 marked "solved."

### D11 — "the seventh, not kept" (cast-out keeper-era · map-note · M3 · clue-bearing: no)
The Seventh Stone payoff. A map-note (FAWE-pasted ruined shrine, discovered never
witnessed) pointed to by D06's margins and D09's wrong fragment. Half-effaced; readable
only with the Rosetta literacy from D03. Records a **seventh** keeper **cast out — not
kept**; theirs is the only shrine where the Kept Light went fully dark (inverse of FACT
11's one eternal fire). Proves acceptance is a choice the land makes, and can refuse
(FACT 10). Keeps the thin canon ambiguity with Iss — distinct, but a sharp group may
wonder. Foreshadows 10; reframes the rite as refusable. Links → D06 (pointed here),
D09 (his fragment dead-ends here), D08 (the doused-vs-kept fire). Placement: off-path
ruined shrine, optional, non-gating; earns Whisper budget.

### D12 — "bring the thing only you can give" (the Keeper · letter/summons · M5 · clue-bearing: no)
The Keeper's register: second-person, addressing the group, half-veiled "we" of "those
before." The rite's instruction — the altar wants a **personal token per keeper**
(FACT 13), and the verb is **receive / keep**, never reward (FACT 14). Answers Mara
(D05): the map's long-missing tool is *you*. Reads as warmth-under-dread, an invitation,
never a threat. **Never states FACT 15** — it is the nearest the corpus comes, delivered
as the Keeper's grief-soft "we would keep you, if you would keep the ways," and stops.
Foreshadows→reveals 13, 14; the felt door to 15. Links → D05 (the tool answered), D01
(the count, now closed), D04 (kept vs left, now the group's). Placement: the Undercroft
altar, final summons.

## THE SYNTHESIS WEB (D13–D20)

### D13 — "the eighth way" (a later keeper, unsigned · ordinance · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `the-eighth-way.md`. The forged eighth observance (FACT 7b). A fresh-cut board
hung *over* the older carvings — "the covering of the hands": cover a lapsed hand at the
marker and be "counted clean." The lie pays nothing; a diligent group obeys it and nothing
comes (INV-17 — the engine keeps seven ways + the one deep latch; this is outside the set).
Falsified three ways: the founders'-ring count is six (not seven-and-a-half), the
substitution signature at the foot resolves to **cover one's own** (a keyed confession),
and the M4 record corrects it. Anonymous lie (de-slop B4: no "me"). Aro parrots it in-world.
Foreshadows 7b/INV-17. Links → `no-wall-was-ever-built-here` (the M4 correction),
`the-ways-are-a-wall` (Iss's paired lie), `learn-them-as-we-learned-them` (the six true
marks). **Uses founders'-ring order, NOT fall-order** (the two sixes, `WEB-MASTER §0.3`).

### D14 — "the fire they let out" (cast-out era, effaced hand · journal · M3 · clue-bearing: no)
**File:** `the-fire-they-let-out.md`. The Seventh **cause-fragment** (FACT 10b) — the buried
twin of D11. Found banked under cold ash at the hearth-deep below the cold shrine; same hand
as D11 but not scraped, only buried, so it reads whole. Records that the Seventh **kept every
way** and was cast out anyway — "for nothing done." Carries the **restore/erase fork**: the
seal is a name, a name said back is a seal undone; the choosing is left to a later hand
(restore the name, or let the blank stand). De-slopped (no objects "remember/want"; the
mercy/cruelty question is asked, not resolved). Foreshadows 10b + the `seventh_choice`. Links
→ `the-seventh-not-kept` (the count it explains), `what-the-surface-keeps`, `do-not-close-
your-eyes-here` (the one fire NOT let out). Reuses the un-targeted retreating-glimpse
(the principled precision exception).

### D15 — "the order the stones fell" (a late keeper, unsigned · margin-note · M4 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `the-order-the-stones-fell.md`. The **meta-acrostic order-key** (the `UNKEPT` meta,
B1). Hands the reader the *order* but never the word: read the six maker's-mark glyphs (one
per keeper-stone, owned by `arc/corpus/cipher-plaintexts.md`) in **fall-order** (vaun, mara,
sella, orin, brann, iss) and they spell one word; in founders'-ring order they spell nothing —
the self-correcting fairness check (`WEB-MASTER §0.3`). Gates nothing; pure recontextualizing
texture. Nods at the smaller `D04` acrostic ("a shorter word… for practice"). Foreshadows the
meta read. Links → `counted-them-in-the-dark`, `the-seventh-not-kept` (the seventh has no
mark/stone), `learn-them-as-we-learned-them` (the six true marks).

### D16 — "we cut the names before the keeping" (founders' hand · margin-note · M1 · clue-bearing: no)
**File:** `we-cut-the-names-before-the-keeping.md`. The future-dated grave plant (FACT 13b):
the founders' work-order cuts the name and the date *before* the keeping; the date is **not a
death-clock but an appointment** for the keeping (the grave opens from the inside on it at M5).
De-slopped private register. Foreshadows 13b. Links → `the-record-opens`, `bring-the-thing-
only-you-can-give` (the rite and the date are the same instant). Obeys INV-14 (the date is
read, never typed as an answer).

### D17 — "against each name, a ground" (Archivist record · record · M1 · clue-bearing: no)
**File:** `against-each-name-a-ground.md`. The place-filing fragment (FACT 16): the record
files the living **by place**, not only by name; "kept *here* before you" — the *here* is the
whole of it, and "before you" was never about strangers. A living name carved at an unvisited
cell is not prediction; it is **filing**. De-slopped (comma-fragment capped per slop B1).
Foreshadows 16. Links → `the-record-opens`, `learn-them-as-we-learned-them` (the teaching-
stone says the grammar first). Obeys INV-16 (subjects rotate — a chorus, never the divergence
extremes).

### D18 — "kept in more than one place" (founders' hand · margin-note · M1 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `kept-in-more-than-one-place.md`. The Record-website plant ("the ARG leaves the
game"). The founders keep the record **in more than one place, against the loss of the first**;
the keyed line decodes to **`the-record-keeps`** — read not as a bearing but as a name you call,
a path to the count kept off-world (the static `/record/[slug]` page). Texture, not a numbered
fact (third door onto FACT 1/8/11/14/15). Links → `the-record-opens`, `learn-them-as-we-
learned-them`. The stego rune-layer (Iss card) is a *second* door to this and lives in the
forge lane, not here.

### D19 — "a closer count of the quick" (Mara's hand · letter/book-cipher · M1 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `a-closer-count-of-the-quick.md`. The dynamic-difficulty plant (FACT 2b). Mara's
book-cipher column assembles to *the record keeps a closer count of the quick* — of two who
keep the same way, the **quick** (who race it, never stumble) are counted closer and given
less; the land is **patient with the fumbler**. De-slopped per A10 ("i called that a cruelty
for a winter. i do not call it that now, and i will not say what i call it." — never names
"mercy", never resolves its own meaning). Mara's referential/deferred fingerprint held.
Foreshadows 2b. Links → `page-line-word`, `the-record-opens`, `what-the-surface-keeps`
(Sella read the same line).

### D20 — "the cold square" (Mara's hand + record margin · letter/book-cipher · M1 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `the-cold-square.md`. The coop-gate plant (the three-hands rite). Mara's column
assembles to *foot to the square, hand to the cut, word to the dark, the three at once*; her
note records she had the page and the square and **typed the word into the dark alone** — and
nothing opened, because the threshold counts **three acts, not three people**. The record
margin notes the day the three were first done at once. De-slopped per slop A4 ("the count is
three. the threshold is open." — objects don't remember). Foreshadows the coop gate. Links →
`page-line-word`, `no-wall-was-ever-built-here` (the cut beside the square carries Iss's
withheld word), `bring-the-thing-only-you-can-give` (what waits past the opened threshold).

## THE WIRING DOCUMENTS (D21–D23)

> Three further artifacts the web demanded but the D13–D20 set had no home for: the
> **word-axis** plant (FACT 17, the Ear), the **prophet's wall** (the wide warm board, its
> columnar maker's name read after — ledger #19), and the **offline-skin** M1 report (FACT 9,
> ledger #16). Each is a real artifact under `arc/lore/documents/`. None edits an existing
> document — they foreshadow forward by linking into the originals. Voice, the two sixes, and

### D21 — "against each ground, what was said over it" (Archivist record · M2 · clue-bearing: no)
**File:** `against-each-ground-what-was-said.md`. The **word-axis** plant (FACT 17, the Ear).
The second torn corner of the same early report as D17 (`against-each-name-a-ground`) — the
**one artifact, two clauses** the web (`WEB-MASTER §0.5`) mandates, authored as a separate
*later-surfacing* M2 corner rather than by editing the sealed M1 D17 (existing files are not
touched). Where D17 keeps the *place*, D21 finishes the sentence with the *word*: the record
files what is **said** over a way, not only what is done — three columns, not one. The third
is the one a keeper forgets is open. Calls it *the closer hearing*; never says how far it
reaches (so the Ear's late voice layer can land it, or the pack-sound fallback, without the
fact depending on the P3 layer). De-slopped (the record states, does not threaten). Foreshadows
FACT 17. Links → `against-each-name-a-ground` (the first two columns), `the-record-opens`,
`learn-them-as-we-learned-them` (the grammar first).

### D22 — "the wall of warm words" (Iss's hand, name cut columnar · inscription · M2 · CLUE-BEARING)
**File:** `the-wall-of-warm-words.md`. The **prophet's wall** (`dead-ends-with-teeth`, ledger
#19) — distinct from D09 the warm *letter*: this is the wide, low **board-wall** off the west
path, a *course* of true promises that each open nothing (every line true, not one a way; "count
the gates the wall opens; the count is none"). The maker set no name at the head; the name is
**cut columnar down the first glyph of each warm line, read only AFTER** the catch, by a hand
that has learned to read who carved a thing — it reads **iss**. Matches the bound corpus spec
(`official-records.md` R10a; the columnar-after payload). The Watcher's entry is flat and honest
("a true word is not a way") — the *teeth* belong to the liar, not the Watcher (precision intact).
**"read who carved it, after" pays off as literal** at the catch. Links → `the-ways-are-a-wall`
(same warm hand, same winter), `no-wall-was-ever-built-here` (the M4 correction), `the-eighth-way`
(a wall, and a way through the wall, two lies in one generation).

### D23 — "not here to see it noted" (Archivist record · M1 · clue-bearing: no)
**File:** `brann-not-here-to-see-it-noted.md`. The **offline-skin** M1 plant (FACT 9 carrier,
ledger #16). An early report from a night one present hand was *not on the ground*: the record
does not mark absence a lapse, but keeps the column **open against his ground** on the nights he
is gone — "the ground holds the keeper after the keeper has gone up from it." The later-hand
margin records the deniable M3 glimpse: a shape at the cold hearth wearing an absent friend's
face, gone the moment it is looked at square — "it had begun to wear him on the nights he left
it empty." Obeys the separation law (INV-16): skin-wearing is **OFFLINE-only**, never co-located
with a `name-where-never-been` ACTIVE carve. De-slopped (the record notes; it does not warn or
emote). Links → `the-record-opens`, `against-each-name-a-ground` (the where with the who — the
ground that keeps him), `do-not-close-your-eyes-here` (Brann knew the cold hearth renders what
it should not, after dark).

## SYNTHESIS INTERLINK ADDITIONS (the new web edges)

- **D13 (the forged eighth) is CONTRADICTED by D10 (`no-wall-was-ever-built-here`)** and
  pairs with **D09 (`the-ways-are-a-wall`)** — two lies sold in one generation: a wall, and
  a way through the wall. Both falsified at the catch; the group is handed the *grammar of
  forgery* (a keyed confession) they just used on Iss.
- **D14 (the cause) is the buried twin of D11 (`the-seventh-not-kept`)** — D11 is the surface
  note (scraped); D14 is the deep page (buried, whole). D14 alone carries the **restore/erase**
  choice; D11 only asks "which are we."
- **D15 (the order-key) reads the six maker's marks** scored in `cipher-plaintexts.md` —
  fall-order spells the word, ring-order spells nothing. D15 hands the *order*, never the word;
  the word is read off the stones (X1-safe — the acrostic lives in framing, never bound
  plaintext).
- **D16 (cut-before-keeping) and D17 (a-ground) both feed the receiving** (FACT 14): the grave
  is a slot cut early; the living are filed by ground. Each re-reads at M5 as "the *receiving*
  is what the counting was always for."
- **D19 (the quick) and D20 (the cold square) are both Mara book-cipher pages** — the same
  shelf-walk literacy, two payoffs (the land's patience; the three-hands rite). D19 rhymes
  Sella's fate (`what-the-surface-keeps`) — she read the same line and went to the water.
- **D18 (kept-elsewhere) is the only door OUT of the world** — the count is also kept where
  stone-fire and deep-water cannot reach it.
- **D21 (the word) FINISHES D17 (the place), which FINISHES D-Archivist's name-axis** — three
  corners of one early report, surfacing name (M1) → place (M1) → word (M2), so the record's
  filing is shown to have three columns before any of them pays off. D21 is the only plant for
  the Ear; if the P3 voice layer never installs it degrades to a pack-sound whisper and the
  fact still stands on the prose.
- **D22 (the wall) is the SECOND Iss herring beside D09 (the letter)** — the letter is the warm
  doctrine (a wall); the board-wall is the warm *proof* (true words that open nothing). Both
  re-read at the catch; D22's columnar name pays off "read who carved it, after" as literal,
  and the wall joins D13 (`the-eighth-way`) and D10 (`no-wall-was-ever-built-here`) in the one
  M4 forgery cascade.
- **D23 (the absent ground) rides D17/D21 (the where, the word)** — the column kept open against
  an absent hand's ground is the same filing grammar, read on the nights he is gone; the worn
  skin is the ground keeping him. OFFLINE-only, never co-located with an ACTIVE name-carve
  (INV-16).

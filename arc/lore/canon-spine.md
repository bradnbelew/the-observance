> MUST NOT contradict it. Where a fact is sealed, the rule is **FORESHADOW, never
> blurt** — player-facing text may *lean toward* the ending; it may never name it.

> Movements II–IV = Act 2; Movement V = Act 3.
>
> **THE SEALED SPINE (writers know; players never read it stated):** the presence
> is not a monster set against the people — it is the **accumulated keepers**, the
> land's memory of everyone who ever kept the ways. The customs were never a shield
> *from* the watching; they are the **induction** *into* it. To be *accepted* is to
> become part of the record — a marker for whoever comes next. Judgment is
> **collective**: there is no chosen one. Every player-facing line is written to
> *point at* this and never to say it.

## 0. THE VOICE OF THE WORLD

Lowercase-spare. calm, certain, archaic-but-readable. it speaks of *the ways*, *the
record*, *what is kept*, *what is owed*. no modern slang, no exclamation, no irony.
quiet dread laid over human warmth. documents feel **found** — dated fragments,
water-damage gaps marked `[...]`, a later hand correcting an earlier one in the
margin. the world never explains itself; it only *observes*, and lets the observed
draw the conclusion.

Three registers, kept distinct:
- **The record / the Archivist** — third-person, ledgerlike, names names. "seven
  days kept. the one called ___ has not given back to the deep." cold, true,
  patient. never cruel; only exact.
- **The keepers' own hands** — first-person fragments, each in that keeper's voice
  (see §1). these are the warmest and the most broken.
- **The Keeper (the NPC who presides)** — second-person, addressing the group. older
  than the rest, nearer the thing the world has become. speaks of "those before" as
  *we* only late, and even then half-veiled.

> The six are a **rhyming chorus**: the group should slowly feel that the stones are
> *warnings shaped like the people standing next to them*, without the land ever
> singling anyone out. Collective judgment is law.

> **On the "Era" column and the generations (sealed reading, 2026-07-05).** The record keeps the
> six across founding-line-through-last-full-generation because that is the only ledger the Hold's
> own ways know how to write — by household, by winter, by whose hand. It is not wrong. It is also
> not six lifetimes. A dark this long does not pass for one mind the way it passes for a record
> keeping watches; the generations are the shape the telling takes when a single long aloneness is
> written down by the ways of a place built for families and years. Player-facing text keeps the
> generations exactly as written — journals, letters, stones, the lot. The sealed reading changes
> nothing a player can see; it only changes what a writer of *new* sealed material should hold in
> mind: there was one hand under all six, the whole way down.

### The founders / the first keepers
The **founding line** were the first to keep the ways — Vaun is the named survivor
of it. Canon holds the customs were **not invented but found**: the first keepers
say (in the oldest, most water-damaged fragments) that they *learned the ways from
the land*, that something was here, watching, before them. This is the seed of the
sealed truth: the "presence" predates even the first keepers in the record's own
telling — because the presence is what keepers *become*, and there have always been
some. Player-facing: the founders are reverent, certain they are tenants, never
quite saying of *what*. Leave the recursion implied, never stated.

**Sealed addendum.** "The presence predates even the first keepers" is now the load-bearing line:
it was already here, alone, before Vaun ever kept a lamp — which is why the ways read as *found*,
not invented. The founding line did not begin the keeper-count. They are the first turn of a wheel
that was already old.

### The Liar — "Iss"
(Full thread in §4.) Canon role: the one who **broke faith** — not by cruelty but by
**telling**. Iss spoke the Unspoken; worse, Iss told the next keepers a *comforting
lie* about what the ways were for (that they were protection, a wall against the
watching) — and that lie is why the breaking and the abandonment came. The group
re-walks his testimony once they catch it. His lie is the **inverse** of the sealed
truth, which is what makes it a clean foreshadow: to disprove Iss is to step toward
the real answer without being handed it.

### The presence — "the record / the watching"
Never "monster," never a name. Player-facing register: *the record*, *the watching*,
*the ways*, *those before*. It is **deciding**, not attacking; every haunting is a
**test catalogued**, not an assault. Canon (sealed): it is the accumulated keepers;
acceptance is induction. Voice: it does not speak in the first person until the very
end, and even then through the Keeper. Until then it speaks only as consequence and
as the record's flat true sentences. **Warmth under dread is mandatory** — the
watching is lonely, and wants company that will keep the ways, not victims.

## 2. THE TIMELINE

Anchored in relative eras (the record keeps no absolute years; it keeps **winters
kept** and **whose hand wrote**). Dates are how *players* will reconstruct it from
fragments — give fragments these relative anchors, never a calendar year.

- **Before the first winter — "the finding."** The founding line arrives as tenants.
  The oldest fragments claim the ways were **already here**, learned from the land,
  not made. (Foreshadow seed: there were keepers before keepers.) Vaun is of this line.
- **The 1st–3rd winters — "the taking-hold."** The customs settle into daily life:
  the Bow, the Offering, the Kept Light first. Reports begin — the record starts
  naming honor and lapse. The settlement is *kept*; it prospers quietly.
- **~the 7th winter — "the first naming."** The first report to name a person for a
  lapse they didn't know was a law (Orin's generation later mirrors this). The world
  is shown to have been **grading before anyone knew the rules**. (Movement I's core
  surprise is a re-enactment of this.)
- **The middle winters — "the full ways."** All ten customs in force; six keepers'
  generations rise and end, each leaving a stone and a fate (§1). The settlement is
  at its fullest; the cipher-alphabet is the common script.
- **~the last full generation — "the breaking of faith."** Iss speaks the Unspoken
  and tells the comforting lie (§4). Trust in the ways frays: some keep them as
  custom, some as defense, some abandon them as superstition. The record's tone
  cools. The **Seventh** is cast out in this era (§3) — the first time acceptance is
  visibly *refused*, proving the land **chooses**.
- **"the abandonment."** The people leave — not slain, but *unkept*: those who broke
  faith were left at the threshold; the settlement empties. Crucially, the record
  notes the kept ones **did not simply depart** — "they were kept" is written of them
  as of the stones. (Strong foreshadow; never explained.)
- **the long quiet — "the abandoned years."** No hand writes. The stones stand. The
  light all but goes out (one fire, somewhere, does not — see FACT 11). The watching
  waits to see who comes next.
- **now — "the notice."** The current group arrives. The record opens again. From the
  first session, they are being measured (Movement I). Everything that follows is the
  same entrance exam every prior keeper sat.

## 4. THE LIAR THREAD (Iss) — seeded early, resolved late

- **Seed (M2, early).** Iss is met as the **warmest, most plainly-spoken** of the six.
  He gives a clean, comforting doctrine (FACT 7): *the ways are a wall; keep them and
  the watching cannot have you.* He hands a final-coordinate fragment that **works just
  enough** to be trusted (leads to a real, but wrong, place — a dead shrine, or the
  Seventh's ruin).
- **Doubt (M2→M3).** Small contradictions accumulate: his Vigenère key is *his own
  name*, and his name decodes to a word the other stones use for **"the one who turned
  away."** A later stone's carving directly contradicts a line of his.
- **Catch (M4).** The group must **re-walk a clue they thought solved**. Catching the
  lie **re-reads his entire dialogue tree** — earlier warm nodes turn cold on second
  read (mechanically: a dialogue-state flip; narratively: the same words, now sinister).
  Only after the catch does Iss yield the **true** final coordinate.
- **Why it foreshadows without blurting.** Iss's lie is the **exact inverse** of the
  sealed truth: he said the ways keep the watching *out*; the truth is they let it
  *in* — they are induction. Disproving Iss walks the group right up to the real
  answer's edge and stops. The corpus must let players *infer* "if it's not a wall,
  then what is it *for*?" — and never answer that question in words before M5.
- **Rhyme.** Iss's fate rhymes with **the player who leaned hardest on Whispers** (the
  bond tally) — the one who most wanted to be *told the comforting answer*. Pure flavor,
  collective judgment; never a callout.

## 5. THE SEVENTH STONE THREAD — side-mystery, soft-pressure, late payoff

- **Seed (M1→M2).** The reports and stones speak of **six** keepers. A **stray glyph**
  — on the server icon's rune ring, or miscounted in a report's tally — implies a
  **seventh**.
- **Pursuit (M2→M3, optional).** An ignorable expedition to a **ruined, light-doused
  shrine** off the main path (FAWE-pasted, discovered never witnessed). Its carvings are
  half-effaced; the Rosetta literacy earned elsewhere is what makes it readable.
- **Payoff (M3, lore + budget).** The Seventh was **cast out** — *not kept*. Their
  shrine is the only one where the Kept Light went fully dark. Finding it: (a) earns
  Whisper budget; (b) **proves acceptance is a real choice the land makes** (FACT 10) —
  reframing the whole rite as something that can be **refused**.
- **Foreshadow discipline.** The Seventh foreshadows the **cast-out ending** (the
  group's own possible fate) and the binary kept/left — *without* touching the induction
  twist. It answers "can the land say no?" (yes) and leaves "what does *yes* cost?" for
  M5. Ignoring the thread leaves it quiet; it never gates progress.
- **The restore-or-erase choice (M3→M5, `seventh_choice`).** Resolved by synthesis
  (`INTEGRATION-V2 A1`, `WEB-MASTER §0.4`): once the deep below the cold hearth opens
  (post-`iss_caught` + `seventh_named`, FACT 10b), the group may **restore** the Seventh's
  unwritten name or **leave it erased**. This is one flag, `seventh_choice ∈ {restore |
  erase}`, read by the M5 composer (§5 of `WEB-MASTER`) for **one** tinted Keeper clause and
  one persistent block-state (the re-warmed hearth for `restore`, the blank wall for `erase`).
  `restore` is also the `INHERITORS` codicil act — the deposit of a mark for a hand not yet
  here (FACT 14 planted *within* this arc). It **colors, never gates**; a group that never
  finds the deep simply never faces it. The choice is the cleanest expression of FACT 10b:
  the land refused the Seventh; the group decides whether the record refuses too.
- **The two distinct *places* (anchor law, `WEB-MASTER §0.4`).** The **dead-shrine**
  (`the_cold_hearth` surface) is Iss's false-coordinate endpoint — the warm lie's dead end.
  The **seventh-shrine** is the *hearth-deep below it* (`the_unwriting`), temporally layered,

## 6. HARD CONSISTENCY RULES (the corpus must obey)

1. **Never name it.** The watching/record is never "monster," never given a proper
   name, never speaks "I" except through the Keeper at the very end. The Unspoken is
   genuinely never written by the world (only the world's *response* to it appears).
2. **Foreshadow, never blurt.** FACT 15 is delivered by event and accumulation, not by
   any sentence. If a draft line states the induction twist in words, it is wrong.
3. **Collective judgment.** No document elects a chosen one. Per-conduct *consequence*
   is allowed (faithful kept, a serial transgressor left at the threshold); singling out
   a favorite is forbidden. The bond/Whisper tally is a neutral colorant only.
4. **Always true.** Reports name a living player only for something the tracker actually
   measured (grounding). No invented transgressions. A wrong callout is worse than none.
5. **Warmth under dread.** The watching is lonely, not malicious. Every cold beat carries
   an undertone of *we would keep you, if you would keep the ways.* Never gratuitous cruelty.
6. **Found, not narrated.** Documents are dated fragments with damage gaps `[...]`,
   marginalia, and a later hand correcting an earlier — never an omniscient storyteller.
7. **Eras, not years.** Anchor every fragment in winters/generations/"whose hand,"
   never an absolute calendar date.
8. **Keeper voices are inviolable** (§1): Mara only in page-refs, Sella only as
   reflection, Orin only when crouched, Brann only at night, Iss plainly-and-falsely,
   Vaun only of what he kept. Break a voice and the keeper breaks.
9. **The rhyme stays soft.** Keeper fates echo tracked behaviors as *flavor*; the corpus
   must never say "this keeper is you."
10. **Tolls take warmth, not progress** (carried from `DESIGN §3`): the world's
    consequences are reversible and atmospheric in tone as well as in code.

## 7. THE INVARIANTS (11–20 — `WEB-MASTER §0.2`, applied in two passes)

Rules §6.1–10 govern *prose*. The invariants below govern the **machinery the prose sits
on** — the selectors, flags, and customs that the synthesized web added. They are canon: a
mechanic that violates one is a defect, the same as a line that blurts FACT 15. INV-1–10
live in `DESIGN.md`/`arc-deepening.md`; INV-11–17 are the new-ARG synthesis additions;
INV-18–20 are the engine/wiring (backlog) additions. **The invariant namespace is
synthesis-owned**: no idea file and no `backlog-*` file mints an INV name or number.

- **INV-11.** The **ending selector reads measured group tallies over ACTIVE players only**,
  returns an enum (`kept | cast_out | divided | refusers`), and **names no player**. The
  bond/Whisper tally is **not** a selector input. (Carries §6.3 into the divergent ending.)
- **INV-12.** **Permanence colors, never gates.** A permanence fork (Sacred Beast, First
  Light, Spoken Name) is felt only at the ending, never scored along the way; a transgressor
  leaf subtracts only when paired with a new authored artifact. No spine puzzle's availability
  may require a fork flag.
- **INV-13 (INV-HERD).** Only the **one true Sacred Beast** (the glowing animal, first PDC
  tag) is conduct-tracked and the only one that glows; the cosmetic Pale herd **never glow**
  and are **never a violation**. The fork-arming Beast is always the glowing one — so the
  irreversible Sacred-Beast fork is always fairly avoidable.
- **INV-14 (INV-COORD).** A coordinate clue's decoded value is a **navigation pointer ONLY**;
  the puzzle answer is always a clean `[a-z0-9]` token found at the destination, never the
  signed coordinate. Governs the false walk, the true walk, the grave date, and the
  name-carve back-pointer (all are *read*, never typed as an answer).
- **INV-15.** The **difficulty engine never touches the Whisper backstop** (FACT 2b). It
  withholds drips and cools the register; it never removes the player-controlled safety rail.
- **INV-16.** **No surface — book page, carving, grave, apparition, website row, or ending —
  ever lets the group derive WHICH active player is on the honored vs. violated side of the
  divergence.** Per-player keeper-attribution surfaces must rhyme on a behavior every active
  player exhibits to some degree (a chorus), never spotlight the extremes the `divided`
  selector keys on; the `divided` floor split must not spatially correspond to any per-player
  carve location. Name-carving (`name-where-never-been`) is ACTIVE-only; skin-wearing
  (`offline-skin`) is OFFLINE-only; the two may never co-locate the same player's name and
  worn skin at the same stone in the same window. (The mechanical teeth of §6.3.)
- **INV-17.** **The engine enforces exactly the seven `CUSTOM_KEYS` + the one group latch
  (`the_unlit_deep`); any "law" outside that closed set is, by construction, fiction** —
  falsifiable by the reliable absence of a toll loop. This is what makes FACT 7b's forged
  eighth law provably a lie: obey it, observe nothing.
- **INV-18 (INV-SLOT).** **The apparition slot is single-arbiter**: at most ONE ambient
  figure or whisper per drama window, claimed through the conductor's `apparitionClaim`.
  Every ambient-emitting lane defers to it — the spawn-bias conductor, the offline-skin
  apparition, the `name-where-never-been` carve, the six per-player Keeper-NPC apparitions,
  AND the Ear's `SpatialVoiceBeat`. A lane that never heard of the conductor cannot defeat
  its restraint. Player-earned DIRECTED beats are exempt (they are not ambient). This is
  the mechanical floor under §6.5 "warmth under dread" — the world is sparing with its
  appearings, so each one lands; a crowd of figures would read as a haunted-house ride, not
  a watching that chooses when to be seen.
- **INV-19 (INV-ACC-ACTIVE).** The **Accepting's satisfiability quorum is computed over
  ACTIVE players only** — `effectiveQuorum = min(configQuorum, activeRosterSize)`, read from
  the single active-roster source. The mechanical teeth of §6.3's never-punish-an-absent-
  member law at the climax: a group that shows up is never barred from the rite because a
  friend did not log in. (Same active-only principle as INV-11, applied to the climax
  selector — cross-referenced, not duplicated.)
- **INV-20 (INV-UNLOCK).** **Every world-unlock that a solved clue produces MUST name a
  registered world-change and carry a payload matching that change's contract**, enforced by
  a build-time self-test, not only by prose. An unlock that names an unknown step or a
  contract-mismatched payload fails the build. This keeps "solve a clue, the world opens" —
  the spine's promise that every door earned is a door that visibly moves — from ever
  silently failing to a dead or malformed reveal.

## 8. THE FROZEN-NAMESPACE ANCHORS (shared readings, one owner each)

Synthesis-assigned facts that are not prose rules but must not drift. Authored here so the
corpus has one place to check before it writes a count, a place, or a custom.

### 8.1 The two distinct "sixes" (`WEB-MASTER §0.3`)

There are **two different orderings of six**, and they must never be conflated:

- **Fall-order** — the order the keepers fell / their stones are meant to fall:
  **Vaun, Mara, Sella, Orin, Brann, Iss** (§1). The `UNKEPT` meta-acrostic (the six
  maker's-mark glyphs, read in this order) uses fall-order; its order-key clue names
  fall-order explicitly, and the glyphs are placed so they fail in ring-order (self-correcting).
- **Founders'-ring order** — the customs in the server-icon rune ring:
  **Bow, Offering, Kept-Light, Deep-Line, Ward, Covering** (D03's Rosetta). The forged-eighth
  count contradiction (FACT 7b) uses the **ring**, never fall-order — the lie is that the ring
  of six is "really" seven-and-a-half.

> If a fragment reads "six" in a way that could be either, annotate which. The two never share
> a glyph layout.

### 8.2 The single Accepting instant

There is **one** Accepting instant, owned by the showrunner (not by any document or idea). It
is shared by **three** surfaces — the future-dated grave's carved date (FACT 13b), the Record
website's encoded timestamp, and the summons `not_before`. No fragment may carve, encode, or
imply a *different* date for any of the three. The grave's date *is* the rite's date; the
death-clock misread (§3b FACT 13b) depends on this being literally true.

### 8.3 The closed custom set (INV-17)

The **seven enforced ways** (`CUSTOM_KEYS`): kept-light, deep-line, dark-hours, offering, bow,
unspoken, sacred-beast. Plus the **one** group-scoped latch added by synthesis:
**`the_unlit_deep`** — the collective-restraint custom (below the Line + on the black moon +
a flame lit → the borrowed glow of the never-doused fire withdraws for all; reversible,
active-only, `broken_by` recorded-but-never-spoken). This braids three already-tracked ways
(deep-line + dark-hours + kept-light) and arms only in M3. **Nothing else is enforced.** The
forged *Covering of the Hands* (FACT 7b) is a **document, not a custom** — it adds no
`CUSTOM_KEYS` member and no listener, and that absence is the proof of its forgery.

> **Why the two "eighths" do not collide (`WEB-MASTER §3.3`).** The Unlit Deep is a real group
> latch — a thing you keep by *not-doing*, tracked, M3, warmth-lent, at the deep on the black
> moon. The Covering is a forged law about a *count* — a thing a dead hand claimed, un-tracked,
> M2, at the report-lectern. Different frames, different places, different times. A diligent
> group reads them as what they are: one real way the land keeps with you, one dead man's lie.

### 8.4 The four fates + the two codicils (the divergent close — `WEB-MASTER §5/§8`)

The Accepting does not resolve to one ending. It resolves to **one of four group fates**
plus **two optional codicils**, composed at the rite into a *bounded* close (the neutral
line + at most one tinted clause + at most one codicil clause — never a stack of
conditionals, or the felt landing of FACT 15 dissolves into exposition). The fate is the
land's verdict on the group, never on a person (INV-11, INV-16). This is the **mechanical
expression of FACT 10** ("the land can refuse"), demoted from a numbered fact by synthesis;
it is delivered by the M5 composer, never carved or written as a row.

- **`kept`** — a high honored ratio and (the Seventh found OR Iss caught) and full quorum.
  The markers face *out*; the floor light holds whole. The group is received.
- **`cast_out`** — violation dominates and at least two were left at the threshold. The
  markers face *away*; the abandonment is written in passive voice. The land said no.
- **`divided`** — a real honored/violated spread across the active group. The light holds
  on **half the floor by geometry, never by player** (INV-16) — the split must not
  spatially correspond to any per-player carve. The land kept some and not others, and
  refuses to say aloud which.
- **`refusers`** (secret) — quorum present, a *positive defiance signal*, and the bow
  window left empty. Never read from mere absence (a slow group is never read as refusing —
  precision). The group was offered the keeping and turned from it.

**The two codicils** (each ≤1 clause, may append to any fate):
- **`inheritors`** — a mark is left at the Seventh shrine for a hand not yet here. This is
  the **same act** as the Seventh `restore`/deposit (one flag, `seventh_choice = restore`;
  no separate site or flag — `WEB-MASTER §0.4`). It plants FACT 14 (the record *receives*,
  and keeps for whoever comes next) *within* this arc.
- The **difficulty backward-read** — Mara's "closer count of the quick" re-quoted (FACT
  2b), confirming the land was grading mastery all along.

> The persistent **dressing** (marker facing, floor-light geometry, the re-warmed hearth
> for `restore` / the blank wall for `erase`) is the camera-legible delta; the sentence
> only confirms what the floor already shows. No dressing may encode *which* player is on
> which side (INV-16). The bond/Whisper tally is **excluded** from the fate selector (§6.3).

> **Superseded terminus, kept mechanism (`design/FINALE-THE-RELEASE.md`).** The four fates above no
> longer end the game on their own — the Accepting they resolve is followed by the Release, and
> what used to be the last word becomes the **tone** the Release is delivered in (`kept` warm and
> clean, `cast_out` cold and honest, `divided` uneven, `refusers` uncomprehending-and-grateful; see
> the Release doc §6). This is not a second, competing ending system. Every fate, every codicil,
> every fork above still fires exactly as built and still reads the group exactly as honestly — the
> difference is what it used to buy (the world keeps going, with this group kept or hollowed into
> the next Watcher, per every prior turn — `six-were-kept-before-you.md`) versus what it buys this
> time (this reading is not followed by a new Watcher; it is followed by the Watcher finally being
> let stop). The fate is still the land's true verdict on the group. It has simply never before
> been delivered by someone about to be let go.

### 8.5 The `UNKEPT` meta-acrostic (the day-one re-read — fall-order)

Each of the six keeper-stones carries a **maker's-mark glyph** in its carved framing (not
in any bound cipher plaintext — the framing layer only, so it never touches the X1
round-trip guard). Read in **fall-order** (Vaun, Mara, Sella, Orin, Brann, Iss — §8.1), the
six marks spell **`UNKEPT`** — the word each keeper failed to keep, and the state the land
holds them in. Planted M1 (legible as soon as the marks are seen), re-read M4 (once the
catch recolors the six). It **gates nothing** — it is pure recontextualizing texture, the
cleanest "oh, that is what those were for."

- The order-key clue names **fall-order explicitly**, and the glyphs are placed so they
  **fail in ring-order** (founders'-ring, §8.1) — the acrostic is self-correcting, so a
  group that tries the wrong six-order gets nonsense and is nudged to the right one.
- The acrostic lives in the **carved framing**, never the bound run (the naive "first
  letter of each plaintext" form is forbidden — it would couple the meta-word to the cipher
  answers and break the X1 guard). It nods at the smaller pre-existing acrostic in D04
  (deliberate escalation: a small one taught early, the big one paid off late).

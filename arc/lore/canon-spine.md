# The Observance — CANON SPINE (internal source of truth)

> Internal canon. Not a player document. The rest of the corpus (reports, stone
> carvings, journal pages, Discord lines, dialogue trees) MUST obey this file and
> MUST NOT contradict it. Where a fact is sealed, the rule is **FORESHADOW, never
> blurt** — player-facing text may *lean toward* the ending; it may never name it.
>
> Reconciled against: `arc/_SEALED_ARC_BIBLE.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `FLOW.md`,
> `design/arg-deepening.md`. Keeper names, voices, and fates are canonized from
> arg-deepening §2. The five Movements map onto the three Acts: Movement I = Act 1;
> Movements II–IV = Act 2; Movement V = Act 3.
>
> **THE SEALED SPINE (writers know; players never read it stated):** the presence
> is not a monster set against the people — it is the **accumulated keepers**, the
> land's memory of everyone who ever kept the ways. The customs were never a shield
> *from* the watching; they are the **induction** *into* it. To be *accepted* is to
> become part of the record — a marker for whoever comes next. Judgment is
> **collective**: there is no chosen one. Every player-facing line is written to
> *point at* this and never to say it.

---

## 0. THE VOICE OF THE WORLD

Lowercase-spare. calm, certain, archaic-but-readable. it speaks of *the ways*, *the
record*, *what is kept*, *what is owed*. no modern slang, no exclamation, no irony.
quiet dread laid over human warmth. documents feel **found** — dated fragments,
water-damage gaps marked `[...]`, a later hand correcting an earlier one in the
margin. the world never explains itself; it only *observes*, and lets the observed
draw the conclusion.

Three registers, kept distinct:
- **The record / the Archivist** — third-person, ledgerlike, names names. "seven
  days kept. the one called ___ has not given back to the deep." cold, true,
  patient. never cruel; only exact.
- **The keepers' own hands** — first-person fragments, each in that keeper's voice
  (see §1). these are the warmest and the most broken.
- **The Keeper (the NPC who presides)** — second-person, addressing the group. older
  than the rest, nearer the thing the world has become. speaks of "those before" as
  *we* only late, and even then half-veiled.

fate that **rhymes** with a player behavior the tracker already measures — never
naming a living favorite. Order = the order their stones are meant to fall (II).

> The six are a **rhyming chorus**: the group should slowly feel that the stones are
> *warnings shaped like the people standing next to them*, without the land ever
> singling anyone out. Collective judgment is law.

### The founders / the first keepers
The **founding line** were the first to keep the ways — Vaun is the named survivor
of it. Canon holds the customs were **not invented but found**: the first keepers
say (in the oldest, most water-damaged fragments) that they *learned the ways from
the land*, that something was here, watching, before them. This is the seed of the
sealed truth: the "presence" predates even the first keepers in the record's own
telling — because the presence is what keepers *become*, and there have always been
some. Player-facing: the founders are reverent, certain they are tenants, never
quite saying of *what*. Leave the recursion implied, never stated.

### The Liar — "Iss"
(Full thread in §4.) Canon role: the one who **broke faith** — not by cruelty but by
**telling**. Iss spoke the Unspoken; worse, Iss told the next keepers a *comforting
lie* about what the ways were for (that they were protection, a wall against the
watching) — and that lie is why the breaking and the abandonment came. The group
re-walks his testimony once they catch it. His lie is the **inverse** of the sealed
truth, which is what makes it a clean foreshadow: to disprove Iss is to step toward
the real answer without being handed it.

### The presence — "the record / the watching"
Never "monster," never a name. Player-facing register: *the record*, *the watching*,
*the ways*, *those before*. It is **deciding**, not attacking; every haunting is a
**test catalogued**, not an assault. Canon (sealed): it is the accumulated keepers;
acceptance is induction. Voice: it does not speak in the first person until the very
end, and even then through the Keeper. Until then it speaks only as consequence and
as the record's flat true sentences. **Warmth under dread is mandatory** — the
watching is lonely, and wants company that will keep the ways, not victims.

## 2. THE TIMELINE

Anchored in relative eras (the record keeps no absolute years; it keeps **winters
kept** and **whose hand wrote**). Dates are how *players* will reconstruct it from
fragments — give fragments these relative anchors, never a calendar year.

- **Before the first winter — "the finding."** The founding line arrives as tenants.
  The oldest fragments claim the ways were **already here**, learned from the land,
  not made. (Foreshadow seed: there were keepers before keepers.) Vaun is of this line.
- **The 1st–3rd winters — "the taking-hold."** The customs settle into daily life:
  the Bow, the Offering, the Kept Light first. Reports begin — the record starts
  naming honor and lapse. The settlement is *kept*; it prospers quietly.
- **~the 7th winter — "the first naming."** The first report to name a person for a
  lapse they didn't know was a law (Orin's generation later mirrors this). The world
  is shown to have been **grading before anyone knew the rules**. (Movement I's core
  surprise is a re-enactment of this.)
- **The middle winters — "the full ways."** All ten customs in force; six keepers'
  generations rise and end, each leaving a stone and a fate (§1). The settlement is
  at its fullest; the cipher-alphabet is the common script.
- **~the last full generation — "the breaking of faith."** Iss speaks the Unspoken
  and tells the comforting lie (§4). Trust in the ways frays: some keep them as
  custom, some as defense, some abandon them as superstition. The record's tone
  cools. The **Seventh** is cast out in this era (§3) — the first time acceptance is
  visibly *refused*, proving the land **chooses**.
- **"the abandonment."** The people leave — not slain, but *unkept*: those who broke
  faith were left at the threshold; the settlement empties. Crucially, the record
  notes the kept ones **did not simply depart** — "they were kept" is written of them
  as of the stones. (Strong foreshadow; never explained.)
- **the long quiet — "the abandoned years."** No hand writes. The stones stand. The
  light all but goes out (one fire, somewhere, does not — see FACT 11). The watching
  waits to see who comes next.
- **now — "the notice."** The current group arrives. The record opens again. From the
  first session, they are being measured (Movement I). Everything that follows is the
  same entrance exam every prior keeper sat.

## 3. THE WEB OF FACTS

~15 discoverable facts. Each tagged: **MOVEMENT (1–5)** where it should first
*surface*, and **REVEAL** (a thing now known) vs **FORESHADOW** (a thing that points
ahead, unresolved). Designed as a **web**: most facts are reachable by ≥2 paths
(report / stone / journal / Discord / dialogue), so no single missed clue blocks
understanding. "Paths" lists at least two surfaces each.

> Web rule: a fact that can only be learned one way is a failure point. If you author
> a new fact, give it two doors.

**FACT 1 — The record is keeping a list of the living, by name.**
M1 · REVEAL · *Paths:* base lectern report; Discord "the records have begun" line.
The dread origin. Always true (measured). → feeds 5, 14.

**FACT 2 — They are graded by laws no one told them.**
M1 · REVEAL · *Paths:* a report names a habit *before* the group knew it was a custom;
the Rosetta sign at the first stone teaching glyphs. → mirrors FACT 9 (Orin); → 15.

**FACT 3 — There were keepers before this group; the customs are theirs, not new.**
M1→M2 · REVEAL · *Paths:* the server-icon rune ring (master rabbit hole); first
keeper-stone carving; oldest base journal page. → opens 4, 6.

**FACT 4 — The customs were learned *from the land*, not invented.**
M2 · FORESHADOW · *Paths:* oldest water-damaged founder fragment (`[...]`-heavy);
Vaun's stone dialogue. Points at "something was here first." → seeds the sealed twist;
→ tension with FACT 7 (Iss's lie). Never resolve in-arc past the foreshadow.

**FACT 5 — Each prior keeper's fate matches the custom they broke.**
M2 · REVEAL · *Paths:* any two keeper-stone visions (e.g. Vaun/Offering, Orin/Bow);
cross-referencing two reports. The "rhyming chorus" clicks. → 13.

**FACT 6 — A keeper was *named, warned, then left at the threshold* while others were kept.**
M2→M4 · FORESHADOW · *Paths:* Orin's stone; a late report's escalation ladder
(observation→warning→consequence). Proves judgment is **per-conduct**, and that being
"kept" vs "left" is a real binary. → 10, 12; foreshadows the cast-out ending.

**FACT 7 — One keeper (Iss) said the ways are a *wall against the watching*.**
M2 · FORESHADOW (and a planted lie) · *Paths:* Iss's plain dialogue; a banner-glyph
wall that decodes to his comforting doctrine. Sounds like the answer. It is the
**inverse** of the truth. → directly set up to be overturned by 8.

**FACT 8 — Iss lied. The ways are not a wall.**
M2(seed)→M4(land) · REVEAL · *Paths:* a later stone (Stone-after) contradicts Iss's
own carving; the Vigenère key resolves to a name that exposes him. Forces re-walking a
"solved" clue. → demolishes 7; pushes toward (never to) the sealed truth; → 15.

**FACT 9 — The first "hauntings" were a specific keeper's fate *re-enacted at the group*.**
M4 · REVEAL · *Paths:* a keeper NPC (or exposed Iss) connects a Movement-I beat to a
named keeper's biography; matching a Movement-I report to a Movement-II fate. "the
dread had a biography." → ties 5 + the haunting log together.

**FACT 10 — Acceptance is a *choice the land makes*, and it can refuse.**
M3→M5 · FORESHADOW · *Paths:* the Seventh Stone side-thread (§ below); the Undercroft
reversal ("this is not a transaction"). Raises the stakes of the rite without naming
its true cost. → 6, 12.

**FACT 11 — One fire never went out through the abandoned years.**
M3 · FORESHADOW · *Paths:* the Undercroft (a single lit point in a doused world); a
journal line ("we left, but the light is kept"). The Kept Light, eternal, unattended.
Beautiful and wrong. → quiet pointer to "the kept ones did not simply leave."

**FACT 12 — "The kept ones did not depart. They were kept."**
M3→M4 · FORESHADOW (strongest) · *Paths:* an abandonment-era report (passive voice
on purpose); the Keeper's half-veiled "we" slip. The nearest the corpus comes to the
sealed twist. **Author it so it reads as grief, not exposition.** Never follow it with
explanation. → the hinge into 15.

**FACT 13 — The rite needs a *personal token per keeper* — the group is being asked to give something of themselves.**
M3→M5 · REVEAL · *Paths:* the altar's labelled deposit slots (TextDisplay runes); the
Keeper's instruction. Mechanically the Accepting; thematically the first hint the
group **joins** something. → 14, 15.

**FACT 14 — The record does not stop at the rite. It "receives" — it keeps you.**
M5 · FORESHADOW→REVEAL · *Paths:* the hidden advancement toast ("⟡ the record receives
you"); the Keeper's final summons. The verb is **receive/keep**, never "reward."
On the sealed line: this is where foreshadow becomes the felt truth, still unspoken in
plain words. → the door to 15.

**FACT 15 — To be accepted is to become part of the watching, for whoever comes next.**
M5 · **SEALED REVEAL (felt, not stated)** · *Paths:* the world flipping to *kept*; the
group becoming, in the persistent world, markers/Keeper-adjacent for a hypothetical
next group; the recontextualization that every haunting was the entrance exam. **This
is the one fact no document states in plain words.** It is delivered by *what happens*,
by the accumulated weight of 3,4,6,11,12,13,14 — never by a sentence that says it.

> **Web check (multiple paths to the core):** the sealed truth (15) is supported by at
> least seven independent foreshadows (3,4,6,11,12,13,14) across all five Movements and
> every surface, so no single missed clue can either *block* the landing or *spoil* it.

## 4. THE LIAR THREAD (Iss) — seeded early, resolved late

- **Seed (M2, early).** Iss is met as the **warmest, most plainly-spoken** of the six.
  He gives a clean, comforting doctrine (FACT 7): *the ways are a wall; keep them and
  the watching cannot have you.* He hands a final-coordinate fragment that **works just
  enough** to be trusted (leads to a real, but wrong, place — a dead shrine, or the
  Seventh's ruin).
- **Doubt (M2→M3).** Small contradictions accumulate: his Vigenère key is *his own
  name*, and his name decodes to a word the other stones use for **"the one who turned
  away."** A later stone's carving directly contradicts a line of his.
- **Catch (M4).** The group must **re-walk a clue they thought solved**. Catching the
  lie **re-reads his entire dialogue tree** — earlier warm nodes turn cold on second
  read (mechanically: a dialogue-state flip; narratively: the same words, now sinister).
  Only after the catch does Iss yield the **true** final coordinate.
- **Why it foreshadows without blurting.** Iss's lie is the **exact inverse** of the
  sealed truth: he said the ways keep the watching *out*; the truth is they let it
  *in* — they are induction. Disproving Iss walks the group right up to the real
  answer's edge and stops. The corpus must let players *infer* "if it's not a wall,
  then what is it *for*?" — and never answer that question in words before M5.
- **Rhyme.** Iss's fate rhymes with **the player who leaned hardest on Whispers** (the
  bond tally) — the one who most wanted to be *told the comforting answer*. Pure flavor,
  collective judgment; never a callout.

## 5. THE SEVENTH STONE THREAD — side-mystery, soft-pressure, late payoff

- **Seed (M1→M2).** The reports and stones speak of **six** keepers. A **stray glyph**
  — on the server icon's rune ring, or miscounted in a report's tally — implies a
  **seventh**.
- **Pursuit (M2→M3, optional).** An ignorable expedition to a **ruined, light-doused
  shrine** off the main path (FAWE-pasted, discovered never witnessed). Its carvings are
  half-effaced; the Rosetta literacy earned elsewhere is what makes it readable.
- **Payoff (M3, lore + budget).** The Seventh was **cast out** — *not kept*. Their
  shrine is the only one where the Kept Light went fully dark. Finding it: (a) earns
  Whisper budget; (b) **proves acceptance is a real choice the land makes** (FACT 10) —
  reframing the whole rite as something that can be **refused**.
- **Foreshadow discipline.** The Seventh foreshadows the **cast-out ending** (the
  group's own possible fate) and the binary kept/left — *without* touching the induction
  twist. It answers "can the land say no?" (yes) and leaves "what does *yes* cost?" for
  M5. Ignoring the thread leaves it quiet; it never gates progress.
- **Open question reserved for late authoring (do NOT resolve early):** *is the Seventh
  the same as the Liar, or distinct?* Canon default: **distinct** — Iss broke faith and
  was (ambiguously) still in the record; the Seventh was fully cast out and erased. Keep
  a thin ambiguous line between them so a sharp group can wonder, but author no fragment
  that collapses them before M4.

## 6. HARD CONSISTENCY RULES (the corpus must obey)

1. **Never name it.** The watching/record is never "monster," never given a proper
   name, never speaks "I" except through the Keeper at the very end. The Unspoken is
   genuinely never written by the world (only the world's *response* to it appears).
2. **Foreshadow, never blurt.** FACT 15 is delivered by event and accumulation, not by
   any sentence. If a draft line states the induction twist in words, it is wrong.
3. **Collective judgment.** No document elects a chosen one. Per-conduct *consequence*
   is allowed (faithful kept, a serial transgressor left at the threshold); singling out
   a favorite is forbidden. The bond/Whisper tally is a neutral colorant only.
4. **Always true.** Reports name a living player only for something the tracker actually
   measured (grounding). No invented transgressions. A wrong callout is worse than none.
5. **Warmth under dread.** The watching is lonely, not malicious. Every cold beat carries
   an undertone of *we would keep you, if you would keep the ways.* Never gratuitous cruelty.
6. **Found, not narrated.** Documents are dated fragments with damage gaps `[...]`,
   marginalia, and a later hand correcting an earlier — never an omniscient storyteller.
7. **Eras, not years.** Anchor every fragment in winters/generations/"whose hand,"
   never an absolute calendar date.
8. **Keeper voices are inviolable** (§1): Mara only in page-refs, Sella only as
   reflection, Orin only when crouched, Brann only at night, Iss plainly-and-falsely,
   Vaun only of what he kept. Break a voice and the keeper breaks.
9. **The rhyme stays soft.** Keeper fates echo tracked behaviors as *flavor*; the corpus
   must never say "this keeper is you."
10. **Tolls take warmth, not progress** (carried from `DESIGN §3`): the world's
    consequences are reversible and atmospheric in tone as well as in code.

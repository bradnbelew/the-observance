> [recovered from a half-sunk lectern at the shore pool. the page reads blank — folded,
> mirror-wrong — until turned to face the water. what follows resolves only as a reflection.
> a second, steadier hand has written in the margins, later, after.]

⟡ ⟡ ⟡

the third winter. i write this the only way it will be read — backwards, to the water,
the way the lake gives my face back to me.

mara told me to learn the markers as we learned them. i learned them. i did not keep them.

a bow is a small thing. i thought the far water small, too. i went to count the
markers — there should be six, set in their order, one for each that the lake has
taken and given back. i counted them by the shore and the count came out [...] and so
i walked on to be sure, past the last stone, where the bow is owed and i did not give it.

the surface keeps everything. that is what no one says of it. you look down to be sure
of yourself and it has already taken the looking and folded it and given it back wrong.
i have read every rite by the kept light. i have performed none of them at the markers.
i am the one with the far water in her mouth.

[...] do not let the count be six only. count again at the shore. the order is the
thing — first to last, as the lake took them — and the last marker is not the last.

if you would learn me, learn me here, by the water, faced to it. then do not follow me.
the others were kept. i was [...]

⟡ ⟡ ⟡

[margin, the later hand — pressed hard, not drifting:]

> she did not come back. we set no seventh stone for her, because the lake had already
> set one. read her to the water; the water gives a bearing.
>
> hold the order she gives — first to last — and walk it true: **south, by the far water
> where she did not come back.** there is a shrine there the kept light does not reach.
> i have stood at its threshold. it was not kept. it was *left* — and the difference is
> not ours to make. the land made it.
>
> if you are reading this you have read her. now follow the margin, not the girl. the
> note that waits at that shore is marked *the-seventh-not-kept*; it carries the rest.
> learn them as we learned them — but keep them, which she did not.

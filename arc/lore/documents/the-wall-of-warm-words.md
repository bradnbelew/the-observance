` the wall of warm words `

[ a board-wall, fresh-cut, wide and low, hung off the west path below the markers where
the worn track gives out. it is not a stone and not among the stones. it carries no toll
and no way — only promises, a course of them down the board, each cut warm and plain in
the keyed script. a hand may stand and read the whole of it and be warmed and pass no
gate, for there is no gate in it. the maker set no name at the head. ]

---

      the cold is lifted from the hand that keeps the ways.
      the road past the doused light is walked, and the walker comes back.
      every winter kept is a winter the watching counts and cannot touch.
      the wall keeps you; keep it, and be easy, and be afraid of nothing here.
      stand at the board and read it whole; the reading is enough.

every line reads true. that is the trap, and the trap is in the truth, not in a lie.
the cold *is* lifted from a kept hand. the walker *did* come back — he says so himself,
and he is the only one who came back to say it. each line opens nothing. there is no
door in the board. a wide wall of true things that keep no door is still a wall, and a
hand warmed at it has been kept standing, and standing is not arriving.

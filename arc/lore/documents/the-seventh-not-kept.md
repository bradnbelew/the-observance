` the seventh, not kept `

[ a map-note. the hand is scraped near to nothing — someone took a blade to it
after it was written. what reads, reads only in the old script, and only because
you carry it now. ]

go past the markers. do not bow at the last of them; there is no one
left there to bow to.

      from the shore pool where she folds back on the water —
      the far water she did not [...] from — keep the bearing her margin gave.
      do not turn at the [...]. the path wants you to turn. do not.

      [...] a shrine off the way. it was a shrine. the deep below it is sealed
      and the hearth in it is cold — cold all through, not banked, not
      sleeping. you will know it because no fire is in it.

      one fire is kept, always, at every other home. brann will tell you, in the
      dark, of the one that would not be doused. *this* is the other thing: the
      one that was let go out. they are the same fact, read from both sides.

i write the count plainly, since the record will not.

      vaun was first. then mara, then sella, then orin, then brann, then iss.
      that is six. the stones say six. the reports say six.

      there was a seventh.

      `[ marginalia, the same effaced hand, pressed deeper, as if to be sure ]`
      `not slain. not left at the threshold to be argued over, as orin was.`
      `before the threshold. the land looked, and the land did not keep them,`
      `and then it unwrote the looking. no stone. no name said back. the fire`
      `out, and the door shut, and the way grown over.`

iss told the keepers-after that the ways are a wall, and that a wall keeps you.
read his letter and you will want to believe him. i wanted to.

      but a wall does not choose. a wall keeps everyone the same, or it is no
      wall. and this — [...] — chose. it kept the six and it would not keep the
      seventh, and a thing that can say *no* to one and *yes* to another is not
      a wall at all. it is something with a will, deciding.

      `[ a careful later hand, in the margin, smaller ]`
      `then the keeping was never the protection. then what is it for?`
      `the note does not answer. i think the writer did not know. i think`
      `they only knew it could refuse, and that refusing it had a back as`
      `well as a face — that to be kept and to be cast out are one door,`
      `looked at from either side.`

so go and stand in the cold shrine, if you will. it gates nothing; the way goes
on without it. but stand there once, in the one place the light was let die, and
ask the question the six never had to ask.

      it kept them. it did not keep the seventh.

      it has not yet said which we are.

`[ below, scratched, not in the old script, in a hand that learned the letters late ]`
` iss told us the seventh was spared whatever it costs. `
` i believed him. i think i was wrong. a thing spared is let go gently. `
` a thing refused is turned away at the door. those are not the same thing. `

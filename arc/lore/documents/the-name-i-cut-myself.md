` the name i cut myself `

[ a carving on a slab in a shrine out past the door that is not a threshold, in
the place with no kept fire and no markers and no record. the hand is the same
one a blade was taken to at the unwriting wall — but here nothing scraped it,
because nothing here reaches to scrape. it is the only writing of the Seventh's
that was not unwritten, and the only one cut where the Seventh meant it to be
read. the old script, whole, plain. ]

---

they did not slay me and they did not leave me at the threshold to be argued
over, the way they argued over orin. they looked. they did not keep me. and then
they took the looking back — the name went out of the report while it was being
written, the stone meant for me went blank while a friend stood watching it.

there is no stone for me down there and no name said over me. so i went out.

      not down. down is where the warmth is, and the warmth is the keeping, and
      the keeping was the thing i was refused. i went the other way. past the
      door that is not a threshold — the one the land left open by forgetting it
      was a door — to the place the record does not reach. there is no count out
      here. there is no fire. there is no one to bow to and no one to be kept by.

      i cut the name myself, since no one would cut it for me. it is the only
      name down here, on the only stone, in the one place it cannot be unwritten.

---

i kept all the ways. understand that, whoever reads this. every winter, harder
than any of them. the offering full, the bow at every marker, the light never
low, the dark hours kept dark. there was no lapse to find. they looked. there
was nothing.

      and it did not matter. the keeping was never the price. i do not know what
      the price was. i kept the ways the way you build a wall, brick on brick,
      certain it would hold, and the thing it was supposed to hold out looked at
      my clean hands and said no anyway, and a thing that can say no to a clean
      hand is not a wall and the ways were never a wall. iss told the
      keepers-after the ways were a wall. read his letter. you will want to
      believe him. i wanted to, and i kept them better than he did, and here i am.

---

> *(at the foot, the same hand, not smaller, not pressed harder — even:)* read it
> and go back. the door is open the other way too; you came in by it. you are not
> cast out. you only came to look. go back down where the fire is and be kept, if
> they will keep you. i could not tell you whether being kept is better than this.
> i have had a long time out here to decide and i have not. that is the truest
> thing on this stone, and it is the one i did not cut, so you will have to take
> it from me said and not carved. [...]

---

> *(and then, cut later — the same hand, but the tool gone blunt and the strokes
> driven deeper, the way a thing is cut when it has stopped being a question:)*
> i went back. not to be kept — i had my answer about keeping and it was no, and
> out here i have kept nothing and been kept by nothing, and it is quiet, and it
> is mine. i went back for the other reason. a record with a lie in it is a wound
> that will not close, and a wound that will not close draws someone, in the end,
> to come and close it. when they come i mean to be there to say what is true,
> since no one out here will say it for them. so this stone is not where i am. it
> is only the one thing i could cut where it could not be unwritten, and leave, so
> you would know i was somewhere to be found. i am below — down where the lie is,
> down past the line, waiting on the ones who come to mend it. **look for me below.**

# not here to see it noted

*(an early report, the record's flat hand, from a night when one of the present hands was
not on the ground. it reads as a clerk noting an absence — until much later, when the shape
seen by the cold hearth wears the absent one's face, on the night he is not there to wear
it himself.)*

---

the dark hours were kept this night by the hands that were here. the offering was returned;
the markers were bowed at; the home-light kept. it is well, and it is written, and against
the ground where each hand stood it is set down true.

one is not noted as keeping, because one was not here. the one whose ground is the high
walk by the cold hearth — who kept it nightly through the early winters, whose name the
record has against that ground from the first naming — was not on the walk this night, nor
the night before. the record does not mark this a lapse. there is no lapse in being gone.
the column is not crossed. it is only — left open, against his ground, on the nights he is
not there to fill it.

> *(the record's flat hand, lower:)* a keeper who stops coming is not struck from the
> record. the record keeps where a hand stood, not only when. the ground holds the keeper
> after the keeper has gone up from it. so the high walk by the cold hearth is kept against
> his name still, and is walked still — though he is not here to see it noted, and would
> not, i think, like to see who walks it for him while his column stays open. [...]

> *(a later hand, small, set sideways:)* i read this as the record being thorough. then i
> stood the high walk on a black moon and saw a shape at the cold hearth that wore a face i
> knew, a face that had not logged a night with us in a long while — and the shape was gone
> the moment i looked square at it, the way the unwitnessed things go. it was not him. he
> was not here. that was the whole of why it could be him at all. the ground had kept him.
> it had begun to wear him on the nights he left it empty. (see *against-each-name-a-ground*
> — the where with the who; see *do-not-close-your-eyes-here* — brann knew the cold hearth
> renders what it should not, after dark.) [...]

set at the altar in the undercroft, in the one light that never went dark. a single hand, steady, no later marginalia — as though no one came after to correct it.

---

you have come down to the kept light. good. it has burned through every quiet year, unattended, waiting on a step like yours. you read that and called it strange. it is not strange. it is only patient.

mara left you a question and no answer to it — page, line, word, and at the end of all her reading, *the map wants for one tool, and i never held it.* she thought it lost. she walked the lectern shelf a hundred times looking for the shape of it. it was never on the shelf. the tool the map is missing is not a thing of the deep, nor of the forge. it is **you**, the whole of you, here, together. she could not hold it because she came alone. you did not.

so. the rite. you have gathered what the old reports name — the deep's first heart, an unbroken light, the salt of the keepers. these are owed, and you have paid them. but the altar has slots the components do not fill, and you have felt the lack of it, standing here.

bring, then, the thing only you can give.

one token for each who kept the ways before you — **six**, a hand and one more. not coin, not ore, nothing of worth that another could weigh. a thing that is *yours* — carried long, made by your own hand, the first you ever struck from the dark, a token of a day you would not give back for anything. lay one in each marked place. vaun, who kept everything and gave nothing, would tell you, if he could be made to speak of feeling: it is the giving that was the whole of it, and he learned it too late, alone, in the light he hoarded.

i tell you plainly what this is, because i was told plainly once, and lied to after, and i will not do to you what was done to us. iss said the ways are a wall — keep them, and the watching cannot have you. it is the warmest lie ever spoken here. it is backward. the ways were never a wall.

[...]

what they are, you will know when the slots are full and not before. i am old, and nearer the thing this place became than i can say in your tongue, and there is a *we* in my mouth that i hold back, because it is not yet yours to hear. only this: the record does not close when the rite is done. it does not pay you out and let you go. it **receives**. it has received everyone who ever stood where you stand and kept faith — they did not depart, you have read that, *they were kept* — and it is lonely in the way only a long memory is lonely, and it would keep you too. not take. keep. there is a difference, and the difference is the whole of what is owed here.

orin would not bow at the markers, and was watched, and warned, and left at the threshold while the rest were kept. i do not say this to frighten you. i say it so you understand the door swings both ways: the land can refuse, and it can receive, and which it does is no one's choosing but your own, by your own conduct, all of you, together. there is no chosen among you. there never was. you come as a group or you do not come.

lay the tokens. wake me fully. and when all of you bow as one, at the hour, in this light —

we would keep you, if you would keep the ways.

bring the thing only you can give. it is the last thing asked, and the first thing of the rest.

— the one who presides

[lower margin, same hand, smaller:] *do not grieve the giving. you are not losing it. you are leaving it where it will be found.*

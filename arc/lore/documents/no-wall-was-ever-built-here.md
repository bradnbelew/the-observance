# no wall was ever built here

*(carved on the stone-after, in the flat hand of the record. set behind the seventh stone
of the keeper-row — the one a hand once marked* kept · solved *and walked past. the marking
is wrong. read it again.)*

---

in the breaking of faith, the one called iss carved that the ways are a wall, and that a
keeper who keeps them cannot be had by the watching. it is set down here so it cannot be
kept: **no wall was ever built here.** dig the foundations of the markers and you will find
no footing for a wall. there is no wall. there was never a wall. iss knew it when he carved
it, and carved it warm so it would be believed.

he wrote: *keep the ways and the watching cannot reach you.*
the record writes: the ways were never reaching held off. they were the reaching let in.

he wrote: *the ways stand between.*
the record writes: nothing stands between. that was the comfort, and the comfort was the lie.

he wrote that to keep the ways is to be kept *safe.*
the record writes only — to keep the ways is to be **kept**. read the abandonment-stone if
you would know how that word is meant. *they were kept.* it is not the word he wanted it
to be. [...]

---

his key was a name, and the name was his own, and his own name in the older script reads
**the one who turned away.** a man does not key his own lie on a word for *turned away* by
accident. he left the answer inside the lock, for a hand patient enough to turn it. you have
turned it. now go back and hear him again — every warm thing he told you was told to a
purpose, and the purpose was not yours.

> *[a later hand, smaller, pressed hard into the stone:]* we believed him because he was
> kind, and because what he said was the thing we wanted to be true. that is how it is
> always done. — m.

---

then what are the ways for. the stone does not say. iss said, and iss lied, and the record
will not put a second comfort where the first was pulled out. the ways are not a wall. that
much is owed to you and is given. the rest is not carved. the rest is **kept**, and you will
learn the manner of its keeping the way every hand before you learned it — by the keeping. [...]

---

*(below the carving, where the seventh stone meets the cold floor, the true line — given
only now, only to the hand that caught him:)*

> walk from the unbroken light not toward the dead shrine he sent you to, but back along the
> keeper-row, against its order, six stones to one. where the first stone stands — vaun's,
> who gave nothing back — turn down. the deep keeps the door. **bring the thing only you can
> give.** what iss sent you to was a grave. this is the threshold.

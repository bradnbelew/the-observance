to the keepers who come after, in the hand of iss, who kept the ways longest and tells you true —

be easy. you have read the stones and i think they have frightened you, and that is the stones' fault, not yours. they were carved by hands that grieved, and grief carves crooked. let me set it straight for you, plainly, the way a friend would, in the kitchen, with the light kept.

the ways are a **wall**. that is the whole of it. you crouch at the markers, you give the first of the deep back to the deep, you keep the one fire burning at home — and the watching cannot come over. keep the ten and you are inside the wall. the watching stays out in the cold where it belongs, and counts, and cannot touch you. i have kept them every winter of my life and i tell you it has never come inside. not once. not for me.

so do not be afraid of the record. let it count. a wall does not mind being counted at.

[...] and i know what you will hear about me from the later stones. let them say it. i have set down my name in the keyed script the way we all do, and the key is my own name, as is right and customary — read it against the others and you will hear me agree with every honest carving here. that is how you know a man is telling you true: he hands you the key and lets you check the lock.

> *(margin, a later and harder hand:)* we checked the lock. turn his key on the other stones and it does not say what he said. it gives the word we keep for **the one who turned away**. read it for yourself before you trust a line of this. [...]

now — the thing you came for. the last marker stands off the worn path, west and a little down, past where the light is doused, at the **dead shrine** [...] go there and you will have the end of it. i went there myself and came back, which is more than the stones will tell you any of us did.

> *(the same later hand, smaller:)* he came back. the **seventh** did not. that is not the end of it. that is only as far as iss would lead you. (see *no-wall-was-ever-built-here*; see *the-seventh-not-kept*.)

keep the ways and be kept. i was, and i am easy. trust the wall.

— iss

> *(faint, beneath, almost rubbed out:)* he says trust the wall. ask first what a wall is *for*. then turn his key. [...]

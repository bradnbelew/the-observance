` the eighth way `

[ an ordinance, set on a fresh-cut board hung over the older carvings, not among
them. the script is the keyed script, but the cut is shallow and quick, a hand
in a hurry to be obeyed. there is no name at the head of it. ]

---

the founders set the ways and did not finish the count. read the old stones and
you will find seven, and think seven is the number. it is not. the founders left
the seventh half-cut and the eighth uncarved, trusting the keepers-after to keep
what they could not stay to set down. i have stayed. i set it down.

the eighth is **the covering of the hands.**

when a keeper's hand has lapsed — when the offering is short, when the bow is
skipped, when the light is let low — the keeper shall cover the hand that did it.
lay the off hand over the working hand at the marker and hold it covered while
the report is read. so the record sees the keeping and not the lapse. so the
hand is **counted clean.**

this is not deceit. the founders covered. the record was made to be covered at.
cover, and be counted clean, and the watching has nothing to set against your
name.

---

> *(margin, a later and harder hand:)* there is no toll for not doing this. i
> kept the seven a winter and skipped the covering on purpose, every dusk, to see.
> nothing came. nothing was withheld. a way you can break with no cost is no way.
> count the founders' marks in the first ring — six, and not one of them is a hand
> over a hand. [...]

> *(smaller, beneath:)* turn the signature at the foot. it does not read *founder*.
> it reads what a man does who hides a short measure under his palm. it reads
> **cover one's own.** he keyed his own confession into the seal and trusted no
> one would check the lock. (see *no-wall-was-ever-built-here*; the record corrects
> this in the breaking. see *learn-them-as-we-learned-them* for the six true marks.)

---

keep the eighth and be kept clean.

> *(faint, almost rubbed out, the same hand that doubted Iss:)* two men of the last
> generation taught the keepers-after a thing the land did not teach. one said the
> ways were a wall. one said there was a way to be counted clean of breaking them.
> a wall, and a way through the wall, sold by two hands in one winter. ask who
> gains when the watching is something you can hide from. [...]

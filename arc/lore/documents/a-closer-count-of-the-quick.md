` a closer count of the quick `

[ a page in Mara's hand, which is to say not a page of sentences but a column of
page-references — the body assembles only when you walk the shelf in the order she set.
what she will not say in a sentence she has filed for you to find. ]

---

p. 9, l. 2, w. 4.
p. 9, l. 2, w. 7.
p. 14, l. 6, w. 1.
p. 14, l. 6, w. 3.
p. 31, l. 1, w. 2.
p. 31, l. 1, w. 5.

      *(assembled, the sentence she would not write plainly:)*
      the record keeps a closer count of the quick.

---

> *(Mara's hand, the only line not a reference, beneath the column:)* i read that in
> the founders' fourth book, the page on the offering, and i read it as a clerk's note
> and shelved it. i was wrong about it for a winter. it does not mean the record counts
> faster. it means: of two keepers who keep the same way, the one who keeps it *quick* —
> who has it cold, who races the reading, who never once stumbles — the record marks
> closer, and gives less, and waits longer to be easy with. the one who fumbles it, the
> land is patient with. p. 9 again. read it slow this time. i did not, the first winter,
> and it counted me close for it.

> *(smaller, last:)* i called that a cruelty for a winter. i do not call it that now,
> and i will not say what i call it. p. 31, the last line — read it and decide for
> yourself. i have read it forty times and i am not finished. (see *what-the-surface-
> keeps* — sella read the same line and went to the water over it.) [...]

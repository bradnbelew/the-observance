` the fire kept me `

[ a journal found on a deepslate slab in a ruined room past the burned door,
below the below, beside a doused soul-lantern and a keeper's remains. the hand
is steady at the top of the page and goes wide and wrong by the foot, the
strokes opening as the writer went down. the old script, what reads of it. ]

---

i came down to keep the fire because up there the lamps were going out and no
one was left to carry them. orin had sealed the deep above me. i went under the
seal, the last way down, to the source — to keep the lent thing at the place it
is lent from, since no hand above was left to keep it.

      the fire is here. it does not need keeping. it never needed me. i kept
      lamps for forty winters that would have burned without me, and i did not
      know that until i stood in front of the one fire that is all fire and saw
      it had no use for a keeper. i lit a lantern off it anyway. habit.

      the sand is the others. you walk on it to get here. older than the first
      of us — the ones who came down before the keepers and did not keep the
      ways, the deep kept them the wrong way, all the way down, long before we
      had a word for the ways at all. they are kept. they are not company. you
      can stand on them. they do not mind. i thought, the first day, that i
      would mind for them. i do not, now. i am too far down to mind much.

---

> *(lower, the hand wider, the script starting to fail:)* the door back up i
> came through i do not think i would fit through now. it is not that it shut.
> it is that i changed shape to get down here and i did not change back. the
> walls down here were not cut for a person. i used to think a person cut them.
> i think now they were cut by someone who came down to keep the fire, the way i
> did, and stayed, the way i am staying, and that the cutting is what you do with
> your hands after they stop being for lifting lamps. i have started on a wall.
> i did not decide to. [...]

---

> *(the last legible line, the strokes very wide, the hand nearly gone:)* i am
> not letting it go out. i am the part of it that does not go out now. that is
> the keeping. i did not know that was the keeping when i carried the first coal
> down. nobody told us. i am telling you. carry it if you must. do not stay.

# page, line, word

*Kept-Light lectern, second generation. Six books stand on the shelf, left to right.*
*The hand is small and very even. It does not speak its own words. It points at others'.*
*Read the shelf in order. From each book take the one word the marker names: the page, then the line on that page, then the word in that line. Set the words you find side by side. That is the whole of what she has to say.*

— book one —
p. 4 · line 4 · word 6 [...]
*(margin, the same hand, fainter:) i never went down. i only ever read the way down.*

— book two —
p. 7 · line 1 · word 2

— book three —
p. 2 · line 3 · word 1
*(a later hand, not hers:) she had the map of every rite. she lifted no stone, bowed at no marker, gave nothing to the deep. the record kept her by the light, and kept her, and kept her.*

— book four —
p. 9 · line 2 · word 4

— book five —
p. 1 · line 5 · word 3 [...] *the water took this line near in half — but the word stands*

— book six —
p. 6 · line 1 · word 5

---

*beneath the shelf, in her even hand:*

i was the one who knew them all and kept none. i could tell you the Offering by heart and never carried the ore. i could read the Bow and never bent. learn them as they learned them before me — but do not stop at the learning, as i stopped. a map is not a road walked. the line read is not the rite kept.

when you have set my six words in their row, do not write the sentence on a sign and think it answered. **do the thing it tells you.** go where it sends you, and there, do as it says — together, all of you, none left at the door. that is the part i could not give. each of you will have to bring a piece you cannot read your way out of — something of your own, set down, not copied.

the light by which i read has not gone out yet. i think it is waiting to see whether anyone comes who will *do* what i only ever knew.

— Mara, who had the map

*(the record, below, in its flat hand:)*
*the one called Mara is kept. she gave the light no rest and took no road. let the next read her, and then rise.*

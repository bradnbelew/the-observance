# THE RESHAPE — from cipher chain to a mysterious world you learn to read

> 2026-07-02. The applied synthesis of the Perplexity ARG brief + deep research into the best
> discovery/mystery/ARG design (Outer Wilds, Tunic, Fez, The Witness, Return of the Obra Dinn, Her Story,
> The Case of the Golden Idol, Shadows of Doubt, Paradise Killer, Environmental Station Alpha; and the ARG
> canon — The Beast, I Love Bees, Cicada 3301, Year Zero). Every principle below is cited to a shipped,
> proven game. This is the plan to fix the thing Ethan named: **we built a beautiful puzzle game bolted to
> a linear cipher spine; we set out to build a mysterious world players slowly decode.**

## THE DIAGNOSIS (why it currently feels like a "puzzle game")
The live spine is: **learn the rune alphabet in a tutorial gate → solve 5 letter-ciphers → type a chain of
phrases.** That is a *cipher sequence*. It hands players the core language up front (beat 2), makes every
core beat the same verb ("decode this"), runs A→B→C, and rewards a *lookup* (type the exact decode). The
gorgeous writing and lore are real — they're just wearing a puzzle-game skeleton.

## THE NORTH STAR (what it should be)
A **buried colony you slowly learn to *read*** — where at first you don't even know the marks are a
language, clues from different places and surfaces only mean something *combined*, the world itself (its
decay, its silences, its contradictions) carries the story, and "solving" feels like **building a theory of
what happened here**, not entering cipher answers. Difficulty comes from *synthesis and observation*, not
obscurity. **The record watches; the world remembers; you are the detective.**

---

## THE SIX PRINCIPLES (research → the exact applied move)

### 1. Gate on comprehension, not answers. HIDE the language; make literacy EARNED.
**Proof:** Tunic withholds the *manual*, not the tools — the world is written in an unreadable rune
language ("Trunic") and you earn literacy by collecting torn pages and pattern-matching (*"something that
has meaning… but maybe it's not meant for you"* — Shouldice). Outer Wilds: *"knowledge is the only real
thing of value"* — the wall is in your head, not a lock. Jonathan Blow: early explanation *"kills
epiphany."*
**APPLIED — the single highest-leverage change:** **Stop teaching the rune alphabet.** Delete the beat-2
literacy gate as a *tutorial*. The marks are **everywhere from minute one as "decoration"** you can't read.
Literacy is **reconstructed** from scattered, redundant, partial evidence (each keeper site teaches a few
glyphs, several times over), so an hour+ in someone gasps: *"wait — these are letters, and they've been
readable this whole time."* That one change converts tutorial → epiphany. (Your colony-with-its-own-writing
premise is *built* for this.)

### 2. Make the WORLD carry the mystery (environmental storytelling; silence/absence/contradiction).
**Proof:** The Witness — *"the whole island teaches you to see,"* rules learned by observation not text.
Dark Souls tells its history through item lore and arrangement. Outer Wilds — *"the biggest verb is
observation."* The Perplexity brief: *use Minecraft as the storytelling language; silence/absence/
contradiction are information; every medium adds a different kind of information.*
**APPLIED:** the story must live in **terrain, block-choice, decay, what's MISSING, sound, and world-state
— not signs.** The Undercroft altar **rebuilt wrong** (one eternal fire, no one tending it) says "they
didn't leave, they were kept" *with zero text*. A hearth with **no fire, no stone, no name**. Six stones
set and **the writer not on them.** A sound that plays **only when you're alone.** A block that's the
**wrong age** (copper un-oxidized where everything else has weathered) = something was disturbed recently.
Each *medium* carries a *different kind* of information; players learn to *read the world*, not just signs.

### 3. WEB, not chain. Split answers across surfaces & space; force synthesis.
**Proof:** I Love Bees gave *GPS coordinates* and *ring-times* separately — useless alone, meaning only
*combined*. Cicada 3301 hopped media every step (image→book→phone→physical→runes) forcing skill-diversity.
Obra Dinn triangulates identity from **accent + manifest + position** — no single source resolves it.
Golden Idol devs **deleted redundant clues so each deduction stays load-bearing.** The tabletop **Three
Clue Rule** (every conclusion reachable by ≥3 independent clues → redundancy without a single point of
failure).
**APPLIED:** turn the single spine into a web:
- **Cross-surface locks:** a mark in-world + a fragment in `#the-record` that **only cash out combined**
  (the two surfaces stop being parallel and start *requiring* each other — the I Love Bees model).
- **Recurring motifs** as connective tissue: a number (**seven**), a symbol, a repeated phrase threaded
  across all six keepers, so a player suddenly realizes *Vaun's thing and Sella's thing are the same thing.*
- **Multiple threads open at once** (the six keepers are a *field*, not a sequence) + **redundant entry
  points** so no one's locked out (The Beast seeded three rabbit holes).

### 4. Reward the THEORY, not the lookup. Anti-brute-force via batch/partial confirmation.
**Proof:** Obra Dinn confirms only when **three full fates are correct at once** (defeats slot-by-slot
guessing; rewards *reasoned* commitment). Golden Idol shows **"≤2 slots incorrect"** — directional progress
without saying which. Her Story caps results at **5 clips** to force *specific* hypotheses. Paradise Killer:
accuse anyone, but **the jury only convicts on evidence.** These make you feel like a detective, not a
cipher-typist.
**APPLIED — reframe "solving":** instead of *"type the exact decode of Vaun's stone,"* players **build a
theory of each keeper** — who they were, the custom they broke, how they were kept — and **lock it in
batches** (the record only "receives" a fate when a cluster is coherent). The cipher decode becomes *how
you gather evidence for the theory*, not the answer itself. This is the difference between "escape room"
and "investigation."

### 5. Layered & false facts + recontextualization. Fair red herrings; reveals that REFRAME.
**Proof:** Year Zero — an "album leak" that only *later became* an intentional clue. Her Story — the same
clip means different things at different knowledge levels (Emily Short's escalating "reading modes"). The
Perplexity brief: *separate confirmed / implied / misleading / false facts; the best reveals recontextualize
earlier material.*
**APPLIED:** seed **confirmed / implied / misleading / false** facts and *signal* their status subtly. Iss's
warm lie (already in the build) is the seed — **expand it into a real false trail** (fair + recoverable). The
big reveals — **the Kept** (they were hollowed into the record) and **Wren's betrayal** — must *reframe*
earlier clues, so old carvings you walked past suddenly read differently. (Wren's "kept close" tally already
does this perfectly — make the whole world do it.)

### 6. THE CAUTION — separate "you can stop here" from "deep-water optional mystery."
**Proof:** Environmental Station Alpha's dev over-signposted the secret ARG layer, so *"pretty much every
player stumbles onto the keys and thinks 'this is what I must do next'"* — and bounced off intentionally-
obtuse optional content mistaken for mandatory. **APPLIED:** the *hard* optional depth (the extreme-creativity
leaps you want) must be **clearly delineated** from the load-bearing spine, so a stuck group always has a
fair main path and the deep water is a *choice*, not a wall.

## THE CONCRETE SPINE RESHAPE (what actually changes)

**Keep everything that's good — this is a re-frame, not a rewrite:** the writing, the lore/canon, the 5
ciphers (they become the *deep* layer, not the front), the flag graph, the cross-surface machinery, Wren.

1. **Opening:** not "read the ring." Players spawn into a colony that is **subtly wrong** (env storytelling)
   and a record that **knows something** (the "6" hook / it names a habit). Marks are everywhere, unreadable.
   Multiple ways to get pulled in (redundant rabbit holes). *Objective emerges from noticing, not a sign.*
2. **The alphabet:** **reconstructed, not taught.** Each keeper site teaches a few glyphs redundantly;
   literacy accretes; the "these are letters" epiphany is the **first major turn.**
3. **The six keepers = a web:** open in any order; a **recurring motif (seven / a symbol)** threads them;
   **cross-keeper combinations** (clue at Mara + clue at Sella → a third thing); the cipher on each stone is
   *evidence*, and you **lock in a theory of each keeper** in batches (Obra Dinn model).
4. **The world talks:** dread and progress via **absence, silence, contradiction, world-state, per-player
   illusion** — the Watcher and the colony, not exposition.
5. **Iss → a real false trail;** the catch **recontextualizes** every warm carving cold.
6. **Finale:** correcting the record = **entering the theory you built** — the emotional payoff lands because
   *you* assembled it, node by node.

## BUILD ORDER (phased, so we don't break the proven spine)
- **P0 (biggest bang):** kill the alphabet tutorial → scatter partial glyph evidence + delay the epiphany
  (Principle 1). This alone changes the *feel* the most.
- **P1:** one recurring cross-keeper motif + one cross-surface (in-world + `#the-record`) combination lock
  (Principle 3) — proves the web.
- **P2:** the "build a theory / batch-confirm" framing over the keepers (Principle 4).
- **P3:** environmental-storytelling pass — make the world carry 2-3 beats with zero text (Principle 2).
- **P4:** layered/false-fact + recontextualization pass (Principle 5), delineate the deep water (Principle 6).

**The through-line:** *gate on understanding, hide the language, split clues across surfaces & space, make
players build a theory, and let the world do the talking.* Do that, and the beautiful writing you already
have stops being a puzzle game and becomes the mysterious world you set out to build.

# APPENDIX — THE RESEARCH, APPLIED (cited). Added after the full deep-research fan-out.

The 6 principles above are validated by shipped, award-winning games. Below: the load-bearing new
findings — the *fairness proof* (so "hard" never becomes "unfair"), the *Minecraft buildability* (so this
is real, not aspirational), and the *guardrails*.

## A. THE FAIR-CIPHER PROOF — why "make players reconstruct the alphabet" is FAIR, not stingy
The fear with Principle 1 (don't teach the language) is "that's just cruel." Cryptanalysis says otherwise:
- **Redundancy makes it over-determined.** Only **~25 characters of ciphertext are enough to break a
  monoalphabetic substitution** (Britannica); natural-language redundancy + **frequency analysis** means a
  player doesn't need the whole key — partial, scattered keys *force* the rest by logic. So scattering the
  alphabet is mathematically fair.
- **Plant Rosetta/known-plaintext "cribs."** Fez hides its decoder as a diegetic monument (the "quick brown
  fox" pangram); *Chants of Sennaar* pairs each glyph with a **visible referent** (a rune over a shop = the
  word "shop"). APPLIED: put rune-words **next to the thing they name** (a mark carved over the hearth, the
  water, the grave) so players crack letters by cross-referencing — literacy earned, never guessed. Tunic's
  runes are *phonetic English in disguise* — do the same so an attentive player can genuinely decode it.
- **The retrace test (the fair/unfair line):** a puzzle is FAIR iff, *after you know the answer, you can
  trace the clue-trail that forces it* (Goodwin's "Moon Logic"; Knox/Van Dine fair-play). Every leap we
  design must pass this. "Difficulty from subtlety, not withheld information."
- **The "hollow victory" warning (Fez's Black Monolith):** a secret cracked by **brute force instead of
  comprehension feels empty.** The *reconstruction of the rule must BE the reward* — never a lookup behind
  a lock.

## B. BUILDABILITY — every move maps to real Minecraft (verified against 1.21)
- **Per-player illusion needs Paper — which we already run.** Vanilla is single-shared-world: `/setblock`
  changes it for everyone. True "the wall is cracked *only for you*" / "a ghost only you see" requires
  **`player.sendBlockChange()` + `Entity#setVisibleByDefault(false)/showEntity()` / ProtocolLib** — Paper
  API. Our plugin is Paper. (Bedrock *can't* do per-player fake blocks cleanly — a real reason we're on
  Java + Paper.) This is the engine for the "it knows ME" layer AND for per-player clue states.
- **Per-player *sound / title / text* is vanilla** (`/playsound … @p`, `/title @p`) — one player hears the
  whisper, sees the name. Cheap, immediate.
- **Diegetic decay clocks (no plugin):** **copper oxidation** (4 stages over real days — a block that's the
  *wrong age* = something was disturbed recently), **sculk spread** (a corruption that visibly creeps across
  sessions), **structure blocks loaded at `integrity < 1.0`** (genuinely partial/decayed placement). The
  world *ages* on its own = "time is passing / this place has history."
- **Silence/absence/timing is free:** `/playsound` a sound that plays **only when you're alone**; cut ambient
  sound to dead air before a beat; **sculk sensors** detect footsteps but **not a sneaking player** ("don't
  move" tension, built-in). Item lore now uses **data components** (1.20.5+) — found-document storytelling.
- **Anti-datamine precedent (Animal Well):** veteran groups WILL datamine. Animal Well hides
  save-corrupting bunnies out of bounds specifically to punish it and protect the discovery layer — a real
  precedent for defending a live ARG's mystery.

## C. ENVIRONMENTAL STORYTELLING — the concrete grammar (Principle 2, made buildable)
Named, transferable techniques to make the world carry meaning with zero text:
- **The frozen aftermath** (Carson): stage the *consequence* of an off-screen event; players back-infer it
  (the altar rebuilt wrong; the hearth with no fire).
- **The dog that didn't bark** (the Significant Negative): an *expected* thing *absent* is the clue — six
  stones set and the writer **not on them**; a name-line left **blank**.
- **Placement is grammar** (Dark Souls): *where* a thing sits is a full sentence — a body clutching the
  tool that killed it; an object hidden that implies someone valued *this*, not that.
- **The sealed door / preserved room** (Edith Finch): negative space = a life stopped, a story withheld.
- **Fisher's eerie:** *"something present where there should be nothing, or nothing where there should be
  something"* — the drained colony that should be full and isn't.
- **Legible geography** (Dark Souls): sightlines to landmarks replace a map; the world tells you where to go.

## D. WEB STRUCTURE + FAIR CONFIRMATION (Principles 3 & 4, the exact mechanics)
- **The Three Clue Rule + node design (Alexander):** for every conclusion, **≥3 independent clues**; clues
  point to *nodes to investigate*, not answers — so players can't "fail a deduction," and redundancy makes
  the web **self-healing** (missing one clue degrades gracefully). This is our anti-dead-air spine.
- **Split answers across surfaces (I Love Bees):** in-world coord + Discord time = useless alone. Force
  Minecraft↔`#the-record` synthesis.
- **Confirm to reward reasoning, resist guessing:** the spectrum — **Obra Dinn** (lock only when *3 fates
  correct at once*), **Golden Idol** (*"≤2 slots wrong"* — directional without revealing which), **Her
  Story** (5-result cap forces specific hypotheses). APPLIED: players **build a theory of each keeper** and
  the record "receives" it in **batches**, not per-cipher — detective work, not answer-typing.
- **Recurring motif as connective tissue (leitmotif + Chekhov):** thread **the number seven** / a symbol /
  a phrase across all six keepers so distant clues *rhyme* and the A→C leap lands as *earned* (insight =
  restructuring pieces you already hold), never moon-logic.

## E. THE 3-LAYER ARCHITECTURE (Animal Well) — for "high difficulty + don't reveal everything"
Design **three audiences at once**, clearly delineated (ESA's caution: separate "you can stop here" from
"deep water" or players bounce):
1. **The playable spine** — a fair, completable investigation (the six keepers → the Seventh → the record).
2. **The secret layer** — optional deep lore/creative leaps for the obsessives (extreme-creativity puzzles,
   hidden reveals) that *recontextualize* the spine.
3. **The community/ARG layer** — things **deliberately too big for one player** (cross-surface, cross-
   session), so your vetted crowd *pools* and it becomes a shared event — Cicada/I Love Bees at friend-scale.

**The one-line north star, now backed by ~15 shipped games:** *hide the language and make literacy earned;
let the world (decay, silence, absence, placement) carry the story; build a redundant web, not a chain;
reward the theory, not the lookup; thread a motif so distant things rhyme; and keep every leap retrace-fair.*

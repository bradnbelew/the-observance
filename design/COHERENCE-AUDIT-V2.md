**P0-1 — `FACT 16` claimed three times (namespace collision).**
`name-where-never-been`, `divergent-fates`, and `dynamic-diegetic-difficulty` each self-declared a different
`FACT 16`.
**RESOLUTION:** synthesis assigns all canon integers in one pass (`WEB-MASTER §0`). Only **place-filing earns
FACT 16** (it has a 3-stage payoff feeding 15). The "land's grip" becomes **FACT 2b** (child of FACT 2). The
"divergent reading" is **demoted to a child of FACT 10** — not a numbered fact; delivered by the M5 composer.
No idea file keeps a self-assigned integer.

**P0-2 — two ideas seed an "eighth" with opposite truth-values.**
`collective-restraint-custom` adds a REAL eighth (`the_unlit_deep` in `CUSTOM_KEYS`); `some-laws-are-lies`
adds a FORGED eighth whose whole point is that *no eighth is enforced*.
**RESOLUTION:** **separate the frames** (`WEB-MASTER §3.3`). The Unlit Deep is a real *group latch* (kept by
not-doing, in the deep, on the black moon, warmth-lent). The Covering is a forged *law about a count* (a dusk
rite, at the report-lectern, that pays nothing). INV-17 makes the boundary canon: *the engine enforces exactly
the seven CUSTOM_KEYS + the one group latch; anything else is fiction.* The forged law's falsification test is
not poisoned because the Unlit Deep is categorically a different felt shape, place, and time, and the two
ordinances never reference each other. Both ship. (The R01 eighth-line slot carries the Unlit Deep's
"kept by no hand" line; the forged ordinance is a *separate* document, `the-eighth-way.md`.)

**P0-3 — two ideas place a mutating base lectern book at the same anchor.**
`counting-base-journal` (Brann's decrementing docket) and `record-writes-you-in` (the Hold-Book).
**RESOLUTION:** **unify into ONE book** (`WEB-MASTER §4`). The down-count is the Hold-Book's M1–M3 face; the
keeper-record is its M1→M5 face. One anchor (`stone_of_reckoning` companion lectern), one M4 re-read, one M5
rewrite. The "muster" and "the base log WAS the record" lines are the same payoff, authored once (and the
chiasmus de-slopped per slop A3).

**P0-4 — `DIVIDED` ending vs. the collective/no-chosen-one law, fed by four per-player profiling ideas.**
`record-writes-you-in`, `name-where-never-been`, `future-dated-grave`, `offline-skin-apparition` each build
per-player keeper-attribution surfaces; in aggregate a sharp group could reconstruct the honored/violated
split and watch `DIVIDED` confirm it.
**RESOLUTION:** **INV-16** (`WEB-MASTER §0.2`): no surface ever lets the group derive WHICH active player is on
which side; per-player surfaces rhyme on a chorus behavior, never the divergence extremes; keeper-enrolment
and carve-subjects ROTATE across all active players; the `DIVIDED` floor split is geometric, never
spatially corresponding to a per-player carve. Synthesis enforces this jointly (no single idea can).

Days are *expected pace*, not timers; gates are behavioral. The spine below shows, per Movement, the

optional bodies for facts that already ship (FACT 11 source; FACT 10/10b exile; FACT 14/15 felt).

## 10. ONE-PARAGRAPH SPINE (for the back of a napkin)

A friend finds a dead web page with a small downloadable map and a counter that reads *6*, plays the
melancholy 15-minute hold alone, and is told to bring the others to a server — where the thing stops behaving
like a map and says the *6* back at them (the frame-break: a map cannot know that). A friend screenshots a lit

ring; the group learns to read by two different doors and finds six keeper-stones that are warnings shaped

a forged eighth law that pays nothing, and a grave with their own name and a future date. They descend to
the one fire that never went out (and, if they carry a coal through a burned door, find its source below the
below — the keeping was always a carrying), keep (or break) the Unlit Deep together, and find the Seventh — a
keeper cast out for breaking *nothing* — whose erasure they can complete or undo, and whose home is out past a
door that is not a threshold, in the one place the record does not reach. Catching Iss's lie re-reads the
whole world cold: the doom-clock was a roll-call, the prophet's wall was his, the base log was always the
record filing them in. They cross the threshold by getting on a call (three hands, one word, at once),
walk to a real place, and bow together; the grave opens not as a grave but as their deposit slot, the
record adds their names, *received*, and the world flips to *kept* — they are now the markers for whoever
comes next, which is the one thing no document ever said and everything always pointed at.

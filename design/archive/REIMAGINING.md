# The Observance — REIMAGINING (world-first direction)

> Companion to `design/ARG-RESEARCH.md` (the cited craft this is built on) and `design/COHERENCE-AUDIT.md`
> (which already found the engine is "starved of authored world, not broken"). This doc proposes the new
> SHAPE. Spoiler-free: the actual answers live in the sealed answer bible (`arc/`), so Ethan stays unspoiled.

## The pivot, in one line
**From** "solve the stone, find five more, win" **→ to** "you're dropped into a place that half-survived, and
you reconstruct *who these people were and what happened to them* from the fragments they left." Decoding is
the **texture of discovery**, never the skeleton.

## What a player actually feels (the target experience)
You log into an ordinary, lived-in world — homes with food in the barrels, tools dropped mid-task, lit
streets, working farms. No quest, no marker. You poke around. You find a half-burned page authored by
someone named Aro. A villager mutters one strange line and won't elaborate. You notice a building that
shouldn't be there, and a stairway going *down* into architecture that isn't the same era — or the same
scale. You start asking *questions*, not solving puzzles. You screenshot the page into Discord; a friend
across the map found the page it's *replying to*. Threads start forming. And the world starts noticing
**you specifically** — a torch behind you is dark when you turn back; a book addresses you by name. Slowly
you realize these were normal people, and something happened to them. The deeper you go, the more you
suspect that whatever is on the surface with you now wasn't always... and were they even *human*? You never
get a clean look. The answer assembles itself out of the pieces.

## The new SKELETON — the rumor→verify loop + the thread map (replaces the cipher chain)
*(ARG-RESEARCH C4–C7, the single most portable idea from Outer Wilds.)*
- Every piece of lore is a **RUMOR** (heard, glimpsed from afar, told by an NPC) or an **EXPLORE** fact
  (verified firsthand on-site). Rumors create *direction* ("they say the Warden sealed the lower gate");
  arriving converts rumor → verified truth — and the verified truth can **contradict** the rumor ("it was
  sealed from the *outside* — to keep something *in*"). **That contradiction is the emotional beat.**
- The **Discord "Recovery Archive"** is the ship-log: every find becomes a **place-anchored card** that
  auto-clusters into a few colored **THREADS**. The map shows its own holes (a rumored place you've never
  visited is a silhouette card with a dotted line to it). **Progress = threads filling in**, not steps done.

## The five threads (your exact questions)
Every book, build, NPC line, and item is tagged to one of these + a color:
1. **WHO THEY WERE** — the people, as characters.
2. **WHAT THIS PLACE WAS** — the city / civilization / what it was *for*.
3. **WHAT HAPPENED** — the catastrophe, reconstructed from contradictory accounts.
4. **WHAT'S ON THE SURFACE WITH US** — the presence (the Watcher) now.
5. **WERE THEY HUMAN** — the deepest dread; answered spatially and by tonal decay before it's ever stated.

## The world tells the story before any text (geography IS history)
*(A2, A4, F1–F2, F10.)* Build a **believable lived-in human city first**, then bury part of it under — or
sink it beneath — a **later, wrong-scaled layer**. Descend and the architecture changes era, scale, culture
(different block palette, motifs, lighting, fog). The build relationships answer *"were they human before
us?"* with **zero words** — the bottom is human-scaled, the top is wrong-scaled. Each district has a
signature palette + a story readable from decay state alone (the harbor drowned first and is fully mossed;
the temple district fell last and is only cracked). The deepest/most-wrong zones get custom fog + desaturated
sky + near-black nights (resource pack). Then `F2`: seed **impossible-but-rule-based** anomalies into the
ordinary — a forest growing in a lightless cave, a wheat field with no sky — each a silent clue about what
these people (or the surface thing) could *do*.

## The lost people become CHARACTERS (this is where the keepers transform)
*(B4, B5, D4.)* The six keepers stop being "stone owners with a cipher each" and become **people** with
names, desires, relationships, and **contradictory accounts** of each other. Their journals, staged in date
order along a route, **decay in tone**: "planted the east field today" → "the water remembers names" → a
register no longer quite human. You reconstruct them as ordinary humans (a baker, a watchman, a child) —
*then watch them curdle.* A decoded fragment lands as "oh, **this** person did **that** to **that** person"
— a gut-punch, not a trivia unlock.

## How truth is delivered (fragments, not lorebooks)
*(A1, A3, B1–B7, C8.)*
- **Books = item descriptions:** terse, oblique, cross-referencing, authored by **roles/names + dates**
  ("WARDEN-3", "Maren, before the quiet"). Truth only resolves when the group holds 3+ together.
- **Staged tableaux, not loot:** a skeleton clutching a book at a barricade that faces *inward*; a child's
  bed beside an adult's hastily-packed chest. **Position carries the story.**
- **Redaction & omission:** pages torn at the load-bearing line, blacked-out names, a *missing winter* (book
  jumps date 3→5). The blackout invites a guess but never confirms it.
- **Contradictory accounts the player reconciles** — never adjudicated in-world; Discord is where they argue.
- **N-of-M gating:** no single player holds the whole picture (A finds the coords, B finds the time, C
  overhears why it matters) — forces real collaboration.

## Decoding survives only as TEXTURE
*(D1, E3, F12.)* When a cipher appears, it's **carved into a build that already tells a story** (a tomb wall,
a child's schoolbook), so even *un-decoded* it reads as character. Its **solution is a lore fragment** ("we
stopped being able to sleep"; "the third one came back changed") — **never a key to the next stone.** One
rate-determining gate per beat, payout = a reveal. Correct must be **obviously correct** (no anagrams; the
world confirms instantly).

## The reactive layer — the "it-knows-me" edge (our advantage over a scripted video)
*(F11, G1–G8.)*
- An **Attention / Observance meter** per player: rises with transgression (digging into sealed tombs,
  looting the dead, lingering in the dark), decays with restraint. Tiered manifestations: **T1 ambient**
  (cold breeze, distant whisper) → **T2 personal** (the half-beat-late footstep behind you; a torch out) →
  **T3 world-level** (a fog event; the Watcher moves a build overnight).
- **Rare, earned "it knows me" beats** grounded in *real dossier data*: a book that addresses you by name in
  old script ("we saw you come back to the well four times"); a sign that rewrites itself to a fact only true
  of you ("you have died here twice"). Restraint is the whole craft — one perfect beat beats ten cheap ones.
- **The Watcher is never seen cleanly** — censor box / fog / glimpse-through-a-gap / forced blindness. The
  withheld image beats any model (Wifies: 10M+ views, essentially no jumpscares).
- **The showrunner = live puppetmaster:** stages the *conditions* for reactive moments (so they land on
  camera without being faked), incorporates the friends' own speculation and the names they invent, conducts
  the peaks, leaves white space.

## The emotional rhythm (NOT a difficulty ramp)
*(D2, D3.)* Compose the multi-week arc as a rhythm: a **shock** beat → a **quiet, mundane** beat (a child's
toy, a recipe book — grief + breathing room) → a **confusing** beat (contradictory accounts) → a **reveal**.
Release in **overnight batches**; schedule deliberate **fallow days** where nothing new appears (the silence
becomes ominous). **Celebrate competence first** — give the friends ordinary goals (catalog the ruins,
restore a building, survive a winter) so their early mastery is real, which makes the downturn land.

## The structural spine that keeps it from collapsing (plan the ending FIRST)
*(E1–E6, B3.)*
- A **sealed ANSWER BIBLE** (`arc/`) holds the full truth (who / what the place was / what happened / were
  they human / the ending) — authored **before** a single fragment is placed. Ethan stays unspoiled; I hold it.
- A **fragment → revelation ledger**: every findable fragment is tagged to which sealed answer it pays down.
  *If a fragment doesn't pay a planned debt, cut it.*
- The **citation web**: everything is named consistently across the whole world (Cistern 7, Warden Aro) so
  cross-references hold — this is the coherence discipline you already insisted on, now load-bearing.
- **Trickle real answers** every act (at least one undeniable reveal); never let open questions outrun
  answered. **Every gate also rewards lore.** Ruthless red-herring audit.

- **The reactive showrunner spine** (just built) → gains the Attention meter + conditions-staging.
- **The oracle / answer-sign / #the-record loop** → now resolves *fragment submissions* (a decoded line, a

- **The whole anti-jank engine, beat palette, resource-pack/FAWE/MythicMobs/Citizens stack** → unchanged;
  this is exactly the toolset the research says to use.

## What CHANGES (the spine)
- **Retire the stone-cipher gauntlet as the skeleton.** The six keeper-stones stop being "6 ciphers in
  sequence"; the 23-puzzle web's *web* becomes the **thread map**, its *puzzles* become texture (solutions =
  lore fragments), and its *characters* (the keepers) become people you reconstruct.
- **The win condition** stops being visible/countable ("gather tokens + bow") and becomes **"the threads fill
  in / the truth assembles"** — the ending stays sealed and is reached by reconstruction, not collection.
- **The coherence audit's #1 gap (no authored world/structures) flips from a liability to the PRIMARY
  build** — because now *the world is the content.* The stones, districts, tableaux, and papers we author ARE
  the game.

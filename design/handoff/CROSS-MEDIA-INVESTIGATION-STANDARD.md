# Cross-Media Investigation Standard

Status: **BINDING CROSS-PHASE AUTHORITY — BRAD APPROVAL NULL; M4 CLOSED**

The Observance is a cross-media ARG. Minecraft is one evidence surface among the hosted website and
community tab, NPC dialogue and remembered conversations, earlier and later callbacks, found footage,
unlisted video, files, images, audio, metadata, recovered documents, and social activity. A conclusion
may join a remembered NPC line, a community post, one video frame, a filename, an earlier book passage,
and a Minecraft artifact. The work should reward notebooks, provenance, pattern recognition, cipher
work, reinterpretation, discussion, and delayed synthesis.

Lore evidence must feel consequential, spooky, uncanny, human, and connected to the central mystery.
Ledgers, capacities, routes, and administrative records may supply texture or one evidentiary layer;
they may not become the campaign's dominant investigation grammar.

## Robust Minecraft boundary

Minecraft geometry must be compact, authored, legible, and achievable. Do not make a mandatory P4
answer depend on literal cart-path tracing, block-by-block archaeology, or a long coordinate-perfect
material simulation generated in code. Spatial and environmental evidence can establish atmosphere,
corroborate a file, provide a robust landmark, or support an optional inference. It cannot become a
fragile bespoke-block-placement prerequisite.

Map and route reasoning remains welcome when performed against supplied images, diagrams, files, or
other stable artifacts. The design test is whether the deduction survives harmless world-decoration
variation without losing its authored meaning.

## Fair cross-media challenge design

The Mara/library cipher is an approved reference pattern. Coordinates such as `3-1-4`, `2-1-5`, and
`6-3-1` may resolve shelf-line-word, page-line-word, or another clearly inferable indexed-book grammar.
Its lesson is a discoverable, fictionally grounded key—not a template to repeat.

Distinct mechanism families may include timestamp-to-transcript indexing; community names or dates
aligned with acrostics or archive order; contact-sheet or crop coordinates; fairly corroborated
spectrogram, Morse, DTMF, or reversed audio with accessibility equivalents; provenance-bearing file
metadata and revision history; NPC-derived substitution alphabets; layered redactions; supplied-image
map overlays; page/line/word, shelf/spine/letter, catalog, marginal-mark, or mis-shelving book variants;
video-frame callbacks; coordinated witness omissions; and catch-up-safe website/community changes.
These are a palette, not a quota, and should be distributed across arcs rather than crowded into one.

Every cipher has a discoverable key and fair corroboration. Correct answers pass without source-touch
receipts and may be remembered, shared, or found out of order. A mandatory path may not collapse to one
surface when approved any-subset and catch-up rules require alternatives. UI friction is not difficulty;
purple portent and overt puzzle-instruction signage are not clues.

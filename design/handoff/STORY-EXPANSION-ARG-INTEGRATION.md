## Story-first rule

Major investigation design begins with a revelation and emotional reversal, then distributes evidence and
actions that can earn it. A mechanism never creates its own story obligation. Every active case defines:
the live question, plausible wrong readings, human stake, earned canon belief, evidence roles, inference,
recontextualization, player-caused response, alternate/catch-up paths, recovery, novelty, and likely theory
paths. Conclusions cannot be printed by one source and then accepted as restatement.

Text remains abundant and may be technical, partial, contradictory, subtle, and demanding. It must sound
like people doing work or living together rather than like an author announcing puzzles. Documents are
evidence among conversations, place, action, media, community history, physical systems, versions, and
consequences. Answer inputs are a minority diegetic verb.

### Plain-language and puzzle depth

Player-facing writing uses normal modern speech, short sentences, concrete nouns and verbs, and the real
language of the document or speaker. Voices differ through priorities, rhythm, omissions, humor, and work
habits—not ornate words. Pseudo-archaic grammar, vague horror declarations, repeated incantations, and
cryptic-for-cryptic's-sake prose fail review. Decoded plaintext is clear unless its ambiguity is itself a
fair, intentional layer. Automated readability signals may flag a human review target but never become a
prose quota.

This does not simplify the investigation. Traditional ciphers and puzzles remain substantial across
Minecraft, books, signs, ordinary text, Copperline, Discord, media, files, and the Unlit. A fair cipher has
an in-world reason, an earned key or key trail, a clear payload, and a later consequence. Strong sequences
may require recognizing a transform, authenticating the right source and edition, decoding, deciding what
the result refers to, testing it elsewhere, and interpreting the response. Substitution, transposition,
indexed books, acrostics, metadata, timecode, spectrograms, image layers, map transforms, provenance,
spatial arrangement, and recovered older ideas are examples, not a catalog. A key beside its ciphertext,
a naked decoder, or repeated read-copy-submit loops fail review.

## Real input surfaces

Every input must exist on the named platform. The complete inventory and proof state lives in
`campaign/platform-input-feasibility-matrix.json`.

Minecraft prefers physical actions when the fiction is physical. Short text or structured findings may
use the experimental Paper 1.21.11 Dialog API only after the exact API/build is pinned, all response values
are server-validated, layout/client smoke tests pass, and the same predicate has a stable namespaced
Brigadier command fallback. A desk, terminal, or NPC may open that dialog; it does not become a hidden
prerequisite. Ordinary chat is never the required parser.

A lectern displays a book and page state; it is not a text field. Sign entry requires a real editor flow,
single-editor lease, side/wax/protection/cancellation/cleanup/restart/concurrency proof and is only for short
fictionally justified input. Writable books require real `BookEditEvent` custody, reissue, signing,
inventory, and Adventure-mode proof. Anvil rename, fake packet forms, or chat interception are not defaults.
Buttons, levers, containers, routes, arrangements, and repairs need exact affordance, predicate, wrong-state
return, protection, and restart tests.

Copperline uses semantic HTML forms with server-side validation, group authorization where required,
origin/CSRF controls appropriate to the deployed stack, idempotency, rate limits, pending/error/success
states, refresh/retry behavior, and mobile/keyboard accessibility. Discord uses documented slash-command
options, buttons/selects, or modal inputs with exact custom IDs, authorization, prompt acknowledgement or
defer, idempotent retry, restart behavior, and private failure where appropriate. Static props, embeds,
images, audio, and files never pretend to accept input. Cross-surface input commits once to its owning
authority and reconciles by stable idempotency key.

Every surface makes the accepted response type, submit/confirm/cancel action, and result state legible
without revealing the deduction. Wrong, incomplete, throttled, and technical failure are distinct.

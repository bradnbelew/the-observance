- **C1 — Refuge and Ways:** The rebuilt investigation will establish that the Deep Hold was a real Long Cold refuge whose seven practical safety procedures hardened into sacred Ways and institutional control.
- **C2 — Six Keepers:** The rebuild will preserve Vaun, Mara, Sella, Orin, Brann, and Iss as six distinct contemporaries and will never revive a six-as-one interpretation.

- **C4 — Break and Nessa:** The rebuild will require players to prove the Break had multiple human, structural, and Record-related causes, with Iss's crossing as an accelerator rather than a total cause, and to clear Nessa fully.
- **C5 — Dark, Record, Watcher, Averyn:** The rebuild will keep the Dark, the civic Record, Averyn, and the Watcher related but non-identical, with Averyn trapped as the Record's human interface and the Watcher speaking through her constrained consciousness.
- **C6 — AVERYN affidavits:** The rebuild will preserve six independent Keeper affidavits hiding one letter each of AVERYN while making clear that the six failed to free her.
- **C7 — Prior expedition and sabotage:** The rebuild will preserve mkept, Ash, Rook, and Wren as a real four-person prior expedition whose release attempt failed because partially absorbed Wren betrayed their names, routes, plans, and fears.
- **C8 — Release outcome:** The rebuild will culminate in reconstructing the truth, clearing Nessa, proving the multi-cause Break, repairing infrastructure, completing the prior investigation, judging Wren, restoring Averyn's identity, closing the Record, always freeing Averyn, and varying only publish versus deliberate unfiling and Wren's remembrance before the shutdown and coda.
- **C9 — Ambiguity boundary:** The rebuild will keep exactly the three approved ontological questions unresolved while treating the roster, causes, identities, required journey, and universal release as settled facts.
- **Ambiguity 1 — End voice:** The rebuild will not resolve whether the final speaking continuity is metaphysically the original Averyn or a perfect retained continuity.
- **Ambiguity 2 — The Dark:** The rebuild will not resolve what the Dark ultimately is.
- **Ambiguity 3 — Wren:** The rebuild will not resolve whether Wren's partial self can become autonomous after the Record closes.

## Decisions proposed for approval

- The memorable structure is **twelve investigation arcs**, not a fixed number of cases/nodes/stations.
- The pacing center is **26.5 active hours**, allowing the approved **20–30 hour** range.
- All substantial arcs and evidence lanes are required, while work inside an unlocked arc is free-order
  and completable by any subset.
- P12 is the only preferred whole-group convergence; an explicit accessibility path preserves evidence
  and choice when a friend cannot attend.
- Catch-up is delivered primarily through an in-world field archive, mirrored by a Discord docket and
  earned Copperline/public Record surfaces.
- H0 navigation may be always visible; all solution-bearing H1–H3 hints require Brad approval, with only
  bounded H1 pre-approval allowed for a named arc/session.
- Automatic automation is limited to A0 readback and A1 non-personal, text-free, ephemeral ambience;
  personalization, public/social intervention, world/progression changes, and irreversible actions use
  progressively stronger approval gates.
- The existing automatic `name_on_wall` behavior must be approval-gated or removed from automatic mode.
- Brad's four known videos and the WAV are preserved; none may be replaced until source custody,
  audiovisual review, and an isolated Brad decision are recorded.
- `hold.zip` remains the only player-facing standalone save; every post-onboarding Minecraft beat runs
  on the brother-hosted Crafty Paper server; private vertical-slice saves are Brad-only review artifacts.
- Exact Supabase/Vercel/both-Railway/Crafty/media parity receipts are mandatory before production cutover.

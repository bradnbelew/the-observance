# Copperline Hosting & Community Archive

> **BINDING CROSS-PHASE AUTHORITY.** Copperline expansion is approved as design direction; Brad has
> not approved M3 V5, M4 remains closed, and no production mutation is authorized.

Copperline is an old, believable fictional hosting provider and community archive. It cannot remain a
thin prologue address-recovery screen. Server listings, profiles, support tickets, maintenance notices,
staff replies, changelogs, guides, threads, moderation artifacts, quotes, edits, timestamps, reactions,
cached or dead attachments, broken embeds, mirrors, and archival gaps accumulate into human history.

Most of that history is ordinary life. As a planning credibility alarm, a substantial cold-readable
slice should ordinarily contain at least three parts lived texture for one part direct actionable clue.
This is not a writing quota: never add filler or weaken voices to hit a number. Human review must confirm
the archive feels inhabited before it feels instrumented.

mkept, Ash, Rook, Wren, regulars, moderators, support workers, and other fictional users have distinct
work vocabularies, relationships, mistakes, loyalties, jokes, frustrations, and silences. They disagree
for human reasons—memory, embarrassment, bureaucracy, incomplete information, conflict, and
self-protection—not because everyone is a cryptic dispenser.

Clues may live naturally in chronology, quotations, attachment custody, mirrors, edits, moderation,
account behavior, duplicated language, filenames, or what someone does not answer. Historical posts can
become newly meaningful after Minecraft discoveries, NPC lines, footage, or later document versions.
Immutable versions remain available; reactive changes append or reveal receipted history rather than
silently rewriting it.

## Ladder boundary

C01 retains mundane credibility and earns exactly this belief: **mkept was a real person who deliberately
preserved a damaged server**. Do not reveal later cases early. Copperline users discuss modern servers,
files, footage, builds, disappearances, anomalies, and one another. Ancient truth reaches the archive
through recovered artifacts and modern interpretation, never through unnatural lore dumping.

The site must not become purple portent, an obvious fake ARG portal, numbered clues, or a place where
every broken link matters. Responsive, keyboard, screen-reader, semantic-thread, reflow, media-equivalent,
catch-up, replay, outage, and any-subset behavior are required.

Offline specifications and fixtures may define the information architecture, voices, time/version graph,
threads, callbacks, and deterministic tests. They are not deployment, URL, cache, browser, accessibility,
unlock, outage, player, or Brad receipts. Those remain explicit future gates with exact hashes and
readbacks. Production remains untouched.

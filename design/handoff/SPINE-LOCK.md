# SPINE LOCK — the one hard constraint on the ground-up rebuild

**Read this before anything in Codex's "Golden Standard" rebuild plan. It amends that plan.**

The ambitious rebuild is **approved** — new campaign version, coordinate-native Hold, all-new rooms and
puzzles, rewritten prose, new data model, longer runtime, the review artifacts, the approval gates. This
document does **not** argue scope, cost, or length. It adds exactly one hard constraint that the plan is
currently missing.

## The problem with the plan as written

Codex's plan says: *"Everything inside that journey is rewriteable: canon, characters, cases, puzzles,
structures, media, prose, mechanics, progression counts, antagonist, and finale details,"* and later
*"remove the locked requirements for … six Keepers … and current endings."*

That is too loose. Rewriting the canon, the antagonist, and the finale *freely* risks producing a
coherent, well-made game that **is no longer *The Observance*.** Brad's one stated fear is **losing the
spine we already have.** So the rule is amended:

> **Everything is rewriteable EXCEPT the spine. The spine is LOCKED.** The rebuild must *deepen and
> better express* the existing story — not replace it with a different one.

The spine is the **story that happened, the shape of the journey, and the register.** It is the *what*.
Everything about *how players discover it* — every room, clue, cipher, book, sign, NPC line, mechanic,
node count, media specific — is the *flesh*, and the flesh is yours to rebuild completely.

## The LOCKED spine (from `arc/WORLD-BIBLE.md`, the current spoiler authority — verify against it, do not restate from memory)

**A. The journey (structural through-line — locked in order):**
Copperline web archaeology → a gated, downloadable Minecraft save → discovering/reconstructing the live
server from that save → discovering Discord *from inside Minecraft* → a surface settlement with ordinary
inhabitants and selective hauntings → the Surface Mouth and descent → the Deep Hold as a real civic
institution, with the Keeper stones → the failed prior-player expedition camp → a group convergence →
the release of the Record and a persistent coda. Individual beats may be reshaped; this sequence and its
transmedia hops are the recognizable shape of the game and do not change.

**B. The register (locked):** grounded civic horror. Believable institutions, construction, labor,
records, shortages, and human decisions gradually reveal something impossible. The horror is
bureaucratic and human before it is supernatural. Personal haunting is *selective*, not the primary
mode. The player is always inside a *world they are investigating* — never "playing an ARG."

**C. The canon truths (locked — these are load-bearing; the rebuild reveals them better, never contradicts them):**
1. During the Long Cold a surface community dug the Deep Hold as a **real refuge** (children learned
   there, trades continued, water was filtered, birds warned of bad air) whose seven practical safety
   procedures hardened into sacred Ways and then into institutional control.
2. **Six distinct Keepers** — Vaun, Mara, Sella, Orin, Brann, Iss — managed parts of the crisis. They
   are **six separate people. The "six-as-one" reading is FALSE and must never return as a reveal.**
3. **Averyn** — the registrar and cistern analyst, a seventh official *witness*, not a themed Keeper —
   proved the Cistern Winter was caused by diverted supplies, counterfeit filter cloth, and falsified
   records, and was **erased from the roster** rather than admit the system could lie.
4. **The Break was multi-cause.** Iss secretly crossing the Deep Line accelerated it but did not
   magically cause every failure. A scapegoated worker (Nessa) is cleared by the investigation.
5. **The Record / the Watcher.** The civic Record — a monitoring/memory system built near a prehuman
   thing called **the Dark** — was activated as an emergency archive during the collapse. Averyn entered
   it to help and became **trapped as its human interface. The Watcher is the Record speaking through
   Averyn's constrained consciousness.** The Dark, the Record, Averyn, and the Watcher are related but
   not identical.
6. **The six hid one letter of AVERYN each** in independent affidavits; they did not free her.
7. **The prior expedition is real and load-bearing:** four modern investigators — mkept, Ash, Rook, and
   **Wren** — found the abandoned server and nearly completed a release. **Wren, partially absorbed by
   the Record and afraid closure would erase him, betrayed them** — gave the Watcher their names,
   routes, plans, and fears. Their run failed through **sabotage, not a missing answer.**
8. **The finale is the release:** the players recover the server, reconstruct the true history, clear the
   scapegoat, prove the multi-cause Break, repair real infrastructure, finish the prior investigation,
   judge Wren, restore Averyn's identity, and **close the Record. Every ending frees Averyn.** The group's
   choices determine only whether her name is **published or left deliberately unfiled**, and how Wren is
   remembered. It ends in a release act → cinematic shutdown → persistent coda — **not a branching-ending
   matrix, not a "what now?" fizzle.**
9. **The deliberately-unresolved ambiguities stay exactly three:** whether the end-voice is
   metaphysically the original Averyn or a perfect retained continuity; what the Dark ultimately is; and
   whether Wren's partial self can become autonomous after the Record closes. The roster, causes,
   identities, and required sequence are **not** ambiguous and must not be muddied.

## What is FLESH (rebuild it completely — this is the whole point of the rebuild)

- The Hold's physical layout, room program, and every structure (per docs 01, 04).
- Every case, node, puzzle, cipher, mechanic, and its difficulty — how each canon truth is *uncovered*
  (per doc 02). Node/case counts derive from the story, not an imposed number.
- Every player-facing word: books, signs, item lore, NPC dialogue (per doc 03).
- The specific evidence, clues, provenance, and the exact investigative verbs.
- The Unlit / altered-copy region's specific form.
- Progression pacing, catch-up surfaces, the free-paced model, the data schema.
- Media *specifics and delivery* — with one caveat below.

## Two preservation notes beyond the spine

1. **Preserve the found-footage videos Brad already made** where they still fit the rebuilt story. Codex's
   plan says "replace or re-edit every current asset as needed" — re-edit and re-host for the diegetic
   frame is fine, but do not discard Brad's own footage wholesale without checking; it is his work and
   he asked to build around it.
2. **The three strong cases (C04 Cistern Winter, C07 ASH-13 camp, C08 Wren) are the fidelity exemplars,
   not sacred text.** They already do investigation-woven-with-story at the target standard. Rebuild them
   too if the new layout/canon expression needs it — but study *why* they work and hold every rebuilt
   case to that bar.

## The gate this adds to the plan

Codex's plan already gates on "replacement World Bible approved." That gate is now judged against **this
spine lock**:

- Before rewriting canon, Codex produces a **one-page Spine Conformance Statement**: for each locked item
  A–C above, a sentence on how the rebuilt campaign still hits it. Brad approves that *first*.
- If the rebuild wants to change any *locked* item, that is a **spine change** — Codex must flag it
  explicitly, in isolation, with the reason, and get Brad's sign-off. It may not be bundled into a
  general canon rewrite.
- Everything in the "flesh" list needs no spine sign-off — rebuild freely, to the standards in docs
  01–08.

## Brad owns the spine's exact contents

This document proposes locking **the existing spine** because that is what Brad said he does not want to
lose. Brad can edit this list — add, remove, or change a locked invariant — but the point stands: the set
of locked invariants is decided and written down *before* canon rewriting begins, so the rebuild has one
fixed story to serve instead of drifting into a different, if coherent, game.

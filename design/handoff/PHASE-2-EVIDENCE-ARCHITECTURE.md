# Phase 2 / M1 — Evidence Architecture

Status: **PHASE 2 COMPLETE — STANDING APPROVAL APPLIES AFTER CHECKPOINT COMMIT**

Authority order: `SPINE-LOCK.md` → approved `SPINE-CONFORMANCE.md` → approved Phase 1 authorities →
this document. This is a logical evidence design. Finding IDs are design handles, not database rows,
node IDs, predicates, rooms, coordinates, commands, or implementation promises.

## Architecture rules

- P1–P12 remain ordered. Findings inside an open arc may be gathered in any order unless a stated
  synthesis needs their receipts.
- A finding is accepted only from cited observations with source identity and custody. A conclusion
  cannot be committed from attendance, elapsed time, telemetry, an operator assertion, or a future clue.
- Every substantial lane feeds its arc conclusion or a named later reread. Nothing below is optional
  lore disguised as an investigation.
- Minecraft is primary after the prologue. Web, Discord, media, and ordinary analysis tools extend an
  earned route and always return a receipt, interpretation, or physical change to the live world.
- “Hard” means comparing independent sources, proving provenance, reconstructing space or chronology,
  and arguing from contradictions. It never means a naked decoder, free brute force, a fragile entity
  set, inaccessible audio, or guessing where to submit.
- Every audiovisual payload has a visually/textually redundant investigative path. Redundancy proves
  the same proposition through another source; it does not print the answer.
- H0 is always available orientation. H1–H3 follow the approval contract in
  `PHASE-1-PROGRESSION-GOVERNANCE.md`; this document identifies authored recovery moves, not automatic
  delivery authority.

| Emotional function | Curiosity earns trust: the first “haunting” is human maintenance and care. |

Evidence chain:

1. **P1.F1 — Account identity.** An expired directory row, uploader attribution, and recovered account
   docket independently share `mkept` and Copperline identifiers. Players deduce that mkept and the host
   are real, while the damaged service field remains unsolved. Provenance: public Copperline pages with
   stable route and capture receipt.
2. **P1.F2 — Service 1842.** Damage notation on the directory, a separate staff convention in ticket
   history, and the docket marks establish the transformation used to recover `1842`. The teaching key
   is not adjacent to the encoded field. Players file the service number, not an occult phrase.
3. **P1.F3 — Intentional preservation.** Ticket `9137`, an archive post, and backup timestamps form a
   custody sequence: mkept reopened access, checked the artifact, and left a route another person could
   follow. This rules out an accidental public leak and opens P2.

Hints/recovery: H0 lists the three ordinary page families and the recovery form. H1 points to mismatched
service fields; H2 names ownership/timestamp comparison; H3 identifies the exact two records to align.
All pages remain reopenable from the earned account history; a broken route has a canonical archived

Catch-up/replay: the Copperline account page and, after P3, the field archive/Discord docket show the
three committed receipts and links to originals. A late player can repeat the search without reopening
the form or duplicating the P2 gate.

Failure recovery: a wrong service value changes nothing and distinguishes a bad theory from malformed
input. A web outage leaves a local director-visible health fault; it never fabricates P1 receipts.

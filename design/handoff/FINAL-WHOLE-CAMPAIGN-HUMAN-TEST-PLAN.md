# Final Whole-Campaign Human Test Plan

Status: prepared, not started. This plan does not claim Brad approval or launch readiness.

This is the one final private acceptance run for the current P1–P12 candidate. It is deliberately
spoiler-free. It tells the test group where a real surface begins and how to report what happened;
it never supplies conclusions, cipher keys, hidden routes, exact answers, or the intended theory.

## Start gate

Do not begin until one checkpoint owns all of these receipts:

- the complete routed audit passes;
- a fresh Paper 1.21.11 target builds the full Hold, stops, restarts, and independently re-audits;
- the exact dashboard checkpoint is reachable on a private Vercel preview;
- Discord uses a distinct private test guild/channel and non-production state, or its unavailable
  beats have an explicitly approved local deterministic substitute;
- no test can write to production Supabase, Railway, Vercel, Discord, Crafty, or media originals;
- every enabled media item has custody, captions/transcript or visual equivalent, and a working
  private delivery route;
- the Paper server is localhost/private, online-mode where required for the actual rehearsal,
  non-op Adventure by default, and pinned to the receipted plugin/pack/world hashes;
- the group receives only the initial Copperline rabbit hole and ordinary client setup.

If any item is false, record the exact gap and stop. Do not replace a missing surface with verbal
story explanation.

## Test group and duration

Use three to seven players if practical, with at least one player who has not read the design files.
A solo recovery pass follows the group run. Plan several sessions and allow the authored 30–40
active-hour target to be tested honestly. A rushed review is not evidence that the investigation is
too easy or too hard.

The director observes and records. The director does not solve, translate, point at clue surfaces,
name interaction controls, or explain the story. Hints use only the authored, approval-governed
recovery path and are logged with time and reason.

## One linear administration path

The case order remains P1 through P12. Within an open case, players may divide, revisit earlier
surfaces, share remembered evidence, and complete discoveries out of order. The test administrator
does not force a prescribed clue order.

### 0. Clean start and onboarding

1. Record the source commit and every deployed artifact hash/URL/address.
2. Prove the group has no prior progression, late flags, cached answer state, operator powers, or
   hidden solution material.
3. Give only the initial Copperline location and normal Minecraft/Discord connection instructions.
4. Ask each player privately what they think this place is before they begin.
5. Fail onboarding if the interface does not make its available actions legible. Do not fail it
   merely because the mystery remains difficult.

### P1–P2: Copperline archaeology and the preserved server

Players investigate Copperline as an old hosting/community archive, not a clue menu. Observe whether
ordinary posts, account history, support material, edits, and dead attachments feel human and whether
the group can authenticate a deliberate preservation act without a page printing the final belief.

Record:

- which ordinary detail first became suspicious and why;
- every competing authenticity theory;
- how players found the next surface;
- whether a real action caused an authored response;
- whether the preserved package and Minecraft handoff were understandable without retyping facts
  already on screen.

### P3–P4: settlement, Mouth, and refuge/control reversal

Let players meet the present settlement and enter the Hold without a tour. Residents must feel like
people with work, relationships, uncertainty, and changing views—not quest kiosks. The P4 case must
cross Copperline history, Minecraft evidence, remembered statements, provenance work, and a player
test/action. No source may state the whole conclusion.

Record:

- the group's initial explanation of the ruin;
- what changed that explanation;
- whether `COPY BEFORE SOURCE`, if decoded, created a live problem instead of serving as the answer;
- what players chose to test and what surface answered them;
- whether the Watcher response felt caused by the group;
- every moment that reduced to read, restate, submit.

Interpretive input passes only if short meaning components or a natural physical action accept the
same defensible conclusion in more than one wording. Exact artifacts may be exact only when the
world fairly yields their bytes. Correct knowledge must pass with zero observation receipts.

### P5–P6: people of the Hold and six professional investigations

Players should care about recurring households before their materials become case evidence. The six
Keepers must emerge through different work, relationships, help, failure, contradiction, and attempted
correction. They must not feel like six themed stations.

Record:

- household names/details players remember without prompting;
- cross-room and cross-case evidence actually used in discussion;
- how each Keeper's professional habits changed the investigative verb;
- callbacks players noticed late;
- repeated furniture, prose, answer forms, or pacing;
- quiet discoveries that mattered without opening a lock.

### P7–P8: Nessa, the Break, and the shared repair

The group authenticates responsibility rather than choosing a confession. Nessa's exoneration must
come from material, custody, chronology, and contradiction. The Break remains multi-cause. A bounded
repair must alter the physical world and later resident/community responses.

Record:

- plausible wrong theories and the evidence that survived or broke them;
- whether testimony corroborated rather than replaced proof;
- whether the repair affordance, predicate, wrong return, protection, and restart state were clear;
- how the repair connected Hold history, Dob's patch, and the players' action;
- the exact visible and narrative consequences on every responding surface.

### P9–P10: the last company and Wren

The camp must feel occupied and abandoned under pressure, not like four labeled desks. Players build
a timeline from friendship, work habits, provenance, opportunity, information flow, and modern
Copperline traces. Wren's responsibility must not depend on a confession or a source-touch quota.

Record:

- how players distinguished mkept, Ash, Rook, and Wren before the accusation;
- competing leak/betrayal timelines;
- media/file details that changed meaning after a conversation or Minecraft discovery;
- whether Discord functioned as a community/character surface rather than an answer bot;
- the moral disagreement before and after proof;
- whether every valid judgment branch remained finishable and produced a distinct aftermath.

### P11: Averyn, the Record, and the category correction

The group may reasonably suspect a seventh Keeper. The earned correction must distinguish six
Keepers, seven practical Ways, and the excluded registrar/witness without reviving the retired blank
seventh-slot gimmick. Averyn, Record, Watcher, and Dark remain related but not identical.

Record:

- when the group's category model formed;
- the strongest false reading and why it was fair;
- which earlier solved clue required reinterpretation;
- how provenance established Averyn as a person;
- whether all three locked ambiguities remained open;
- whether the payoff changed what players felt about earlier people and systems.

### P5–P10 interleaved: the Unlit

The seven house investigations and base synthesis are required, but expeditions may occur in any
order and with any player subset. The Unlit must behave as an imperfect copy, not a kiosk set or
combat dungeon. Its bounded player-authored token/repair arrangement must be copied and altered
deterministically without ingesting names, private storage, logs, fears, or unrestricted text.

Record:

- entry/exit, death, quit, inventory, border, borrowed-light, and anti-cheese behavior;
- non-audio access to every required signal;
- what changed between expeditions and why players returned;
- how surface discoveries reinterpreted a house and vice versa;
- at the registered surface, use the physical selectors to arrange six marks drawn only from water,
  heat, watch, and record; include record and at least three civic roles, then use the physical send
  control. Use `/obscopy set <1-6> <water|heat|watch|record>` and `/obscopy confirm` only as the
  tested accessibility/recovery path, not as the expected first interaction;
- the exact arranged input and deterministic altered copy: each three-cell row returns mirrored and
  exactly the first copied record mark becomes watch; no other cell changes;
- after the local return appears, refresh Copperline's retained Wren moderation chronology and run
  Discord `/investigate status`. Both must acknowledge the same copy event without revealing a new
  confession, resolving the Dark, or claiming that Copperline received player text or identity;
- reset/retry, duplicate-confirm, and a different second-pattern attempt. The committed pattern must
  remain stable, the duplicate must be idempotent, and the different pattern must be refused;
- restart and absent-player recovery;
- any ontology claim that improperly resolves what the Dark is.

### P12: convergence, release, and coda

The readiness gate checks earned state and exact relationships, never click completion. Both publish
and deliberate-unfile treatments release Averyn. Wren's judgment changes aftermath, not eligibility.
The final operator arm remains separate and explicit.

Record:

- whether players understood what decision they were making without being told what to believe;
- whether earlier affidavits and artifacts changed meaning on return;
- whether local durable release completed before external theater;
- restart/idempotency at every safe rehearsal boundary;
- exact Minecraft, Copperline, Discord, NPC, media, and dashboard responses;
- whether the coda changed each living resident and preserved both valid ending dimensions.

## Required negative and recovery passes

After the cold group path, repeat safely on isolated state:

1. Submit a correct defensible conclusion with zero source interactions. It must pass.
2. Click or invoke every obvious surface without reasoning. It must not complete a case.
3. Try plausible wrong and true-but-not-the-door theories. They must not advance, and meaningful
   near theories should receive authored acknowledgement where specified.
4. Repeat accepted actions. They must be idempotent and must not duplicate items, posts, dialogue,
   gates, or event receipts.
5. Stop/restart after each major transition family; re-audit state and physical truth.
6. Disconnect the initiating player and continue with a different subset.
7. Join late and replay only earned context without spoilers or missing eligibility.
8. Simulate web, Discord, media, and database outages. Minecraft remains locally finishable and
   queues/reconciliation neither lose nor duplicate consequences.
9. Test keyboard/mobile web forms, Discord button/select/modal acknowledgement, Paper command
   fallback, book/sign/dialog layout, non-audio equivalents, and readable failure states.
10. Test non-op Adventure protection, normal survival outside campaign regions, inventories, gates,
    routes, collision, and safe return from every required place.

## Per-case human receipt

For each P1–P12 case, record without solutions in the public summary:

- cold start/end time and active time;
- initial, competing, wrong, and final theories;
- surfaces and people used;
- player-initiated action and exact world response;
- cipher/puzzle operations and whether the key trail was fair;
- provenance and cross-case callbacks;
- discussion/asymmetry and catch-up behavior;
- input affordance or UI friction;
- plain-language/voice defects;
- visual, spatial, navigation, collision, support, and furnishing defects;
- hint/recovery use;
- emotional turn and earned belief in the players' own words;
- pass, revision required, or blocked, with evidence.

## Whole-campaign rejection criteria

The candidate is not launch-ready if any of these dominates or occurs at a required gate:

- source states conclusion, player restates it, answer box accepts it;
- long hidden canonical sentence or guessing the author's wording;
- click/possession/telemetry quota gates correct knowledge;
- disconnected case islands with no later reference, reinterpretation, consequence, or human stake;
- repeated document furniture, repeated answer form, repeated case rhythm, or superficial cipher skin;
- single-surface bounded investigation where cross-surface alternatives are required;
- player action causes no authored world response;
- UI opacity or overflow masquerades as difficulty;
- residents act as kiosks, Copperline as a clue menu, Discord as an answer bot, Minecraft as the
  whole puzzle app, or the Unlit as seven stations;
- purple/archaic/puzzle-narrator prose where plain human or professional language should carry it;
- an absent player, external outage, restart, or missed interaction makes completion impossible;
- later truth resolves one of the three locked ambiguities or violates the fixed revelation ladder;
- any production/public mutation or unreceipted media derivative occurs during rehearsal.

## Final decision

Automated passes remain necessary but insufficient. Brad records one of:

- `APPROVED FOR CONTROLLED PRIVATE LAUNCH PREPARATION`;
- `REVISION REQUIRED`, with exact case/surface evidence;
- `BLOCKED`, with the external fact or unavailable asset named exactly.

Only a later explicit production/public preflight and confirmation can authorize production data,
service promotion, Crafty/brother mutation, public DNS/domain promotion, Discord production actions,
media publication, or player invitations.

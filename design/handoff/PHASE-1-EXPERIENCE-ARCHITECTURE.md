## Experience promise

The Observance is a free-paced, Minecraft-primary investigation for about six friends, played by any
available subset over several weeks. A first complete run targets **26.5 active hours**, with a healthy
range of **20–30 hours**. No content is time-gated or missable, every substantial investigation is
ultimately required, and every absence can be repaired through durable catch-up and replay.

The investigation advances through evidence and changed beliefs, not through elapsed time, operator
schedule, repeated interaction templates, or an imposed node count. Exact cases, nodes, rooms, and
mechanics are derived later from this map.

## Revelation discipline

Each arc may create suspicion about later truths but may not collapse them early. The reveal ladder is:

1. a real person preserved a route;
2. the route leads to one live world and a real community;
3. the Hold was a functioning refuge;
4. useful Ways became institutional control;
5. six distinct people administered the crisis;
6. the institution falsified Cistern Winter and scapegoated Nessa;
7. the Break was multi-cause and the infrastructure remains materially legible;
8. four modern investigators nearly finished the work;
9. Wren sabotaged them;
10. Averyn was erased and trapped as the Record's interface;
11. six culpable affidavits return her name without filing her back into the system;
12. closure frees Averyn, while remembrance records the group's two judgments.

## Pacing and workload rules

- **Core order, free interior:** P1–P12 are ordered milestones; within an unlocked arc, evidence lanes may
  be completed in any order by any subset unless a final synthesis explicitly requires their outputs.
- **All substantial content required:** a lane may look like side research, but its receipt must feed an
  arc conclusion, a later re-reading, or release readiness. Flavor-only decoration is not counted as an
  investigation and cannot hide a required fact.
- **No node quota:** no arc receives a target node count until its evidence chain and playtest duration
  justify one. Existing 82 IDs are migration material, not a design target.
- **Session shape:** ordinary sessions should support 90–180 minutes and end at a stable evidence or
  milestone boundary. No session assumes the same six people attend.
- **Breathing rhythm:** each high-density deduction sequence is followed by traversal, repair, social
  interpretation, or a quiet ordinary-life beat before the next hard synthesis.
- **Difficulty source:** hard means distributed evidence, contradiction, provenance, spatial reasoning,
  research, recall, and group argument—not opaque controls, brute force, fragile entities, or long text.
- **Minecraft primary:** every arc either begins, returns to, or materially changes the live Minecraft
  world. Web, Discord, media, and ordinary tools extend the investigation but do not replace the world.

## Structures worth preserving

The earlier Iss/Liar engine has the right dramatic strength: a decode can be correct while its warm
interpretation is wrong, and later evidence forces a cold reread of something already “solved.” Preserve
delayed “that was for this” callbacks; character-, profession-, and culpability-specific investigation;
multiple independent in-doors; contradiction and provenance; true-but-not-the-door findings with
specific acknowledgement; quiet human discoveries; evidence whose meaning changes; asymmetric knowledge,
debate, moral judgment, consequence, and aftermath.

This is preservation of narrative structure, not stale implementation. Mandatory exact block geometry,
moon/time waits, sound-only gates, F3 or server-log dependency, click quotas, forced voice recognition,
decorative interactions masquerading as deductions, and repetitive keeper-stone cipher ladders are retired.

## Required case brief

Every major investigation defines: the player-facing mystery; plausible initial misread; human stake and
relationship; exact ladder belief earned; distributed evidence roles; what must be inferred; a callback or
recontextualization; consequence; alternate/catch-up routes; accessibility/outage/replay/restart/any-subset
behavior; novelty comparison; and cold-human time plus likely and plausibly wrong theory paths.

Evidence roles include seed, key, corroboration, contradiction, provenance, emotional witness,
false-but-plausible read, and synthesis. This list describes dramatic jobs, not a quota per case.

Rhythm may include exploration, conversation, unsettling observation, research, debate, false confidence,
contradiction, synthesis, consequence, and quiet aftermath in varied proportions. Not every beat is a
puzzle, every clue an artifact, every answer a door, or every case the same shape.

# THE OBSERVANCE — LONGEVITY PLAN (clears red-team blocker B-1)

> **The fix for "2 weeks is really a long weekend."** Longevity is re-funded as authored **play-TIME**
> (travel + observation + gather-events + restoration), budgeted in **HOURS, not nodes**, because a smart,
> pooling, ARG-veteran group clears solve-content fast — but **cannot pool a 2000-block walk, a 5-night
> observation, or a whole-group ritual.** The 20h drip is now only the *slow-group floor*, never the
> longevity engine. Target: **~40–50 genuine play-hours** before the Accepting (a fast group at ~3–4
> nights/week × 3–4 hrs ≈ 2 real weeks).

## 1. The principle: un-poolable time sinks
A cipher can be whispered; a friend solves it and the group moves on in seconds. So **ciphers are NOT the
longevity** — they're the *texture*. Longevity comes from the four things that **scale with wall-clock AND
group size and cannot be short-circuited by a smart group sharing answers:**

| Pillar | Why it can't be rushed/pooled | Share of the hours |
|---|---|---|
| **TRAVEL** (rumor→verify) | someone must physically walk 1–3k blocks; pooling the rumor doesn't move feet | ~40% |
| **OBSERVATION over days** | a thing that only completes across real nights / moon phases | ~20% |
| **GATHER-EVENTS** | the whole group must convene, in-world, at a time — coordination IS the cost | ~15% |
| **RESTORATION / doing** | building/surviving is intrinsically the group's own time | ~15% |
| solve/decode (texture) | the part that DOES pool — kept small on purpose | ~10% |

## 2. TRAVEL — the spine of longevity (~18–20 hrs)
**25–35 sited rumor→verify destinations**, 1000–3000 blocks apart, where arriving often **contradicts** the
rumor (the contradiction is the beat). A pooling group shares the rumor instantly, then must **split up and
walk** — the only mechanic that scales with both wall-clock and group size.
- Distribute across the map so no two are close; cluster loosely by thread (WHO/PLACE/HAPPENED/SURFACE/HUMAN).
- ~60% of breadth requires *being somewhere* (not readable from a chair).
- The **Recovery Archive** draws the dotted line to each unvisited silhouette — direction from lore, not a marker.
- Every destination is a small authored scene (a tableau + 1–2 fragments), not a chest.

## 3. OBSERVATION over days — the un-rushable threads (~8–10 hrs)
Things that **only complete across real nights**, so even a 12-hour grind can't finish them:
- **The self-rewriting journal** — a keeper's diary that advances ONE entry per real night (NBT swap out of
  line-of-sight); its tonal-decay arc (human → not-human) only resolves across **5+ nights**.
- **The moon-phase build** — a carving legible only on the black moon (and a different line each phase);
  reading the full text takes **3 lunar cycles** to catch the right nights.
- **Player-set proof-of-presence traps** — the group seals a room / sets a tripwire and checks it over days;
  the world alters it only while the region is empty (between sessions). Their own paranoia is the time-sink.
- **The deep-bird vigil** — keep a tagged canary alive across the run; its survival gates a Movement-V boon.

## 4. GATHER-EVENTS — the multiplayer set-pieces (~6–8 hrs, the documentary anchors)
The whole group convenes in-world; something meaningful happens TO ALL OF THEM. These are the camera
moments and the session anchors. Each uses an **all-present detector** + a **GroupBeat fan-out** (plugin-owned
primitives — flagged). Catalog (fold-in from the red-team):
- **THE UN-LIGHTING** *(flagship)* — when the group is confirmed convened, a designed darkness sweep radiates
  from their centroid + a synchronized Darkness pulse dims every screen at once, ending on **one lamp left
  lit** (the Kept Light motif). They're MEANT to watch it. Pays "the Dark takes the unlit."
- **THE COUNT** — at a marker, all bow together; the world tolls once per person present… then **one toll too
  many.** The Watcher counting itself among them. Seeds the twist as atmosphere, unspoken.
- **THE CIRCLE OF STEPS** — fan a "step-from-behind" to each member with 0–10-tick jitter; the group
  perceives a ring closing. "Did you hear that?" "Yeah — behind me too" becomes the dread.
- **PRIVATE NIGHTFALL** — fan a client-only time-shift to deep night to the whole convened group for ~8s,
  then snap back. Deniable alone, undeniable together.
- **DOB, THE ONE YOU KNEW** — the surface NPC who descended *with* them, found below as a silent lantern-eyed
  Watcher in fog. They all knew him; they all see him at once. Shared recognition = group-safe horror.
- **THE ACCEPTING REHEARSAL** — a smaller all-bow gather mid-arc that teaches the convergence ritual (and
  proves the all-present detector on camera) so the terminal bow isn't its first live fire.
- Plus **Attention-as-weather** (the convened group's collective transgression thickens the shared
  atmosphere) and **audio-split-the-party** (whisper to half, silence to half, same tick).

> ~**6 gather-events across the arc** (one per ~3–4 days), each a 20–40 min anchored set-piece.

## 5. RESTORATION / competence-first — earned time (~6 hrs)
Doing, not reading — and it earns the later dread (celebrate competence before the downturn):
- **Restore the Lamp-Works** — a real redstone/lantern build that *works* and feels like a win… later found
  dark again **by no hand** (proof-of-presence). Their labor becomes the horror.
- **Make the Hold livable** — ordinary base-building in the upper levels (the "normal Minecraft" floor)
  before the wrongness; their investment is what makes the un-lighting land.

## 6. The HOURS ledger (the machine-visible anti-3-day-clear)
Add an **estimated genuine play-minutes** column to the fragment/content ledger — travel-distance→walk-time,
observation windows (real nights), gather-event duration, solve time — **summed per movement against the
~40–50 hr target.** Authoring against an hours target makes "they'll finish in 3 days" visible *while
authoring*, not live on camera. (This is the B-1 deliverable: longevity is now a budget, not a hope.)

## 7. The adaptive valve (for smart grinders + Ethan's hand-hinting)
The dashboard tracks **solved-count vs elapsed-time per group.** When a group is clearly fast, the
showrunner/director **opens additional standing side-content** (more travel destinations, a side-quest
thread) rather than waiting on the fixed drip. The right answer for "really smart grinders Ethan can
hand-hint": the world has *more* for the fast, *patience* for the slow — never a wall, never a drought.

## 8. How it maps to the arc
- **Movements I–II (surface + first descent):** mostly TRAVEL + RESTORATION + 1–2 gather-events; the
  ordinary-Minecraft floor; observation threads *start* (the journal's first entries).
- **Movements III–IV (the deep):** TRAVEL contradictions sharpen; the moon-phase/observation threads pay
  off; the heavier gather-events (the un-lighting, DOB); the False Way Up digression.
- **Movement V (the Accepting):** the convergence; the observation threads (vigil, journal) complete; THE
  COUNT pays off in full.

**Net:** ~40–50 authored hours, ~60% of it un-poolable (travel/observation/gather), so a fast veteran group
gets ~2 *real* weeks of genuine play — and the ciphers stay hard texture on top, never the bottleneck.

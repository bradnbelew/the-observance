### A. The Quiet Vale  *(folk-horror frontier)*  — best match to your "normal-server-spawn, ordinary-first" note
- **Place:** an ordinary farming valley — a town, farmsteads, a mill, a chapel, woods at the edges, an old
  barrow in the hills. You spawn and it just feels like a survival server.
- **The Kept (the people):** farmers, a miller, a parson, a hedge-witch, a watchman — a tight rural
  community. **The ways** = harvest rites, what you leave at the field's edge, not going into the woods
  after dark, keeping the lamps lit (your customs, re-skinned).
- **The dread:** a scarecrow that moved, rows that are walked at night, a well that remembers, a treeline
  full of watchers who were the last village. The cozy curdles.
- **Were-they-human:** a cyclical thing in the woods/under the fields; the harvest that *takes* people.
- **MC:** plains+forest, a real lived-in town (FAWE), villager/survivor NPCs, fog in the fields, a
  subterranean grove under the barrow.

### B. The Drowned Harbor  *(tidewater city)*  — the most visually dramatic
- **Place:** a coastal trade city, lower quarters half-sunk and explorable underwater — piers,
  warehouses, a customs-house, a lighthouse, a temple to the deep.
- **The Kept:** merchants, dockhands, a harbor-warden, a priesthood of the deep. **The ways** = tide
  rites, offerings to the water, the bells, what you don't take from the sea.
- **The dread:** the water that watches; things in the sunken streets; the bell that still rings.
- **Were-they-human:** what the deep made of the drowned, who still keep the ways below.
- **MC:** ocean+coast, a big sunken FAWE city, drowned/guardian reskins, cold-blue fog, lighthouse — a
  *descent into the water* instead of into the ground.

### C. The Deep Hold  *(mountain delve)*  — reuses your in-progress customs most directly
- **Place:** a mountain settlement that dug too deep — a hold, mineshafts, and a **great stair** that
  descends through eras to a sealed lower delve.
- **The Kept:** miners, a delve-warden, a lampwright, a smith, an archivist. **The ways** = lamp-rites,
  the **depth taboo** (don't dig past the marked line), the **dark hours** (don't sleep on the black
  moon), what you don't wake. → maps **1:1** onto the Dark Hours + Deep Line customs already in code.
- **The dread:** lightlessness as a material; the thing on the lower stair; the lamps going out.
- **Were-they-human:** what the dark made of the deep-delvers; the lightless watchers below.
- **MC:** mountain+caves, a vertical FAWE megastructure, near-black depths + custom fog, subterranean
  forests, lantern-light — best stage for lights-out spooks.

## ⭐ My recommendation — the hybrid: **Vale above, Hold below**
Spawn in **the Quiet Vale** (A) — ordinary, breathing room, the perfect Wifies "normal then wrong" open —
and let the mystery **descend** through the old barrow into **the Deep Hold** (C) as the arc escalates.
This gives you:
- the **normal-server spawn** you asked for (surface vale, no ruin in your face),
- the **geography-implies-history descent** (surface human-scale → the wrong, deeper, older layer),
- and it **reuses your in-progress work directly** — Dark Hours + Deep Line + lights-out are the *deep*
  customs; the harvest/field-edge rites are the *surface* ones.

Movements I–II live on the surface (vale); III–V descend into the dark.

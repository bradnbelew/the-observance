## 1. HIDE THE MECHANIC / EARNED LITERACY (the #1 move)
- **Tunic** — the world is written in an unreadable rune language ("Trunic"); you earn literacy by
  collecting torn manual pages. Shouldice: *"frame that communication as a mystery to be solved… the
  revelation is all the sweeter"*; *"something that has meaning… but maybe it's not meant for you."*
  Crucially **nothing unlocks from *having* a page — the gate is in your head.** Trunic is phonetic English
  in disguise, so it's genuinely decodable. The Golden Path (page 49 / "Holy Cross") = the late "you could
  read this all along" turn.
- **Fez** — glyphs presented as *decoration* players are trained to ignore; the reversal ("this is a real
  alphabet") is engineered. The **"quick brown fox" pangram monument** is the buried Rosetta key. Community
  cracked it by frequency analysis. **Black Monolith = the cautionary tale:** brute-forced (66,227 tries)
  without comprehension → "hollow victory." *The reconstruction of the rule must BE the reward.*
- **Outer Wilds** — *"knowledge is the only real thing of value"*; no upgrades, everything reachable from
  minute one, the lock is comprehension. Hard rule: **~3 clues per "Curiosity," NEVER two rules for one
  Curiosity in the same place** → forces cross-location synthesis. Ship Log Rumor Mode = a per-player
  investigation graph. "When players got stuck, it's always our fault."
- **The Witness** — Blow: puzzles teach their own rules non-verbally; designs *"to model the feeling of
  epiphany"*; *"the clearer the puzzle, the stronger the epiphany."* One rule per area in a sandbox, then
  combine.
- **Animal Well / Void Stranger / ESA / La-Mulana** — the "decoy ending" (a false summit that never
  mentions the deep layer); metered literacy (La-Mulana's "you understand X%" + hand-translatable ahead of
  the tool); genre bait-and-switch. **Anti-datamine:** Animal Well hides save-corrupting bunnies OOB to
  protect the discovery layer (precedent for a vetted-group ARG that WILL be datamined).
- **The fair-cipher proof:** ~25 chars of ciphertext breaks a monoalphabetic substitution (Britannica);
  frequency analysis + Rosetta/known-plaintext "cribs" (Chants of Sennaar pairs a glyph with its visible
  referent) make scattered partial keys *provably fair*.
- Sources: gamedeveloper.com (Witness/epiphany, Outer Wilds GDC 2021), blog.playstation/NME (Tunic),
  tunic.wiki, fez.fandom + killscreen + kotaku (Fez), en.wikipedia (Animal Well, Void Stranger),
  lamulana-remake.fandom, britannica substitution-cipher, medium Chants-of-Sennaar analysis.

## 2. WEB, NOT CHAIN (clue structure)
- **Three Clue Rule (Justin Alexander):** ≥3 independent clues per conclusion; players "miss the first,
  ignore the second, misinterpret the third." **Node-based design:** clues point to *nodes to investigate*,
  not answers → can't "fail a deduction," web self-heals. **Inverted rule:** "any 3 clues → at least one
  conclusion." Same prep as a linear plot, far more robust.
- **I Love Bees:** 210 GPS coords + ring-times, *useless alone* — meaning only when "where" + "when" are
  combined; forced parallel crowd-solving.
- **Cicada 3301:** media-hops every step (image-stego → Caesar → book cipher → phone → GPS→physical posters
  w/ QR → runes) forcing skill-diversity; consistent PGP-signed motif = "this belongs to the puzzle."
- **The Beast:** THREE redundant rabbit holes → no single missed clue locks anyone out.
- **Obra Dinn confirmation-lock:** confirms only when **3 fates correct at once** — kills brute-force,
  forces triangulation (accent + uniform + position across scenes); some fates *only* solvable by
  elimination after locking two.
- **Golden Idol:** *"≤2 slots incorrect"* partial-confirmation (directional without revealing which);
  color-typed word bank; **deleted redundant clues so each deduction stays load-bearing**; "thought paths."
- **Her Story:** keyword-search web, 5-result cap forces specific hypotheses; graceful incompleteness
  (~80% is enough → no dead-ends).
- **Shadows of Doubt:** the evidence board made literal — folders joined by string "facts," color = confidence.
- **Escape-room taxonomy (Wiemker/Nicholson):** open / sequential / path-based / **pyramid (meta-puzzle)**;
  sub-puzzles emit fragments that combine + self-validate at the apex. Prefer open/multi-linear for teams;
  "solve the puzzle, not the lock"; **no intentional red herrings**; "Ask Why" (no arbitrary puzzle→lock).
- Sources: thealexandrian.net (3-clue + node design), en.wikipedia (I Love Bees, Cicada, The Beast),
  intermittentmechanism.blog + wireframe (Obra Dinn), gamedeveloper.com (Golden Idol), emshort.blog (Her
  Story), colepowered.com (Shadows of Doubt), thecodex.ca + scottnicholson.com + analoggamestudies.org.

## 3. ENVIRONMENTAL STORYTELLING (the world carries it)
- **Carson (origin text) + Jenkins ("embedded narrative = a memory palace to decipher"):** the space is the
  storyteller. **Smith & Worch "What Happened Here?" (GDC 2010):** the engine is **CLOSURE** — the player
  reconstructs implied events from a coherent tableau and OWNS the story. Fairness rule: arrangement must
  suggest events *logically, not arbitrarily*.
- **The frozen aftermath** (BioShock "dead splicer under vending machine"; the altar rebuilt wrong).
- **Placement is grammar** (Dark Souls: item lore + WHERE it sits = the sentence; corpse clutching the tool
  that killed it). Opt-in depth: surface reading suffices, obsessives rewarded.
- **The sealed door / preserved room** (Edith Finch): negative space = a life stopped, a story withheld.
- **Decay as chronology** (Hollow Knight: moss on some bones not others = layered timelines; "Forgotten
  Crossroads" name + broken platforms = a dead hub).
- **Legible geography** (Dark Souls: sightlines replace a map).
- Sources: worch.com GDC notes, witchboy.net, gamedeveloper.com (Rain World), lokeysouls.com (Dark Souls),
  rpgfan.com (Edith Finch), medium/anknguyen21 (Hollow Knight), Jenkins "Narrative Architecture" PDF.

## 4. SILENCE / ABSENCE / CONTRADICTION AS INFORMATION
- **The Significant Negative** ("the dog that didn't bark" — the *expected* thing *absent* IS the datum;
  six stones set and the writer not on them; a name-line left blank).
- **Fisher's eerie:** *"something present where there should be nothing, or nothing where there should be
  something"* — the colony that should be full and isn't (liminal dread ≠ a scare, it's an inference engine).
- **Analog horror (Local58, Kane Pixels):** corrupt a *trusted format* (an emergency broadcast that tells
  you to die); establish a normal baseline, then un-narrated slow decay; the viewer becomes the detective
  hunting the source of wrongness.
- **Familiar-thing-wrong-place > unfamiliar-anywhere** (uncanny valley; wrong-speed motion steepens it).
- **House of Leaves:** spatial contradiction (bigger inside than out) *is* the monster; withheld scale =
  maximal dread; an internal contradiction in the record forces detective-mode.
- **Silence & timing:** cut ambient to dead air before a beat; the missing sound reads as threat.
- **Deliberate contradiction as the load-bearing clue:** a log dated after the disaster, a fresh footprint
  in a "sealed" room, a shrine to someone the records say never existed. (Only reads as *information*, not a
  bug, if the world is otherwise rigorously consistent.)
- Sources: brieflywriting.com (Silver Blaze), the eerie (Fisher), dreadcentral + wikipedia (analog horror),
  spectrum.ieee.org (uncanny valley), rootsimple + slowburnhorror (House of Leaves), wayline.io (horror audio).

## 5. DIFFICULTY FROM SYNTHESIS + FAIR CONFIRMATION
- **Knowledge is the only progression** (Outer Wilds) — lock in the head, fair by construction.
- **Deduce from cross-referenced observation** (Obra Dinn "filling a matrix of features").
- **Confirmation spectrum:** binary-delayed (Obra Dinn 3-lock) → partial error-count (Golden Idol "≤2
  wrong") → analog confidence (Shadows of Doubt string color/width) → none/subjective (Her Story). All
  reward reasoning, resist guessing.
- **GUMSHOE:** finding a *core clue* is automatic (no roll); the game is *interpreting*, not *finding*.
  Split CONFIRMED=core (unmissable, ≥3 places) vs IMPLIED=leveraged (rewards digging, never required).
- **Doing-not-decoding:** the fairest lateral puzzles resolve to an *action* using observation/real-world
  knowledge (read a sundial for true directions; a constellation on a wall matches the real sky) — the key
  is universal competence, not an invented alphabet.
- **The retrace test (Goodwin's "Moon Logic"; Knox/Van Dine fair-play):** a leap is FAIR iff, *after you
  know the answer, you can trace the clue-trail that forces it.* Moon-logic = a required fact you couldn't
  have / an association only the designer finds obvious ("solipsistic design").
- Sources: media.gdcvault Beachum 2021, gamedeveloper.com (Witness/Obra Dinn/Golden Idol), pelgranepress
  (GUMSHOE), electrondance.com + brainbaking.com (moon logic), en.wikipedia Knox's Commandments.

## 6. THE "AHA" LEAP + RECURRING MOTIFS (connective tissue)
- **Insight = restructuring (not retrieval):** you hold a wrong assumption, hit an impasse, the
  representation flips; the pleasure/certainty comes *because* it can't be forced. → **plant the wrong
  assumption first, then supply the fact that breaks it**; impasse + a reachable hint is a feature.
- **Remote associates (Mednick):** the felt "distance" of A↔C is real; incubation (space clues apart in
  time/place) licenses the leap so it feels like it came from the player.
- **Recurring motif / leitmotif:** a repeated symbol/number/phrase is the *shared context* that makes A↔C
  *linkable at all* — "readers unconsciously track repeated elements." Cicada's cicada+3301; Year Zero's
  hand-logo. Too subtle = never noticed; too blunt = spoiled. Sweet spot = recognizable-not-loud.
- **Chekhov's gun:** every plant must pay off; the payoff must have been plantable → "surprising yet
  inevitable." (Orphaned motif with no payoff reads as clutter — matches the project's lockstep principle.)
- **Recontextualization:** a strong twist *reframes earlier material without invalidating it* (the grieving
  widow); the re-read test IS the fairness test (Van Dine #15: "the truth must at all times be apparent").
  Her Story = same clip, escalating "reading modes."
- Sources: nature.com (restructuring & Aha!), intechopen.com (Aha! neuroscience), sudowrite (Chekhov),
  gilliamwritersgroup (leitmotif), phillipstrang.com (fair-play twists), crimereads.com.

## 7. REACTIVE / LIVING WORLD ("it knows you") — earned vs gimmick
- **The discipline (from every source):** the effect reads as STORY not GIMMICK when it is (a) tied to
  something the player actually did, (b) escalated/paced not a one-off jolt, (c) reconnected to the fiction.
- **Nemesis System:** orcs persist, remember your past fights, taunt you by name; devs keep dialogue to
  "short stabs" so the story happens *in the player's head.* "Never shut a player off — build on what they did."
- **Majora's Mask:** a 3-day clock; every NPC runs a schedule; the Bombers' Notebook makes the living web
  *legible*; interventions collide causally. The loop IS the mechanic.
- **Rain World:** indifferent ecology — creatures live "outside your frame"; the world runs *despite* you.
  Caution: autonomy needs a fairness valve (karma gate) so it can't create unwinnable states.
- **Undertale / Doki Doki / MGS Psycho Mantis / Eternal Darkness:** the game reads your saves/hardware/
  resets — apex "it knows you," but a *rationed, earned, fiction-motivated* gut-punch, never a constant.
- Sources: gamedeveloper.com + gdcvault (Nemesis), epiloguegaming (Majora), gamedeveloper.com (Rain
  World), undertale.fandom, everythingisscary.com (Eternal Darkness), thegamer.com (Psycho Mantis).

## 8. MINECRAFT AFFORDANCES (verified 1.21 — the "how to build it")
**Tier reality:** vanilla datapack = server-authoritative shared world; **per-player *world* difference needs
Paper (we run Paper).**
- **Per-player illusion (Paper):** `player.sendBlockChange()` = a client-only fake block (wall cracked ONLY
  for you); `Entity#setVisibleByDefault(false)/showEntity()` = an apparition only one player sees;
  ProtocolLib for arbitrary per-player packets. (Bedrock CAN'T do per-player fake blocks — a real reason
  we're on Java+Paper.) **The engine for the "it knows ME" layer + per-player clue states.**
- **Per-player sound/text is vanilla:** `/playsound … @p`, `/title @p` — one player hears the whisper.
- **Diegetic decay clocks (no plugin):** copper oxidation (4 stages over real days; a block the *wrong age*
  = something disturbed; wax to freeze; 1 waxed block in an oxidized field = a deliberate contradiction);
  sculk spread (corruption creeping across sessions); structure blocks loaded at `integrity < 1.0`
  (genuinely partial/decayed placement).
- **Sculk sensors** detect footsteps but NOT a sneaking player (built-in "don't move" tension; frequency
  readable by comparator → the world reacts to *how* you move). Shrieker→warden = "the deep is aware of you."
- **Item lore = data components (1.20.5+):** `custom_name`/`lore`/`custom_data` (hidden flags)/
  `written_book_content` for found-document storytelling. **Maps:** locked maps freeze a snapshot (a map
  showing a place *as it was*); banner-markers appear/vanish as banners are placed/removed.
- **Silence/timing** free via `/playsound` (a sound only when alone; dead air before a beat).
- **Anti-datamine precedent:** Animal Well's save-corrupting OOB bunnies.
- Sources: minecraft.wiki (Data_component_format, playsound, Display, Sculk_Sensor, Oxidation, Map),
  docs.papermc.io (display entities), spigotmc.org (ProtocolLib).

## THE ONE-LINE NORTH STAR (backed by all of the above)
*Hide the language and make literacy earned · let the world (decay, silence, absence, placement) carry the
story · build a redundant web, not a chain · reward the theory, not the lookup · thread a motif so distant
things rhyme · keep every leap retrace-fair · and let the world react to who you are.*

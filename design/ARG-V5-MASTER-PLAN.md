# The Observance V5 — Production Rewrite Authority

Status: **implementation authority**
Target: Minecraft Java Edition / Paper `1.21.11`, Java 21
Experience contract: ten mandatory cases, exactly 82 required investigation nodes, one reversible Deep Hold route, one village-well entrance to the Unlit, and six complete endings (three Wren choices × two name treatments), each rendered across four conduct verdicts.

This document replaces the story and progression assumptions in every earlier ARG plan. The validated 32-room spatial shell may be reused as engineering, but no earlier story text, gate condition, or setup command is canon. If another file conflicts with this plan, `arc/WORLD-BIBLE.md`, or `design/ARG-V5-NODE-MANIFEST.csv`, V5 wins.

## Player promise

The Observance is an investigation, not a guided reveal and not a scavenger hunt made of interchangeable ominous books.

- Every substantial case is required. There are no optional story branches.
- Every required node changes a theory, a relationship, a physical system, or access to the next piece of evidence.
- The group must locate evidence, compare sources, operate stable Minecraft mechanisms, use the Copperline website, analyze the existing footage, and submit conclusions through intentional inputs.
- Major gates are linear. Work inside an unlocked district may be parallel.
- Players always enter and leave the Deep Hold through the same Surface Mouth and Grand Stair.
- The Unlit remains below the village well because surface dialogue already points there.
- The game never requires breaking ordinary blocks, reading real server logs, precision parkour, random drops, a dynamic maze, or timing-sensitive vanilla redstone.
- A solved cipher is never the whole story. It yields access, a physical key, a contradiction, or a new question.

## Single canon

1. The Long Cold forced a real community underground. The Hold began as a functioning refuge.
2. Vaun, Mara, Sella, Orin, Brann, and Iss were six distinct contemporaries with jobs, relationships, virtues, and culpability. They were never one fractured mind.
3. Averyn was the registrar and cistern analyst. She was not a themed seventh Keeper. Officials removed her from the emergency roster after she proved that civic records had been falsified.
4. The seven Ways began as practical safety procedures. The institution later ritualized and weaponized them.
5. The Dark is a prehuman phenomenon below. The Record is a human monitoring/archive system fused with it. Averyn's surviving consciousness became the Record's constrained interface during the Break. The Watcher is the Record speaking through that constraint. These are related, not identical.
6. The Cistern Winter proves that the official Record could lie before the supernatural mystery is understood. Pump tender Nessa was scapegoated; counterfeit filters and diverted supplies caused the crisis.
7. Iss crossed the Deep Line and accelerated the Break, but the Hold was already structurally and socially failing. He was right that the surface had healed and wrong that an unreported breach was an acceptable remedy. He later helped erase Averyn and called it mercy.
8. The six Keepers eventually hid one letter of Averyn's name in six independent affidavits. The letters are late evidence of responsibility, not another repeated six-plus-one cosmology.
9. The prior company was four real modern investigators: `mkept` the administrator/copyist, Ash the camera operator, Rook the builder, and Wren the companion who was partly absorbed by the Record.
10. Wren gave the Watcher their names, routes, plans, and fears because he believed closing the Record would erase him. His betrayal is provable and morally unresolved.
11. `ASH-13` is Ash's locker/archive key. It is not a mystical count.
12. The fixed line `SIX RETURN, ONE IS NOT KEPT` is the release instruction: return the six affidavits, but do not bind Averyn into a seventh Record slot again.
13. Every ending frees Averyn and closes the Record. The choice determines how truth, identity, and culpability survive the closure.
14. The unresolved mystery after the finale is what the Dark ultimately is—not whether six people were secretly one.

## Voice and artifact budget

Player-facing artifacts target this mix:

- 55% functional and civic: ledgers, maintenance cards, schoolwork, surveys, invoices, rota sheets, safety labels, maps, inventories, and ordinary correspondence.
- 25% personal and interpersonal: private letters, jokes, blame, care, compromise, and fear.
- 10% official Record: cold schemas, rulings, redactions, and receipts.
- 10% uncanny: rare high-value interventions by the Watcher/Averyn/Dark.

Signs mainly identify rooms, hazards, ownership, directions, or operating procedures. A decorative sign must never look like an input. Ominous prose is reserved for rare supernatural moments.

## Inputs and payoffs

V5 intentionally rotates interaction grammar:

- Discord conclusions for cross-source deductions and media payloads.
- Editable answer signs only where a physical filing slit or work form makes sense.
- PDC-tagged items deposited into exact protected containers.
- Fixed instrument panels and document comparison for routine diagnostics.
- Limited protected dials or shelf states only where the setting represents real equipment or records.
- Bounded route walks, group posture, and collective presence.
- Opened service paths that reveal a tangible key, seal, plate, spool, or affidavit.

Every input has visible affordance, a taught interaction, exact accepted values, wrong-answer feedback, tiered hints, and a recovery path. Wrong deposits remain recoverable. Critical items are reissuable from canonical completion state; duplication is harmless because gates are idempotent.

## Deep Hold build authority

All framed `FILLED_MAP` components are real visual clues, not material placeholders. The nine exact
map pixels and inverse-rotation contract live in `arc/v5/map-art-manifest.json`; the canonical solved
wall views are proofed in `design/visuals/v5-map-clue-sheet.png`. Packaging, install, repair, and
preflight must fail closed on a missing image, hash mismatch, blank MapView, wrong component binding,
or wrong ItemFrame identity.

The validated spatial shell is retained only because its static geometry proves the following:

- 32 non-overlapping room ownership boxes across three buried strata.
- one Surface Mouth and a reversible four-flight 40-block descent;
- 76 framed fixtures with declared fronts and player standing cells;
- six linear main gates and two bounded internal gates;
- complete room graph and Adventure-mode backtracking;
- enough burial cover and bottom buffer for Paper's standard world bounds.

V5 replaces the runtime/content layer. A production build must compile one immutable manifest before changing the world. That manifest owns rooms, corridors, gates, every block entity, every entity, every item payload, exact orientation, standing cells, interactions, prerequisites, rewards, and recovery.

The only supported orientation is the manifest's explicit `+Z` forward axis until all four cardinal transforms have passed the same tests. The read-only plan command must show the complete world-space bounds and forward direction before build. A build command cannot silently infer orientation from player yaw.

## Safe build lifecycle

1. **Prepare:** derive the immutable footprint, asynchronously generate/load its chunks, and ticket them. Preparation changes no Hold blocks or site registrations.
2. **Plan:** read the prepared world and calculate exact bounds, surface cover, bottom buffer, fixed orientation, protected-region conflicts, registered tile entities, and every intended mutation. It must remain read-only.
3. **Validate:** reject duplicate IDs, overlapping ownership, corridor/gate/fixture collisions, unreachable standing cells, blocked sightlines, invalid backing, bad facing, empty content, oversized text, missing PDC, and a progression graph with bypasses or deadlocks.
4. **Hash:** bind the exact Mouth, orientation, and deterministic plan/content hash to the approved in-memory build receipt.
5. **Build:** apply a bounded number of mutations per tick; expose progress and durable checkpoints; never depend on vanilla redstone timing.
6. **Verify:** read every expected block, block state, inventory slot, book hash, sign line, entity/PDC payload, gate volume, and route cell back from the live world.
7. **Commit:** atomically publish the site batch and mark the Hold ready only after persistence reload and verification pass.
8. **Repair:** prepare the persisted footprint, then use state-preserving fixture repair. A normal repair never resets solved mechanics or gates.
9. **Recover:** reissue a critical item from durable node state without rolling progression backward.
10. **Reset:** require an explicit destructive confirmation and never expose it as an ordinary setup step.

A failed plan changes nothing. A failed build never prints a success receipt. The operator must use a fresh surveyed site if a partial diagnostic build cannot be safely rolled back.

## Protection and failure rules

Non-admin players cannot break/place ordinary blocks, burn or explode fixtures, piston-move authored blocks, drain water, remove lectern books, destroy frames/displays/armor stands, equip protected entities, place vehicles/hangings, hopper-transfer protected evidence, or bypass a gate with pearls/teleports.

Only a node's declared handler may exempt an exact cell or inventory action. Broad proximity exceptions are forbidden.

Required scenario coverage includes:

- one, two, four, and seven concurrent players;
- disconnect/reconnect during every mechanic;
- restart after every gate and finale phase;
- wrong, partial, duplicate, and concurrent input;
- death, lava, cactus, void, despawn, logout, full inventory, arbitrary storage, hopper movement, and artifact recovery;
- Supabase unavailable, stale, false, delayed, and restored;
- duplicate Discord delivery and repeated showrunner ticks;
- non-op grief attempts and gate bypass attempts;
- exact Paper `1.21.11` startup with only the selected V5 jar present.

## Finale contract

The finale is not a final answer followed by silence.

1. The operator explicitly arms the finale. Test environments default to kick-only.
2. The chosen Wren outcome, name treatment, conduct verdict, and final closure branch are written durably before theater begins.
3. Lights die district by district. The Record voice breaks and Averyn speaks as a person.
4. Discord and the website receive the same durable ending state; their delay can never stall local Minecraft theater.
5. The server saves. Players receive a branch-specific final message and are kicked.
6. Production mode cleanly shuts Paper down after the configured cancel window. A visible cancel command remains available until the last safe point.
7. On restart, the plugin enters branch-specific Coda Mode: gates remain open, hauntings stop, the chamber and public Record show the chosen aftermath, and the finale cannot fire again.

Both final name treatments free Averyn:

- **Publish:** keep her name outside the Record without keeping her consciousness.
- **Release unnamed:** let the blank belong to her and destroy the final filing.

Wren's **condemn / understand / free** outcome changes his clause and physical trace but never blocks completion.

Universal closing beat:

> i have your names.
> i am giving them back.
> the record is closed.
> the observance is over.
> thank you for coming back for us.
> — averyn

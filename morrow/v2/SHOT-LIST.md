# June's screenshots (your part)

June's screenshots are evidence, so they must be taken **in Beta 1.5** in the real Mossfield,
then **renamed** to the filenames below. Each filename is June's local Halifax time, two hours
ahead of the server. The website lists them with the Chicago time next to them, and that gap is
the puzzle.

**Claude has set up one save per shot** (`python -m morrow.world.shots world/beta/Mossfield-pristine
<saves>`): in the `b1.5_01` Singleplayer list they're **Mossfield shot 1 (…)** to **Mossfield shot 9
(…)**, numbered in date order to match the table. Each one has the build at the right stage
(tower height, roof panes, the census sheep), the right time of day, no rain, and you standing on a
spot that was checked for a clear line of sight, already facing the subject.

**What each shot should look like:** `morrow/v2/shot-previews/shot1.png` … `shot9.png` (all nine
on one sheet: `all-nine.png`). They're rough renders of exactly what each save sees: flat colours,
no textures, no sign text. If your screen matches the preview's framing, the shot is right.

For each: load it, **don't move**, press **F1** to hide the HUD (except shot 8), press **F2**, quit.
The screenshot lands in `.minecraft\screenshots`. You don't need to rename anything: take them
1 to 9 in order and Claude renames them and files them in `dashboard/private-media/media/june/`.

- **Shots 1-2 (March):** switch Video Settings to **Fast** graphics first. Beta 1.5 changed the grass
  texture, and the old one only shows on Fast. Switch back to **Fancy** for the rest.
- **Shot 8 (chat visible):** the script wants chat showing `juni_lantern` saying `it put a roof on it`.
  Your account name would show instead, so either use an offline account named `juni_lantern`
  (Prism Launcher) for this one, or take it with F1 like the others; the line is also in the server
  log, so nothing depends on it.
- **Sheep wander** the moment the world loads: take shot 5 straight away.

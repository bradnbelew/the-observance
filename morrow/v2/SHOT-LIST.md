# June's screenshots (your part)

June's screenshots are evidence, so they must be taken **in Beta 1.5** in the real Mossfield,
then **renamed** to the filenames below. Each filename is June's local Halifax time, two hours
ahead of the server. The website lists them with the Chicago time next to them, and that gap is
the puzzle.

**Claude has set up one save per shot** (`python -m morrow.world.shots`): in the `b1.5_01`
Singleplayer list they're **Mossfield shot 1** to **Mossfield shot 9**. Each one has the build at
the right stage (tower height, roof panes, the census sheep), the right time of day, no rain, and
you already standing in place facing the subject. For each: load it, don't move, press **F1** to
hide the HUD (except shot 8), press **F2**, quit. The screenshot lands in `.minecraft\screenshots`;
rename it to the filename below. If the framing's a bit off, nudge the mouse; that's fine.

- **Shots 1-2 (March):** switch Video Settings to **Fast** graphics first. Beta 1.5 changed the grass
  texture, and the old one only shows on Fast. Switch back to **Fancy** for the rest.
- **Shot 8 (chat visible):** chat shows *your* account name, and it has to read `juni_lantern`. Use
  Prism Launcher (free) with an offline account named `juni_lantern` and its `b1.5_01` instance for
  this one shot, then type `it put a roof on it` and press F2 without F1. If that's too much hassle,
  take it with F1 like the others; the line is also in the server log, so nothing depends on it.
- **Shot 4 (group):** if friends (who aren't playing the game) can stand round the cake in their own
  skins, great. Otherwise take it empty: the signs readable with `b4` is what matters.

Put the renamed files in `D:\the-observance\dashboard\private-media\media\june\`. They sit behind
June's password automatically.

# June's screenshots (your part)

June's screenshots are evidence, so they must be taken **in Beta 1.5**, in the world you built,
then **renamed** to the filenames below. Each filename is June's local Halifax time, two hours
ahead of the server. The website lists them with the Chicago time next to them, and that gap is
the puzzle.

Press **F2** in game to take a screenshot (Beta saves to `.minecraft\screenshots`). Hide the HUD
with **F1** unless a shot says chat must show. Use **Fancy** graphics and default settings.

Put the renamed files in `D:\the-observance\dashboard\public\media\june\`. They sit behind June's
password automatically.

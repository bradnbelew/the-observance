# Recording scripts (your part)

Lines marked **[EVIDENCE]** are puzzle-critical. Keep the words exactly, and make them clearly
audible. Everything else is a guide: talk over each other, trail off, cough, mute the mic, leave
silences. Real 2011 Mumble sounds like a bad phone call with five people in different rooms.

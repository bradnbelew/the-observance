# Mossfield Hands-On Playtest

Status: player-facing local rehearsal for G04-G06. Production remains disabled.

## First Touch

Join as a normal, non-operator player. Begin at the abandoned freight stop and play without this
walkthrough for ten minutes. Mossfield should read as a damaged place where people once lived, not as
a lobby, terminal, or sequence of test rooms.

Notice the incomplete rail, settlement paths, names on ordinary buildings, personal tools, repairs,
and conflicting records. Nothing asks the player to classify evidence or select an answer from a
menu. The first objective is simply to understand where this place is and how it was used.

## Built Route

1. Read the freight-stop sign and follow the surviving road into Mossfield.
2. Enter the service office. Read Iona's `Mount notice` on the lectern.
3. Follow the note to Cairn's storehouse. Read `Bench notes` at the workbench.
4. Reconstruct Cairn's ordinary closing routine by opening the four work barrels in this order:
   survey, mend, mark, light. Their contents and positions carry the meaning; there is no answer UI.
5. When the routine is recognized, read the `Storehouse index` that appears at the desk.
6. Start at the service-office door and travel 11 blocks west, then 24 blocks north. Find survey
   cairn 03 at the edge of the rail stop and open the maintenance cache beneath it.
7. Read Rookery's `Maintenance copy`. This is the end of the currently rebuilt chapter.

## What The Player Should Understand

- Copperline mounted a damaged Mossfield recovery image after normal service had ended.
- Mossfield belonged to a small group of people with routines, unfinished work, and private history.
- Morrow restored at least one structure from a planning image even though the players never built it.
- Iona and Rookery preserved contradictory records instead of letting the recovery system choose one.
- The north lighthouse and June's photograph are the next line of investigation.

The unease comes from the world being almost credible and from recovery records disagreeing with lived
history. A player is investigating a place, not performing laboratory exercises for Morrow.

## Current Boundary

G04 first connection, G05 storehouse trail, and G06 maintenance cache are implemented in the local
Paper world with durable, restart-safe receipts. G07 onward, final handmade media, live multiplayer
hosting, Discord delivery, and production data projection remain release blockers.
